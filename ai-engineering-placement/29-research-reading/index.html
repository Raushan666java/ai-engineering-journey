29 — Research Reading & AI Literature

Overview​

Learn how to read, understand, and apply AI research papers. Covers the three-pass reading method, keeping up with AI research, navigating major conferences, and reproducing papers.

Sub-Chapters​

#Sub-ChapterTopicsQ&A
01Reading Research Papersthree-pass method, paper anatomy, abstract scanning, literature review10
02Keeping Up with AI ResearcharXiv, Hugging Face Papers, newsletters, Twitter/X, podcasts10
03Major AI ConferencesNeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, CVPR — proceedings, workshops10
04Reproducing & Implementing Papersrepo setup, benchmark reproduction, ablation studies, blog writing10

Learning Path​

  1. Read in order
  2. Practice by picking a paper and applying the three-pass method
  3. Try reproducing a simple paper

Prerequisites​

Basic ML and DL understanding from Module 08 and Module 09.
