# Day 45 — Divide & Conquer

## Learning Objectives
- Understand divide and conquer algorithms: break problems into subproblems, solve, and combine.
- Implement Merge Sort and Quick Sort (randomized pivot) and reason about their complexity.
- Implement Closest Pair of Points via Divide & Conquer (O(n log n)).

## Topics
- Merge sort and its variants
- QuickSort pivot choice and worst-case behavior
- Closest Pair problem: plane separation, strip checking, and merging by y coordinate

## Practice Problems
- Implement Merge Sort: test with random arrays
- Implement QuickSort with randomized pivot: understand average/worst case
- Closest pair: practice on 2D point datasets
