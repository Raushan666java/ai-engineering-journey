Verbal Ability — 100 Question Drill Bank (Any Company)

Rule: 30-60 seconds per question. Answers with explanations at the bottom. Covers all 10 sections from verbal-all-sections.md. Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

Section 1 — Grammar Rules (10 questions)​

  1. (E) The committee ___ yet to announce the results. (A) has (B) have (C) are (D) were
  2. (E) She is allergic ___ peanuts. (A) from (B) to (C) with (D) of
  3. (M) Each of the students ___ submitted the assignment. (A) have (B) has (C) are (D) were
  4. (M) He has been living in Pune ___ 2015. (A) for (B) since (C) from (D) at
  5. (M) The number of employees ___ increasing every year. (A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have
  6. (M) Neither of the two answers ___ correct. (A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have
  7. (H) One of my friends ___ a pilot. (A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have
  8. (H) The manager, along with his assistants, ___ attending the conference. (A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have
  9. (H) There ___ many reasons for his success. (A) is (B) are (C) was (D) has
  10. (H) A number of students ___ protested against the decision. (A) has (B) have (C) was (D) is

  7. (H) "Ubiquitous" means: (A) rare (B) found everywhere (C) dangerous (D) hidden
  8. (H) "Meticulous" is closest to: (A) careless (B) extremely careful (C) quick (D) forgetful
  9. (H) "Pragmatic" means: (A) idealistic (B) practical (C) emotional (D) theoretical

  4. (M) Antonym of "transparent": (A) clear (B) opaque (C) thin (D) bright

  9. (H) Synonym of "obsolete": (A) modern (B) outdated (C) useful (D) popular
  10. (H) Antonym of "generous": (A) kind (B) stingy (C) giving (D) warm

Section 6 — Fill in the Blanks (10 questions)​

  1. (E) If it ___ tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. (A) rains (B) rained (C) will rain (D) rain
  2. (E) She is ___ to win the race given her training. (A) likely (B) likely to (C) likely of (D) like
  3. (M) He is allergic ___ dust, so he avoids gardens. (A) of (B) to (C) from (D) with
  4. (M) The results were ___ than we expected. (A) good (B) better (C) best (D) well
  5. (M) The committee ___ not reached a decision yet. (A) has (B) have (C) are (D) were
  6. (M) ___ you work hard, you will not succeed. (A) Unless (B) If (C) Because (D) Although
  7. (M) He is interested ___ learning Spanish. (A) at (B) in (C) on (D) for
  8. (H) The manager, as well as the employees, ___ responsible. (A) are (B) is (C) were (D) have
  9. (H) Not only did he lose the match, ___ he also got injured. (A) but (B) and (C) so (D) or
  10. (H) Each of the applicants ___ to submit their documents. (A) have (B) has (C) are (D) were

Section 7 — Para Jumbles (10 questions)​

  1. (E) (a) The company then trained staff on the new tool. (b) Customer complaints dropped sharply. (c) A bank adopted an AI chatbot. (d) As a result, response time halved. Order?
  2. (E) (a) She finally reached the office. (b) The bus was delayed by an hour. (c) She had left home on time. Order?
  3. (M) (a) This keeps the soil fertile. (b) Farmers rotate crops every season. (c) Different crops use different nutrients. Order?
  4. (M) (a) As a result, sales doubled. (b) The company slashed prices. (c) Customers flocked to the stores. Order?
  5. (M) (a) The report was published yesterday. (b) It took the team six months to write. (c) The government requested the study last year. Order?
  6. (M) (a) The ice began to melt. (b) The sun rose over the mountains. (c) Rivers swelled downstream. Order?
  7. (M) (a) He then submitted the proposal. (b) After weeks of revision, the draft was complete. (c) The team approved it unanimously. Order?
  8. (H) (a) This feedback loop drives continuous improvement. (b) Teams release small updates frequently. (c) Users report issues within days. (d) Developers patch them in the next sprint. Order?
  9. (H) (a) However, the initial investment is high. (b) Solar power is becoming cheaper every year. (c) The long-term savings outweigh the cost. (d) Many households are switching to rooftop panels. Order?
  10. (H) (a) The signal reaches the antenna. (b) A satellite orbits 36,000 km above the equator. (c) It retransmits the signal to the ground station. (d) Your phone converts it to a call. Order?

Section 8 — Reading Comprehension (10 questions)​

Passage: "Urban rooftop gardens cool buildings in summer by shading the roof and releasing moisture. They also absorb rainwater, cutting the load on city drains. Cities like Singapore and New York have offered tax breaks to encourage them. Critics note the upfront cost is high and maintenance requires skilled labour, but supporters argue the long-term energy savings pay for the installation within a decade."

  1. (E) What are two benefits of rooftop gardens mentioned?
  2. (E) Which is NOT a benefit mentioned? (A) cooling buildings (B) absorbing rainwater (C) growing rare flowers (D) reducing drain load
  3. (M) Why do some cities offer tax breaks? (A) to discourage gardens (B) to encourage rooftop gardens (C) to fund skyscrapers (D) to reduce taxes overall
  4. (M) What is the criticism of rooftop gardens? (A) they are too small (B) high upfront cost and skilled maintenance (C) they attract pests (D) they block sunlight
  5. (M) According to supporters, when do the savings cover the cost? (A) within a month (B) within a decade (C) never (D) within a century
  6. (H) The tone of the passage is best described as: (A) one-sided (B) balanced (C) hostile (D) humorous
  7. (H) "Cutting the load on city drains" means: (A) increasing drainage (B) reducing pressure on drainage systems (C) destroying drains (D) building new drains
  8. (M) The passage suggests rooftop gardens primarily help with: (A) food production (B) urban climate and drainage (C) wildlife habitat (D) tourism
  9. (H) Which word best fits the passage's view of maintenance? (A) unnecessary (B) skilled work required (C) automatic (D) impossible
  10. (M) What would be the best title? (A) The Cost of Gardening (B) Rooftop Gardens: Benefits and Challenges (C) How to Farm Indoors (D) Singapore's Economy

Section 9 — Cloze Test (10 questions)​

Passage: "The team worked ___ (81) for weeks to meet the deadline. ___(82), the client changed the requirements. The team had to ___ (83) the entire design. This ___ (84) their frustration, ___ (85) they delivered on time and the client was satisfied ___ (86) the result."

  1. (E) (A) hardly (B) hard (C) harder (D) hardest
  2. (E) (A) However (B) Therefore (C) Moreover (D) Meanwhile
  3. (M) (A) keep (B) rework (C) simplify (D) cancel
  4. (M) (A) increased (B) decreased (C) ignored (D) solved
  5. (M) (A) but (B) because (C) so (D) and
  6. (M) (A) at (B) with (C) of (D) in

Passage 2: "Online learning has grown ___ (87) in the last decade. Students now have access to courses ___ (88) top universities ___ (89) their homes. ___ (90), self-discipline remains the biggest challenge."

  1. (H) (A) slow (B) rapidly (C) rare (D) equally
  2. (H) (A) of (B) from (C) for (D) at
  3. (H) (A) in (B) from (C) to (D) by
  4. (H) (A) Therefore (B) However (C) For example (D) As a result

Section 10 — Idioms & Phrases (10 questions)​

  1. (E) "To hit the nail on the head" means: (A) to work as a carpenter (B) to be exactly right (C) to cause damage (D) to act quickly
  2. (E) "A blessing in disguise" means: (A) a hidden gift (B) an apparent misfortune that turns out well (C) a religious ceremony (D) a lucky charm
  3. (M) "To let the cat out of the bag" means: (A) to release a pet (B) to reveal a secret (C) to buy a cat (D) to create confusion
  4. (M) "Once in a blue moon" means: (A) every night (B) very rarely (C) twice a week (D) at dawn
  5. (M) "To bite the bullet" means: (A) to eat quickly (B) to face a difficult situation bravely (C) to fight (D) to waste time
  6. (M) "To beat around the bush" means: (A) to explore a forest (B) to avoid the main point (C) to work hard (D) to finish quickly
  7. (M) "The ball is in your court" means: (A) it's a sports game (B) it's your turn to act (C) you lost (D) the game is over
  8. (H) "To burn the midnight oil" means: (A) to waste fuel (B) to work late into the night (C) to start a fire (D) to relax
  9. (H) "To break the ice" means: (A) to melt frozen water (B) to start a conversation (C) to smash something (D) to end a meeting
  10. (H) "To cut corners" means: (A) to do something cheaply or wrongly to save time (B) to decorate a room (C) to take a shortcut in travel (D) to be precise

Answer Key​

Section 1 — Grammar Rules​

  1. A) has (committee = singular unit)
  2. B) to (allergic to)
  3. B) has (each = singular)
  4. B) since (point in time)
  5. B) is ("the number of" = singular; "a number of" = plural)
  6. B) is (neither = singular)
  7. B) is (subject = one, not friends)
  8. B) is (subject = manager; ignore "along with his assistants")
  9. B) are (subject = many reasons, plural)
  10. B) have ("a number of" = plural; "the number of" = singular)

Section 2 — Vocabulary​

  1. B) kind
  2. B) intending harm
  3. B) examining one's own thoughts (intro + spect = look within)
  4. A) after death
  5. B) short-lived
  6. B) frank (open and honest)
  7. B) found everywhere (ubi = everywhere)
  8. B) extremely careful
  9. B) practical (pragmat = action/business)
  10. B) open to multiple interpretations (ambi = both)

Section 3 — Synonyms & Antonyms​

  1. B) fluent
  2. A) deep
  3. B) plentiful
  4. B) opaque
  5. B) delicate
  6. B) contract
  7. B) hardship
  8. B) lazy
  9. B) outdated
  10. B) stingy

Section 4 — Spelling​

  1. B) accommodation (double m, double d... actually double c, double m)
  2. B) separate
  3. C) definitely
  4. C) receive (ei after c)
  5. B) privilege
  6. B) embarrass (double r, double s)
  7. C) occurrence (double r, double c)
  8. A) maintenance
  9. A) conscience
  10. A) government

Section 5 — Sentence Correction​

  1. "are" → "is" (subject = manager; with his team is a prepositional phrase)
  2. "since" → "for" (duration = for)
  3. "Each of the boys has completed his homework" (each = singular)
  4. "One of my friends is a doctor" (subject = one)
  5. "...sales are better than last year's" ("more better" = double comparative)
  6. "Neither of the answers is correct" (neither = singular)
  7. "The committee has decided..." (committee = singular)
  8. Correct as is ("who always arrive" agrees with "people", the antecedent of who)
  9. Correct as is (split infinitive is acceptable in modern English)
  10. Correct as is (scarcely...when = correct correlative pair; never "scarcely...than")

Section 6 — Fill in the Blanks​

  1. A) rains (first conditional)
  2. A) likely ("likely + to + verb": She is likely to win the race; B would create a double "to")
  3. B) to
  4. B) better (comparative + than)
  5. A) has (committee = singular)
  6. A) Unless (unless = if not)
  7. B) in (interested in)
  8. B) is (subject = manager; as well as = prepositional)
  9. A) but (not only...but also)
  10. B) has (each = singular)

Section 7 — Para Jumbles​

  1. c, a, d, b (adopt → train → result → final outcome)
  2. c, b, a (left on time → bus delayed → reached office)
  3. b, c, a (rotate → different nutrients → keeps fertile)
  4. b, c, a (slashed prices → customers flocked → sales doubled)
  5. c, b, a (requested → took six months → published)
  6. b, a, c (sun rose → ice melted → rivers swelled)
  7. b, a, c (draft complete → submitted → approved)
  8. b, c, d, a (release updates → users report → developers patch → feedback loop)
  9. b, d, a, c (solar cheaper → households switching → however initial high → long-term savings outweigh)
  10. b, a, c, d (satellite orbits → signal reaches antenna → retransmits → phone converts)

Section 8 — Reading Comprehension​

  1. Cooling buildings in summer; absorbing rainwater
  2. C) growing rare flowers (not mentioned)
  3. B) to encourage rooftop gardens
  4. B) high upfront cost and skilled maintenance
  5. B) within a decade
  6. B) balanced (mentions both benefits and criticisms)
  7. B) reducing pressure on drainage systems
  8. B) urban climate and drainage
  9. B) skilled work required (maintenance requires skilled labour)
  10. B) Rooftop Gardens: Benefits and Challenges

Section 9 — Cloze Test​

  1. B) hard (work hard = adverb)
  2. A) However (contrast)
  3. B) rework (result of changed requirements)
  4. A) increased (frustration grows from rework)
  5. A) but (contrast: frustrated, yet delivered on time)
  6. B) with (satisfied with)
  7. B) rapidly (grown rapidly)
  8. B) from (courses from top universities)
  9. B) from (access...from their homes)
  10. B) However (contrast: growth but challenge)

Section 10 — Idioms & Phrases​

  1. B) to be exactly right
  2. B) an apparent misfortune that turns out well
  3. B) to reveal a secret
  4. B) very rarely
  5. B) to face a difficult situation bravely
  6. B) to avoid the main point
  7. B) it's your turn to act
  8. B) to work late into the night
  9. B) to start a conversation
