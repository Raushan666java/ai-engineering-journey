## Tips

- **Pehle Learning-Techniques padho** — kaise padhna hai yeh samajhna zaroori hai
- **Study-Plans dekho** — overall roadmap samajhne ke liye
- **Phir Main-Course shuru karo** — 90-Day-Plan follow karo
- **DSA-Practice parallel chalte raho** — har din 1-2 problems
- **PDF-References tab use karo** jab koi concept clear na ho
- **Hinglish mein padho** — samajh aana chahiye, english ratna nahi

> Placement dena hai toh yeh enough hai. Bas consistently padho.
