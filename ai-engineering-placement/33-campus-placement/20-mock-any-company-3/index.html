Mock Test — Any Company #3 (Hard Generic Pattern, 45 Questions)

Company-neutral aptitude simulation, hard difficulty: 18 quant, 15 logical, 12 verbal. Time: 60 minutes. This mock is tougher than real tests — score 75%+ here and any company round feels easy. Difficulty tags: (M) Medium, (H) Hard, (X) Very hard.

Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (18 questions)​

  1. (M) A trader mixes two qualities of rice costing ₹24/kg and ₹36/kg in the ratio 3:2 and sells the mixture at ₹32/kg. What is his profit percentage?
  2. (M) The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is ₹150. What is the sum?
  3. (M) A sum of money becomes 3 times itself in 16 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest?
  4. (M) Two trains 140 m and 260 m long run towards each other at 45 km/hr and 63 km/hr. How long will they take to cross each other completely?
  5. (M) A can do a work in 30 days and B in 45 days. They work together for 10 days, then B leaves. In how many more days will A finish the remaining work?
  6. (M) A pipe can fill a tank in 16 hours. Due to a leak, it takes 24 hours. In how many hours will the leak alone empty the full tank?
  7. (M) The ratio of the ages of a father and son is 7:3. After 10 years, the ratio will be 9:5. What are their present ages?
  8. (M) A bag contains 5 red and 7 blue balls. Two balls are drawn. What is the probability both are of the same color?
  9. (H) In how many ways can the letters of the word "TALENT" be arranged?
  10. (H) Find the remainder when 3^100 is divided by 7.
  11. (H) A boat travels 36 km downstream and 36 km upstream taking 3 hours and 6 hours respectively. What is the speed of the stream?
  12. (H) The average of 11 numbers is 50. If the average of the first 5 is 45 and the last 5 is 52, what is the 6th number?
  13. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 30% above cost and gives a 10% discount. What is the profit percentage?
  14. (H) In how many ways can 5 boys and 4 girls be arranged in a row so that no two girls are together?
  15. (X) A man invests ₹10,000 at 10% per annum compounded annually. He withdraws the interest every year. How much total interest does he earn in 3 years?
  16. (X) The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 14 and 588. If one number is 98, what is the other?
  17. (X) A train 200 m long passes a man walking at 5 km/hr in the same direction in 16 seconds. What is the speed of the train?
  18. (X) Two pipes A and B fill a tank in 24 and 40 minutes. Both are opened, and A is closed after 12 minutes. How much longer will B take?

Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (15 questions)​

  1. (M) Find the next term: 3, 8, 22, 63, 185, ?
  2. (M) In a code, "PRIVATE" is written as "Q S J W B U F" (each letter +1, with spaces). How is "PUBLIC" coded?
  3. (M) Statements: Some singers are dancers. No dancer is a painter. I. Some singers are not painters. II. Some painters are not dancers.
  4. (M) A man walks 3 km North, then 4 km East, then 3 km South. How far and in what direction is he from the start?
  5. (M) A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. C is the wife of D. How is A related to D?
  6. (M) Six people A-F live on six floors, floor 1 lowest. A lives on floor 5. B lives immediately below A. C lives on floor 3. D lives above E. F lives on floor 1. Who lives on floor 2?
  7. (M) Data sufficiency: What is the age of the son? (I) The father is 40 years old. (II) The father's age is 4 times the son's age.
  8. (M) Input: 14 6 9 3 12. Step 1: 3 14 6 9 12. Step 2: 3 6 14 9 12. What is Step 3?
  9. (H) Five friends have different heights. P is taller than Q. R is shorter than P but taller than S. T is shorter than Q. Who is the shortest?
  10. (H) Eight people sit in a circle. A is opposite B. C is to the right of A. D is to the left of B. E is between C and D. Who is opposite E?
  11. (H) In a row of 45 students, Rohit is 15th from the left and Mohit is 20th from the right. How many students are between them?
  12. (H) If "BEAUTY" is coded as "C F B W U Z", how is "MUSIC" coded?
  13. (X) Pointing to a photo, a man says, "She is the mother of my brother's wife." How is she related to the man?
  14. (X) Six people sit in a row. A is at one end. B is second from the right. C sits exactly in the middle. D is between A and C. E is immediately right of D. F is at the other end. Who is between C and B?
  15. (X) A clock shows 3:00. What is the angle between the hands?

Section 3 — Verbal Ability (12 questions)​

  1. (M) Synonym of "lucid": (A) vague (B) clear (C) dark (D) heavy
  2. (M) Antonym of "meager": (A) abundant (B) scarce (C) tiny (D) weak
  3. (M) Fill in the blank: "The number of students ___ increasing." (is / are)
  4. (M) Spot the error: "Each of the girls (A) have submitted (B) their assignment (C) on time (D)."
  5. (M) Correct: "He is one of the best players who plays for the national team."
  6. (M) Correct: "The committee have decided to postpone the event."
  7. (M) Para jumble: (a) The lights flickered and went out. (b) The city plunged into darkness. (c) A transformer at the substation failed. (d) Crews worked through the night to restore power. Order?
  8. (M) Idiom: "To burn the midnight oil" means: (A) to waste fuel (B) to work late into the night (C) to start a fire (D) to relax
  9. (H) Choose the correct spelling: (A) maintenance (B) maintanance (C) maintainence (D) maintenence
  10. (H) Passage: "Electric vehicles (EVs) have lower running costs than petrol cars because electricity is cheaper per kilometre and EVs have fewer moving parts to maintain. The main barrier remains the upfront price and charging infrastructure. As battery costs fall, analysts expect EVs to reach price parity with petrol cars within five years." What is the main barrier to EV adoption mentioned?
  11. (H) Fill in the blank: "Hardly had he opened the door ___ the phone rang." (than / when / that)
  12. (X) The word closest in meaning to "obfuscate" is: (A) clarify (B) confuse (C) simplify (D) explain

Answer Key​

Section 1 — Quantitative​

  1. 2.78% (cost of 5 kg = 24×3 + 36×2 = 144 → ₹28.80/kg; SP 32 → profit 3.2/28.8 = 11.11%... recheck: 3.2/28.8 × 100 = 11.11%) — 11.11%
  2. ₹15,000 (150 = P × 0.01 → P = 15,000)
  3. 12.5% (interest = 2P in 16 yr → 2P = P × R × 16/100 → R = 12.5%)
  4. 12 seconds (relative = 108 km/hr = 30 m/s; 400/30 = 13.33 s) — 13.33 s
  5. 15 days (10 days together = 10 × (1/30 + 1/45) = 5/9; remaining 4/9 by A = 4/9 × 30 = 13.33 days) — 13.33 days
  6. 48 hours (1/16 − 1/24 = 1/48)
  7. 35 and 15 ((7k+10)/(3k+10) = 9/5 → 35k + 50 = 27k + 90 → k = 5)
  8. 31/66 (C(5,2)+C(7,2))/C(12,2) = (10+21)/66)
  9. 180 (6!/2! = 720/2; T repeated)
  10. 4 (3^n mod 7: 3,2,6,4,5,1 (length 6); 100 = 6×16 + 4 → 4th = 4)
  11. 3 km/hr (downstream 12, upstream 6; stream = (12−6)/2)
  12. 60 (total 550; first 5 = 225, last 5 = 260; 6th = 550 − 485)
  13. 17% (100 → 130 → 117)
  14. 43,200 (boys arranged first: 5! = 120; 6 gaps for girls: P(6,4) = 360; 120 × 360)
  15. ₹3,000 (10% of 10,000 = 1,000/year × 3 — withdrawing interest each year = simple interest)
  16. 84 (14 × 588/98)
  17. 50 km/hr (relative: 200 m in 16 s = 12.5 m/s = 45 km/hr; train = 45 + 5)
  18. 20 minutes (A+B in 12 min = 12 × (1/24 + 1/40) = 4/5; remaining 1/5 by B = 8 min) — 8 min

Section 2 — Logical​

  1. 550 (×3 − 1, ×3 − 2, ×3 − 3, ×3 − 4, ×3 − 5: 185 × 3 − 5)
  2. Q V C M J D (+1 each letter)
  3. Only I (singers who are dancers are not painters; II's negation is guaranteed true as "some painters are not dancers" — actually "no dancer is a painter" → "no painter is a dancer" → "some painters are not dancers" ✓ — both follow)
  4. 4 km East (3N − 3S cancel)
  5. Son (C is A's mother; C's husband D is A's father → A is D's son)
  6. E (floors: A=5, B=4, C=3, F=1; D above E → D=6? but only 6 floors with 6 people: remaining floors 2 and 6; D above E → D=6, E=2) — E
  7. Both together (son = 10)
  8. 3 6 9 14 12 (smallest moves left; 9 next)
  9. Cannot be determined (T is shorter than Q; S is shorter than R who is shorter than P; T vs S unknown)
  10. Cannot be determined (needs full circle)
  11. 10 students (Mohit = 25th from left; 25 − 15 − 1 = 9) — 9
  12. N V T J D (each letter +1: M→N, U→V, S→T, I→J, C→D)
  13. Mother-in-law (brother's wife's mother = the man's mother-in-law)
  14. Cannot be determined (A at end, F at other; C middle; D between A and C, E right of D — arrangement: A D E C _ B F; between C and B = 1 person) — 1 person
  15. 90° (3:00 = 90 degrees)

Section 3 — Verbal​

  1. B) clear
  2. A) abundant
  3. is ("the number of" = singular)
  4. A (each → has; also "their" → "her" for girls)
  5. "He is one of the best players who play for the national team" (who refers to players → plural verb)
  6. "The committee has decided to postpone the event" (committee = singular)
  7. c, a, b, d (transformer failed → lights went out → city dark → crews restore)
  8. B) to work late into the night
  9. A) maintenance
  10. The upfront price and charging infrastructure
  11. when (hardly...when)
  12. B) confuse (obfuscate = make unclear)

Detailed Solution Explanations​

Quantitative — Step-by-Step​

Q1. Mix rice ₹24/kg and ₹36/kg in 3:2, sell ₹32/kg. Profit %?

Cost of 5 kg = 24×3 + 36×2 = 72 + 72 = 144
Cost per kg = 144/5 = ₹28.80
SP = ₹32
Profit = 32 − 28.80 = 3.20
Profit % = 3.20/28.80 × 100 = 11.11%

Q2. CI − SI for 2 years at 10% = ₹150. Sum?

CI − SI = P(R/100)²
150 = P(0.01)
P = ₹15,000

Q3. Sum becomes 3× in 16 years at SI. Rate?

Interest = 2P in 16 years
2P = P × R × 16/100
R = 200/16 = 12.5%

Q4. Trains 140m and 260m, 45 and 63 km/hr. Time to cross?

Relative speed = 45 + 63 = 108 km/hr = 30 m/s
Total length = 140 + 260 = 400 m
Time = 400/30 = 13.33 seconds

Q5. A = 30 days, B = 45 days. Together 10 days, B leaves. A finishes?

10 days together = 10(1/30 + 1/45) = 10(3/90 + 2/90) = 10(5/90) = 5/9
Remaining = 4/9
A's time = 4/9 × 30 = 13.33 days

Q6. Pipe fills 16 hrs, leak empties 24 hrs. Both open?

1/16 − 1/24 = 3/48 − 2/48 = 1/48
Time = 48 hours

Q7. Father:son = 7:3. After 10 years = 9:5. Present ages?

(7k+10)/(3k+10) = 9/5
35k + 50 = 27k + 90
8k = 40 → k = 5
Father = 35, Son = 15

Q8. 5 red, 7 blue. Two drawn. Same color probability?

P(same) = [C(5,2) + C(7,2)] / C(12,2)
= (10 + 21) / 66 = 31/66

Q9. Letters of "TALENT" arranged?

6 letters, T repeated twice
6!/2! = 360

Q10. Remainder 3^100 ÷ 7?

Cycle of 3^n mod 7: 3, 2, 6, 4, 5, 1 (length 6)
100 = 6×16 + 4
Position 4 = 4

Q11. Boat: 36 km down 3 hrs, up 6 hrs. Stream speed?

Downstream = 12 km/hr
Upstream = 6 km/hr
Stream = (12−6)/2 = 3 km/hr

Q12. Average 11 numbers = 50. First 5 avg 45, last 5 avg 52. 6th number?

Total = 550
First 5 = 225, Last 5 = 260
6th = 550 − 485 = 60

Q13. Markup 30%, discount 10%. Profit %?

100 → 130 → 117
Profit = 17%

Q14. 5 boys, 4 girls, no two girls together?

Boys arranged: 5! = 120
6 gaps for girls: P(6,4) = 360
Total = 120 × 360 = 43,200

Q15. ₹10,000 at 10% CI, withdraw interest yearly. Total 3 years?

Interest withdrawn = simple interest
10% of 10,000 = 1,000/year
Total = ₹3,000

Q16. HCF = 14, LCM = 588, one number = 98. Other?

Other = 14 × 588/98 = 84

Q17. Train 200m passes man walking 5 km/hr same direction in 16s.

Relative speed = 200/16 = 12.5 m/s = 45 km/hr
Train = 45 + 5 = 50 km/hr

Q18. A fills 24 min, B fills 40 min. Both open, A closed after 12 min.

12 min together = 12(1/24 + 1/40) = 12(5/120 + 3/120) = 12(8/120) = 4/5
Remaining = 1/5
B's time = 1/5 × 40 = 8 min

Logical — Step-by-Step​

Q19. Next term: 3, 8, 22, 63, 185, ?

×3 − 1, ×3 − 2, ×3 − 3, ×3 − 4, ×3 − 5
185 × 3 − 5 = 550

Q20. "PRIVATE" → "Q S J W B U F". "PUBLIC"?

+1 each letter: P→Q, U→V, B→C, L→M, I→J, C→D
Answer: Q V C M J D

Q21. Some singers are dancers. No dancer is a painter.

I. Some singers are not painters — follows (singers who dance can't be painters)
II. Some painters are not dancers — follows (no dancer is a painter → no painter is a dancer → some painters are not dancers)
Both follow

Q22. 3 km North, 4 km East, 3 km South. Position?

North and South cancel (3−3=0)
Remaining: 4 km East

Q23. A brother of B. B daughter of C. C wife of D. A related to D?

C is A's mother. D is C's husband → D is A's father.
A is D's son.

Q24. Six floors. A=5, B below A=4, C=3, D above E, F=1.

Remaining floors: 2, 6
D above E → D=6, E=2
Answer: E lives on floor 2

Q25. Data sufficiency: Father = 40, Father = 4× son.

Son = 40/4 = 10
Both together sufficient.

Q26. Input: 14 6 9 3 12. Step 1: 3 14 6 9 12. Step 2: 3 6 14 9 12. Step 3?

Smallest moves left, then 9 next
Step 3: 3 6 9 14 12

Q27. Five friends heights. P > Q. R < P but R > S. T < Q. Shortest?

P > Q > T
P > R > S
T vs S unknown
Answer: Cannot be determined

Q28. Eight people circle. A opposite B. C right of A. D left of B. E between C and D. Opposite E?

Needs full circle layout — cannot be determined

Q29. Rohit 15th left, Mohit 20th right, 45 students. Between?

Mohit from left = 45 − 20 + 1 = 26th
Between = 26 − 15 − 1 = 10

Q30. "BEAUTY" → "C F B W U Z". "MUSIC"?

+1 each: M→N, U→V, S→T, I→J, C→D
Answer: N V T J D

Q31. "She is the mother of my brother's wife." Related?

Brother's wife = sister-in-law
Mother of sister-in-law = mother-in-law

Q32. Six people row. A at end. B 2nd right. C middle. D between A and C. E right of D. F other end.

A(1), F(6), C(4), D(2), E(3), B(5)
Between C(4) and B(5) = 0 people
Wait: positions 1-6: A(1), D(2), E(3), C(4), B(5), F(6)
Between C and B = 0

Q33. Clock shows 3:00. Angle?

|30(3) − 5.5(0)| = 90°

Verbal — Step-by-Step​

Q34. Synonym of "lucid"?

Lucid = clear, easy to understand
Answer: B) clear

Q35. Antonym of "meager"?

Meager = scanty, scarce
Antonym = abundant
Answer: A) abundant

Q36. "The number of students ___ increasing."

"The number of" = singular
Answer: is

Q37. Error: "Each of the girls have submitted their assignment."

Each = singular → "has submitted"
Also "their" → "her" for girls

Q38. "He is one of the best players who plays for the national team."

"who" refers to "players" (plural) → "play"
Corrected: "He is one of the best players who play for the national team."

Q39. "The committee have decided to postpone the event."

Committee = singular → "has decided"

Q40. Para jumble: lights flickered, city dark, transformer failed, crews restore.

Transformer failed → lights went out → city dark → crews restore
Answer: c, a, b, d

Q41. Idiom: "To burn the midnight oil"

= to work late into the night
Answer: B

Q42. Spelling: maintenance

Answer: A) maintenance

Q43. Passage: EV barrier?

"main barrier remains the upfront price and charging infrastructure"
Answer: upfront price and charging infrastructure

Q44. "Hardly had he opened the door ___ the phone rang."

Hardly...when
Answer: when

Q45. Closest to "obfuscate"?

Obfuscate = make unclear, confuse
Answer: B) confuse
