# Capgemini Mock Test — Full Pattern Simulation (42 Questions)

> Simulates the Capgemini aptitude section: 18 quant, 12 logical, 12 verbal.
> Time limits: Quant 30 min, Logical 20 min, Verbal 15 min. Total: 65 minutes.
> Capgemini is arithmetic-heavy — profit/loss, SI/CI and mixtures appear every year.
> Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

## Capgemini Pattern — What You Need to Know

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Aptitude | 18 questions in 30 minutes |
| Logical | 12 questions in 20 minutes |
| Verbal | 12 questions in 15 minutes |
| Negative marking | No penalty in most years |
| Passing | 70%+ recommended |
| Flavor | Profit/loss, SI/CI, mixtures & alligation, number system heavy |

## Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (18 questions) — 30 minutes

1. (E) What is 45% of 320?
2. (E) A shopkeeper sells an article for ₹828 at a 15% profit. What was the cost price?
3. (M) A man buys 20 pens for ₹200 and sells each at ₹12. What is his profit percentage?
4. (M) A train 240 m long crosses a platform 360 m long in 30 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
5. (M) A and B together can do a job in 8 days. A alone takes 24 days. How long will B take?
6. (M) What is the CI on ₹12,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years?
7. (M) In what ratio should water be mixed with milk costing ₹48 per litre to sell at ₹36 with no profit or loss?
8. (M) The average of 15 numbers is 60. If one number 90 is removed, what is the new average?
9. (M) Find the next term: 6, 14, 30, 62, 126, ?
10. (M) A bag contains 9 red and 5 blue balls. Two are drawn. What is the probability both are red?
11. (M) The product of two numbers is 840 and their HCF is 14. What is their LCM?
12. (M) A sum of money becomes 3 times itself in 12 years at SI. What is the rate of interest?
13. (M) A boat travels 28 km downstream in 2 hours and 28 km upstream in 7 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
14. (M) Two pipes fill a tank in 18 minutes and 27 minutes. Both open. How long to fill?
15. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 25% above cost and gives a 12% discount. What is the profit percentage?
16. (H) The ages of a father and son are in the ratio 5:2. After 10 years, the ratio will be 7:4. What is the son's present age?
17. (H) In how many ways can the letters of the word "BETTER" be arranged?
18. (H) Find the remainder when 5^87 is divided by 6.

## Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (12 questions) — 20 minutes

    I. Some engineers are managers. II. All engineers are graduates.

## Section 3 — Verbal Ability (12 questions) — 15 minutes

## Detailed Solution Explanations

### Quantitative — Step-by-Step

**Q1.** 45% of 320?

```
320 × 0.45 = 144
```

**Q2.** SP ₹828, profit 15%. CP?

```
828/1.15 = ₹720
```

**Q3.** 20 pens ₹200, sell each ₹12. Profit %?

```
Cost per pen = 10, SP = 12
Profit = 2/10 = 20%
```

**Q4.** Train 240m, platform 360m, 30 seconds. Speed?

```
600/30 = 20 m/s = 72 km/hr
```

**Q5.** A+B = 8 days, A alone = 24 days. B alone?

```
1/B = 1/8 − 1/24 = 3/24 − 1/24 = 2/24 = 1/12
B = 12 days
```

**Q6.** CI on ₹12,000 at 10% for 2 years?

```
12000 × 0.21 = ₹2,520
```

**Q7.** Water mixed with milk ₹48/litre, sell ₹36. Ratio?

```
Alligation: (48−36):(36−0) = 12:36 = 1:3
Water:milk = 1:3
```

**Q8.** Average 15 numbers = 60. Remove 90. New average?

```
Total = 900, minus 90 = 810
810/14 = 57.86
```

**Q9.** Next term: 6, 14, 30, 62, 126, ?

```
×2 + 2: 126 × 2 + 2 = 254
```

**Q10.** 9 red, 5 blue. Two drawn. P(both red)?

```
C(9,2)/C(14,2) = 36/91
```

**Q11.** Product 840, HCF 14. LCM?

```
840/14 = 60
```

**Q12.** Sum becomes 3× in 12 years at SI. Rate?

```
2P = P × R × 12/100
R = 200/12 = 16.67%
```

**Q13.** Boat: 28 km down 2 hrs, up 7 hrs. Stream?

```
Down = 14, Up = 4
Stream = (14−4)/2 = 5 km/hr
```

**Q14.** Two pipes: 18 min and 27 min. Together?

```
18 × 27/(18+27) = 486/45 = 10.8 min
```

**Q15.** Markup 25%, discount 12%. Profit?

```
100 → 125 → 110
Profit = 10%
```

**Q16.** Father:son = 5:2, after 10 years = 7:4. Son's age?

```
(5k+10)/(2k+10) = 7/4
20k + 40 = 14k + 70
6k = 30 → k = 5
Son = 2k = 10
```

**Q17.** Letters of "BETTER" arranged?

```
6 letters, T repeated twice, E repeated twice
6!/(2!×2!) = 180
```

**Q18.** Remainder 5^87 ÷ 6?

```
5^n mod 6 = 5 for all n (since 5 ≡ −1 mod 6, and (−1)^odd = −1 ≡ 5)
Answer: 5
```

### Logical — Step-by-Step

**Q19.** Next term: 4, 8, 16, 32, ?

```
×2: 32 × 2 = 64
```

**Q20.** "CHINA" → "DIJOB". "JAPAN"?

```
+1 each: J→K, A→B, P→Q, A→B, N→O → KBQBO
```

**Q21.** All engineers are graduates. Some graduates are managers.

```
I. Some engineers are managers — NOT guaranteed
II. All engineers are graduates — stated
Answer: Only II
```

**Q22.** 6 km East, 8 km North. Distance?

```
√(36+64) = 10 km
```

**Q23.** A son of B. B sister of C. C related to A?

```
B is A's mother. B's sister C is A's aunt/uncle.
```

**Q24.** Priya 15th from left, 40 students. Position from right?

```
40 − 15 + 1 = 26th
```

**Q25.** Data sufficiency: Son = 15, Father = 3× son.

```
Father = 45
Both together sufficient.
```

**Q26.** Input: 8 3 6 1 5. Step 1: 1 8 3 6 5. Step 2: 1 3 8 6 5. Step 3?

```
Smallest moves left, then 6 next
Step 3: 1 3 6 8 5
```

**Q27.** Odd one out: 9, 16, 25, 36, 45

```
9=3², 16=4², 25=5², 36=6², 45=not a square
Answer: 45
```

**Q28.** Seven people. A 3rd left, B 3rd right, C between A and B.

```
A(3), B(5), C(4)
Between C and B = 0 people
```

**Q29.** Company offers free training to all new hires.

```
I. New hires need training — reasonable
II. All companies offer free training — overgeneralization
Answer: Only I
```

**Q30.** "SUN" → "TVO". "MOON"?

```
+1 each: M→N, O→P, O→P, N→O → NPPO
```

### Verbal — Step-by-Step

**Q31.** Synonym of "huge"?

```
Huge = enormous
Answer: B) enormous
```

**Q32.** Antonym of "increase"?

```
Increase = grow, expand
Antonym = decrease
Answer: B) decrease
```

**Q33.** "The number of accidents ___ declining."

```
"The number of" = singular
Answer: is
```

**Q34.** Error: "Each of the boys have completed."

```
Each = singular → "has completed"
```

**Q35.** "The manager, along with his team, are attending."

```
Subject = manager (singular) → "is attending"
```

**Q36.** Para jumble: website live, registered, prepared, handled.

```
Prepared → went live → registered → handled
Answer: c, a, b, d
```

**Q37.** Spelling: accommodation

```
Answer: A) accommodation
```

**Q38.** Idiom: "To make a long story short"

```
= to summarize briefly
Answer: B
```

**Q39.** "She is allergic ___ dust."

```
Allergic to
Answer: to
```

**Q40.** Passage: Data centers consume?

```
"Electricity and water"
```

**Q41.** "Hardly had she finished ___ the bell rang."

```
Hardly...when
Answer: when
```

**Q42.** Closest to "candid"?

```
Candid = frank, honest
Answer: B) frank
```
