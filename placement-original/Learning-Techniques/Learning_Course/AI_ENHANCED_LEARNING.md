### Ethical AI Learning

#### Best Practices
**Appropriate Use:**
- Understanding concepts, not copying answers
- Generating practice problems, not homework solutions
- Explaining logic, not providing direct code
- Supporting learning, not replacing thinking

**Avoiding Pitfalls:**
- Don't become dependent on AI for basic thinking
- Verify AI explanations with authoritative sources
- Practice without AI assistance regularly
- Maintain critical thinking skills

#### Academic Integrity
**Guidelines:**
- Use AI for learning, not assessment completion
- Cite AI assistance when required
- Understand concepts, don't just copy
- Develop independent problem-solving skills
