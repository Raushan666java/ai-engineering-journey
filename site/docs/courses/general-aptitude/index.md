# General Aptitude

> A complete aptitude training course covering quantitative ability, logical reasoning, verbal ability, and data interpretation — from foundational concepts to advanced problem-solving for competitive exams, campus placements, and interviews.

## Prerequisites

Basic arithmetic and high school mathematics. English reading ability at the 10th-grade level. No specialized preparation required.

## Course Description

General aptitude is the single most tested skill across campus placements, GATE, CAT, GRE, GMAT, banking, and government exams. This course provides systematic coverage of every aptitude category, with progressive difficulty from beginner through advanced. Each chapter follows a learn-by-doing approach: concise theory → formula reference → solved examples at three difficulty levels → graded exercise sets.

The course is structured for self-paced learning. Quantitative and logical reasoning chapters build foundational numeracy and analytical thinking. Verbal ability chapters strengthen command of English. Data interpretation and non-verbal reasoning chapters develop visual and inferential skills tested in modern assessments.

## How to Use This Text

1. **Learn the formulas** in each chapter before attempting problems
2. **Solve each example** on your own, then compare with the worked solution
3. **Time yourself** on exercise sets — speed is as important as accuracy
4. **Review weak areas** using the cross-reference table in the summary
