# Phase 10 — D-Day Protocol

> **The countdown.** What you do in the 7 days, 24 hours, and 60 minutes before an interview matters more than another LeetCode problem.

This phase is not generic prep advice. It's a calendar-locked playbook. Each drill is anchored to a specific time marker — 7 days out, 72 hours, 24 hours, 4 hours, 1 hour, *during the interview when you blank*, and *after you walk out*.

You don't read these end-to-end. You open the one that matches where you are on the clock.

---

## The 7 drills

| When | Drill | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 7 days before | [Day 7 — The Week Plan](day-7.md) | Daily 2–4 hour blocks across the week |
| 3 days before | [Day 3 — The Crunch Playbook](day-3.md) | What to drill, what to skip, what to abandon |
| 24 hours before | [Day 1 — No New Learning](day-1.md) | Review, logistics, sleep |
| 4 hours before | [Four Hours Before](four-hours-before.md) | Morning routine + quick review |
| 1 hour before | [One Hour Before](one-hour-before.md) | Travel, mindfulness, posture |
| During | [When You Blank](when-you-blank.md) | Recovery scripts for live moments |
| After | [After the Interview](after-interview.md) | Notes, thank-you email, follow-up |

---

## How to use this phase

- **Two weeks out from an interview?** Open `day-7.md`, follow the week plan.
- **Got the call three days ago and panicking?** Open `day-3.md`, do the 5-thing prioritization.
- **It's tomorrow?** Open `day-1.md` and put the laptop away.
- **Walking into the lobby?** Open `one-hour-before.md` on your phone.
- **Mid-interview, drawing a blank?** You won't open the file then — but if you've drilled `when-you-blank.md` once, the recovery line will surface automatically.
- **Walked out?** Open `after-interview.md` within the hour. The follow-up email is a competitive edge.
