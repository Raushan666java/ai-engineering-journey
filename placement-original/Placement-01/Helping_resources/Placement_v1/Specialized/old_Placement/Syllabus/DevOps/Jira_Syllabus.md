# Jira for DevOps Syllabus

- Introduction to Jira: project management concepts
- Creating and managing projects
- Issue types: stories, tasks, bugs, epics
- Boards: Scrum, Kanban
- Backlog management
- Sprints: planning, starting, closing
- Assigning and tracking issues
- Workflows: statuses, transitions, custom workflows
- Filters and search queries (JQL)
- Dashboards and reporting
- Integrations: GitHub, Slack, CI/CD tools
- Automation rules
- Permissions and roles
- Best practices for DevOps teams
