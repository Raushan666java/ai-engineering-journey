## 🎓 KEY LEARNINGS

### Technical Concepts
1. **Rate limiting** prevents abuse and ensures fair resource allocation
2. **Circuit breakers** prevent cascading failures in distributed systems
3. **Sharding** enables horizontal scaling of databases
4. **Caching** dramatically improves performance and reduces load
5. **Consistent hashing** minimizes redistribution during scaling
6. **Saga pattern** manages distributed transactions without 2PC
7. **Event sourcing** provides complete audit trail and time travel
8. **CQRS** enables independent scaling of reads and writes

### Design Principles
1. **Design for failure**: Everything fails; plan accordingly
2. **Scale horizontally**: Add more machines, not bigger ones
3. **Cache aggressively**: Cache at every layer
4. **Decouple components**: Use queues and events
5. **Monitor everything**: Metrics, logs, traces

### Interview Strategies
1. **Use framework**: Requirements → Design → Deep Dive
2. **Discuss trade-offs**: No perfect solution exists
3. **Calculate estimates**: Back of envelope calculations
4. **Draw diagrams**: Visual > verbal explanations
5. **Ask questions**: Clarify requirements before designing
