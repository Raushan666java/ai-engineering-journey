## 🔍 Tips
- Sort properly: end-time, start-time, or ratio-based sorting
- Use priority queues for online greedy decisions (job scheduling)
- Watch for counterexamples; greedy isn't always optimal

**Practice Tip:** Solve at least 3 greedy problems daily and understand why sorting key works.
