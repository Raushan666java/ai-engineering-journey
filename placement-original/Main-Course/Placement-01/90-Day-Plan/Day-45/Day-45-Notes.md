# Day 45 Notes

- Merge Sort uses O(n) additional space in typical implementation; bottom-up and in-place variants exist.
- Quick sort average O(n log n), worst O(n^2) depending on pivot; choose random pivot or median-of-three.
- Closest pair using divide and conquer sorts points by x and by y when needed, and checks a 'strip' for O(n) merging.
- Be careful about squared distances vs real distances to avoid precision issues.
