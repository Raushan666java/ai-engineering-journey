### 💡 **Key Takeaways**

> **"When to use which collection?"**
> - ArrayList: Fast random access
> - LinkedList: Frequent insertions at ends
> - HashSet: Unique elements, fast operations
> - TreeSet: Sorted unique elements
> - HashMap: Key-value pairs, fast lookup
> - TreeMap: Sorted key-value pairs
> - PriorityQueue: Priority-based processing
> - ArrayDeque: Stack/queue operations

> **"PECS Principle"**
> - Producer Extends: List&lt;? extends T&gt; for reading
> - Consumer Super: List&lt;? super T&gt; for writing

> **"Type Erasure"**
> - Generic types removed at compile time
> - T becomes Object (or first bound)
> - Cannot create T[], use List&lt;T&gt;
> - Pass Class&lt;T&gt; for runtime type info
