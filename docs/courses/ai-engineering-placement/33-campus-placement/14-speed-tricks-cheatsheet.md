# Speed Tricks Cheatsheet — 40 Instant-Recognition Rules

> Memorize these cold. Recite them from memory before every drill session.
> Each rule saves 30-60 seconds in the real test — that is the difference between
> "attempted 60%" and "attempted 95%".

## Percentages & Profit/Loss (1-8)

1. **Net percentage change**: a% then b% = `a + b + ab/100` (with signs). +20% then −20% = **−4%**.
2. **Successive discounts** d1, d2 = single discount `d1 + d2 − (d1×d2)/100`. 20% + 10% = **28%**.
3. **Percent more/less asymmetry**: if A is r% more than B, B is `r/(100+r) × 100%` less than A. 25% more → B is **20%** less.
4. **Price rise → consumption cut** for constant spend: cut = `r/(100+r) × 100%`. 25% rise → cut **20%**.
5. **Discount is on MP, profit is on CP.** Always chain through 100: CP 100 → MP → SP → profit %.
6. **x% of y = y% of x**: 16% of 25 = 25% of 16 = **4**.
7. **Loss/profit difference trick**: sell at p% profit vs l% loss, difference `(p + l)%` of CP. 15% profit vs 5% loss, ₹120 apart → CP = **₹600**.
8. **Markup+discount combo**: markup m%, discount d% → net profit = `m − d − md/100`. 30% + 10% = **17%**.

## Interest (9-13)

9. **CI − SI (2 years)** = `P × (R/100)²`. At 8%, P 20,000 → diff = 20,000 × 0.0064 = **₹128**.
10. **CI − SI (3 years)** = `P × (R/100)² × (3 + R/100)`.
11. **SI doubling time** = `100/R` years. Rate 8% → doubles in **12.5 years**.
12. **Interest-withdrawal = simple interest**: if interest is withdrawn yearly, CI reduces to SI. 10,000 at 10% for 3 years withdrawn yearly = **₹3,000**.
13. **Amounts in geometric progression** under CI: P, Pr, Pr²... 3-year amount at 10% = ×1.331. Reverse: divide by 1.331.

## Speed, Distance & Trains (14-19)

14. **km/hr → m/s**: × 5/18. 72 km/hr = **20 m/s**. m/s → km/hr: × 18/5.
15. **Average speed (equal distances)** = `2ab/(a+b)`. 30 and 20 km/hr → **24 km/hr**. Never the arithmetic mean.
16. **Trains: add the lengths.** Crossing each other: (L1+L2) / relative speed. Crossing a pole: train length only.
17. **Relative speed**: same direction = difference, opposite = sum.
18. **Train passes a walking man**: train speed = relative speed + walker speed (same direction).
19. **Boats & streams**: downstream = b+s, upstream = b−s. Boat speed = (down+up)/2, stream = (down−up)/2. Memorize both.

## Work & Pipes (20-24)

20. **Together days** = `ab/(a+b)`. A 12, B 18 → **7.2 days**.
21. **One works, one leaves**: compute fraction done, remaining = 1 − done; extra days = remaining × (leaver's partner's time).
22. **Pipes: leaks are negative rates.** Fill 12, leak 18 → net 1/36 → **36 hours** (bigger than either — sanity check!).
23. **Men × days = constant** (man-days). 20 men × 12 days = 240; for 8 days → **30 men**.
24. **"A is twice as good as B"**: rates 2:1 → total rate 3x. Together time known → solve x, then A alone.

## Ratios, Mixtures & Alligation (25-29)

25. **Unify ratios**: a:b and b:c → scale b to LCM. 4:5 and 3:7 → **12:15:35**.
26. **Age ratios**: (ratio + k) form two equations; solve for k. Never guess ages.
27. **Alligation cross**: cheaper : dearer = `(mean − dearer) : (cheaper − mean)`.
28. **Water added to milk**: water : milk = `(cost − mean) : (mean − 0)`.
29. **Alligation gives the RATIO, not quantities** — multiply by total for actual amounts.

## P&C and Probability (30-33)

30. **Order matters → permutation, not → combination.** "Arranged" = P, "chosen/committee" = C.
31. **Girls/boys together**: block = 1 unit → `(n − g + 1)! × g!`. 5 boys, 3 girls together → 6! × 3! = **4,320**.
32. **No two girls together**: arrange boys first (n!), then fill gaps P(gaps, girls). 5 boys, 4 girls → 5! × P(6,4) = **43,200**.
33. **"At least one" = 1 − P(none).** 3 coin tosses, at least one head = 1 − 1/8 = **7/8**.

## Number System & Series (34-37)

34. **Cyclicity**: last-digit cycles. 2: 2,4,8,6; 3: 3,9,7,1; 7: 7,9,3,1. Exponent mod 4 → position. 7^250 → cycle pos 2 → **9**.
35. **LCM × HCF = product** of two numbers. Always the first move for two-number problems.
36. **Divisibility**: 3/9 → digit sum; 11 → alternating sum; 5 → ends 0/5; 4 → last two digits.
37. **Series = differences first.** No pattern in differences → try ×2+k, ×3−k. Second-level differences catch squares.

## Clocks, Calendar & Geometry (38-40)

38. **Clock relative speed 5.5°/min.** Angle = |30H − 5.5M|. 4:30 → |120 − 165| = **45°**. Hands overlap every 65 5/11 min.
39. **Odd days**: normal year = 1, leap year = 2. Day = start + odd days mod 7.
40. **Geometry quickies**: square diagonal d → area d²/2; inscribed square in circle r → area 2r²; 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17 Pythagorean triples.

## The 5 Rules of Speed

1. **Recite the list from memory** every morning (2 minutes).
2. **Never open formulas during drills** — looking up = not memorized.
3. **When stuck >90s, mark and move.** A mock score with 3 skips beats a full paper with 6 wrong.
4. **Do arithmetic on paper quickly** — calculators are rarely allowed in real tests.
5. **Wrong answer > blank answer** in no-negative-marking tests. Always guess after eliminating.
