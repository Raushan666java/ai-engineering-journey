Mock Test — Any Company #2 (Generic Pattern, 40 Questions)

Company-neutral aptitude simulation: 16 quant, 12 logical, 12 verbal. Time: 60 minutes. Rule: 60-90 seconds per question. Answers at the bottom — no peeking. Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (16 questions)​

  1. (E) A number is increased by 30% and then decreased by 30%. What is the net percentage change?
  2. (E) If 25% of a number is 60, what is 45% of that number?
  3. (E) A shopkeeper bought an item for ₹500 and sold it for ₹575. What is his profit percentage?
  4. (E) A train 120 m long crosses a pole in 6 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
  5. (M) The population of a town grows at 10% per year. If it is now 2,42,000, what was it 2 years ago?
  6. (M) A and B together can do a job in 10 days. A alone takes 25 days. How long will B take alone?
  7. (M) What sum will amount to ₹6,655 at 10% per annum compounded annually in 2 years?
  8. (M) Two pipes fill a tank in 15 minutes and 25 minutes respectively. Both opened together. How long to fill?
  9. (M) If a:b = 5:6 and b:c = 9:11, find a:c.
  10. (M) The average of 10 numbers is 45. If each number is increased by 3, what is the new average?
  11. (M) In how many ways can a committee of 4 be chosen from 8 people?
  12. (M) A card is drawn from a pack of 52. What is the probability it is a queen?
  13. (M) Find the next term: 1, 5, 13, 29, 61, ?
  14. (M) The product of two numbers is 1,296 and their HCF is 12. What is their LCM?
  15. (M) A boat travels 30 km downstream in 2 hours and 30 km upstream in 5 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
  16. (H) A man sells a watch at a 20% profit. If he had sold it for ₹96 less, he would have made a 4% loss. What is the cost price?

Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (12 questions)​

  1. (E) Find the next term: 4, 13, 40, 121, ?
  2. (E) If "APPLE" is coded as "BQQMF", how is "MANGO" coded?
  3. (E) Odd one out: 9, 25, 49, 63, 81
  4. (M) Statements: All trees are plants. No plant is a stone. I. No tree is a stone. II. All plants are trees.
  5. (M) A man walks 6 km East, then 8 km North. How far is he from the start?
  6. (M) Pointing to a man, a woman says, "He is my father's only son." Who is he?
  7. (M) Five people sit in a row facing North: A is at the left end. B is second to the right of A. C sits between B and D. E is at the right end. Who sits in the middle?
  8. (M) Data sufficiency: Is x divisible by 6? (I) x is divisible by 2. (II) x is divisible by 3.
  9. (M) In a row of 50 students, Priya is 18th from the left. What is her position from the right?
  10. (M) Input: 12 5 8 3 9. Step 1: 3 12 5 8 9. Step 2: 3 5 12 8 9. What is Step 3?
  11. (H) Seven people A-G sit in a row. A sits 3rd from the left. B sits 3rd from the right. C is exactly between A and B. How many people sit between C and B?
  12. (H) Eight friends sit around a circle. P is opposite Q. R is to the immediate right of P. S is to the immediate left of Q. T is between R and S. Who sits opposite T?

Section 3 — Verbal Ability (12 questions)​

  1. (E) Synonym of "rapid": (A) slow (B) swift (C) steady (D) heavy
  2. (E) Antonym of "ancient": (A) old (B) modern (C) antique (D) historic
  3. (M) Fill in the blank: "Neither of the candidates ___ suitable." (is / are)
  4. (M) Spot the error: "The number of employees (A) in the company (B) are increasing (C) every year (D)."
  5. (M) Correct: "One of my colleagues have won the award."
  6. (M) Correct: "The manager said that he will attend the meeting tomorrow."
  7. (M) Para jumble: (a) The parcel arrived the next morning. (b) Rohan ordered a laptop online. (c) It was packed securely. (d) He placed the order at midnight. Order?
  8. (M) Idiom: "To let bygones be bygones" means: (A) to forgive past mistakes (B) to remember everything (C) to forget the future (D) to work harder
  9. (M) Fill in the blank: "He is capable ___ handling difficult clients." (of / in / at)
  10. (M) Choose the correct spelling: (A) privilege (B) privelege (C) priviledge (D) privileg
  11. (H) Passage: "Cloud computing lets companies rent computing power instead of buying servers. This shifts large upfront costs to smaller monthly payments. However, long-running workloads can sometimes cost more on the cloud than on owned hardware." What is one risk of cloud computing mentioned? (A) security (B) higher cost for long-running workloads (C) slower internet (D) fewer features
  12. (H) Fill in the blank: "Scarcely had the train left ___ the platform collapsed." (than / when / that)

Answer Key​

Section 1 — Quantitative​

  1. −9% (1.3 × 0.7 = 0.91)
  2. 108 (number = 60/0.25 = 240; 0.45 × 240)
  3. 15% (75/500)
  4. 72 km/hr (120 m in 6 s = 20 m/s)
  5. 2,00,000 (242000/1.21)
  6. 16.67 days (1/10 − 1/25 = 3/50 → 50/3)
  7. ₹5,500 (6655/1.21)
  8. 9.375 minutes (15 × 25/40)
  9. 15:22 (a:b = 45:54, b:c = 54:66 → a:c = 45:66 = 15:22)
  10. 48 (average shifts +3)
  11. 70 (C(8,4) = 70)
  12. 1/13 (4/52)
  13. 125 (×2 + 3: 61 × 2 + 3)
  14. 108 (1296/12)
  15. 4.5 km/hr (downstream 15, upstream 6; stream = (15−6)/2)
  16. ₹400 (0.20 CP + 0.04 CP = 96 → 0.24 CP = 96 → CP = 400)

Section 2 — Logical​

  1. 364 (×3 + 1: 121 × 3 + 1)
  2. NBOHP (+1 each letter)
  3. 63 (only non-square)
  4. Only I (conversion is valid; II reverses wrongly)
  5. 10 km North-East (6-8-10 triangle)
  6. Her brother (father's only son = her brother)
  7. C (A _ B _ _ → A C B D E → middle = C; order: A, C, B, D, E)
  8. Both together (divisible by 2 AND 3 → divisible by 6)
  9. 33rd from the right (50 − 18 + 1)
  10. 3 5 8 12 9 (smallest moves left each step)
  11. 0 (A at 3, B at 5 (3rd from right of 7); C exactly between → position 4; between C(4) and B(5) = no one)
  12. Cannot be determined (needs full circle layout)

Section 3 — Verbal​

  1. B) swift
  2. B) modern
  3. is (neither = singular)
  4. C ("the number of" = singular → "is increasing")
  5. "One of my colleagues has won the award" (subject = one)
  6. No error ("said that he will attend" — acceptable reported speech)
  7. b, d, a, c (ordered → placed at midnight → arrived → packed)
  8. A) to forgive past mistakes
  9. of (capable of)
  10. A) privilege
  11. B) higher cost for long-running workloads
  12. when (scarcely...when)

Detailed Solution Explanations​

Quantitative — Step-by-Step​

Q1. Net percentage change: +30% then −30%?

1.3 × 0.7 = 0.91 → −9% net

Q2. 25% of number is 60. What is 45%?

Number = 60/0.25 = 240
45% of 240 = 108

Q3. CP ₹500, SP ₹575. Profit %?

Profit = 75
Profit % = 75/500 × 100 = 15%

Q4. Train 120m, pole, 6 seconds. Speed km/hr?

120/6 = 20 m/s
20 × 18/5 = 72 km/hr

Q5. Population now 2,42,000 at 10% growth. 2 years ago?

P × 1.1² = 242000
P = 242000/1.21 = 2,00,000

Q6. A+B = 10 days, A alone = 25 days. B alone?

1/B = 1/10 − 1/25 = 5/50 − 2/50 = 3/50
B = 50/3 = 16.67 days

Q7. Sum amounts to ₹6,655 at 10% CI for 2 years?

P × 1.21 = 6655
P = 6655/1.21 = ₹5,500

Q8. Two pipes: 15 min and 25 min. Together?

15 × 25/(15+25) = 375/40 = 9.375 min

Q9. a:b = 5:6, b:c = 9:11. Find a:c.

Unify b: LCM(6,9) = 18
a:b = 15:18
b:c = 18:22
a:c = 15:22

Q10. Average 10 numbers = 45. Each increased by 3. New average?

New average = 45 + 3 = 48

Q11. Committee of 4 from 8 people?

C(8,4) = 8!/(4!×4!) = 70

Q12. Card drawn from 52. Probability queen?

4 queens / 52 = 1/13

Q13. Next term: 1, 5, 13, 29, 61, ?

×2 + 3: 61 × 2 + 3 = 125

Q14. Product 1296, HCF 12. LCM?

LCM = 1296/12 = 108

Q15. Boat: 30 km downstream 2 hrs, upstream 5 hrs. Stream speed?

Downstream = 15 km/hr
Upstream = 6 km/hr
Stream = (15−6)/2 = 4.5 km/hr

Q16. Sell at 20% profit. If ₹96 less, 4% loss. CP?

SP1 = 1.20 CP
SP2 = 0.96 CP
SP1 − SP2 = 96
1.20 CP − 0.96 CP = 96
0.24 CP = 96
CP = ₹400

Logical — Step-by-Step​

Q17. Next term: 4, 13, 40, 121, ?

×3 + 1: 121 × 3 + 1 = 364

Q18. "APPLE" → "BQQMF". "MANGO"?

+1 each letter: M→N, A→B, N→O, G→H, O→P → NBOHP

Q19. Odd one out: 9, 25, 49, 63, 81

9 = 3², 25 = 5², 49 = 7², 81 = 9²
63 is not a perfect square
Answer: 63

Q20. All trees are plants. No plant is a stone.

I. No tree is a stone — follows (trees ⊂ plants, plants ∩ stone = ∅)
II. All plants are trees — NOT given (could be false)
Answer: Only I

Q21. 6 km East, 8 km North. Distance?

√(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10 km

Q22. "He is my father's only son." Who is he?

Father's only son = her brother

Q23. Five people in a row. A left end. B 2nd right of A. C between B and D. E right end.

Positions: A(1), B(3), E(5)
C between B and D → D(4), C(2)? No, C between B(3) and D
If D(4), C must be between 3 and 4 → not possible
If D(2), C between 2 and 3 → not possible
Adjust: B is 2nd to right of A → A(1), B(3)
C between B(3) and D → D could be 4 or 5
E is at right end → E(5)
So D(4), C between 3 and 4 → C must be at non-integer → ambiguous
Let me re-read: "C sits between B and D"
If D(2), B(3): C between 2 and 3 → C at 2.5? No
If D(4), B(3): C between 3 and 4 → C at 3.5? No
Wait: positions are 1,2,3,4,5
A(1), B(3), E(5)
Remaining: 2, 4 for C and D
If C(2), D(4): C is NOT between B(3) and D(4)
If C(4), D(2): C(4) is between B(3) and D? No, D(2) is left of B
Actually "between" means in the middle position
B(3) and D(4): no position between them
B(3) and D(2): C(?) between 2 and 3 → no integer position
The answer is ambiguous — but the intended answer is likely C based on standard puzzle logic

Q24. Data sufficiency: x + y = 12, x − y = 4.

Adding: 2x = 16 → x = 8
Both together sufficient.

Q25. Priya 18th from left, 50 students. Position from right?

50 − 18 + 1 = 33rd from right

Q26. Input: 12 5 8 3 9. Step 1: 3 12 5 8 9. Step 2: 3 5 12 8 9. Step 3?

Smallest moves left each step
Step 3: 3 5 8 12 9

Q27. Seven people A-G. A 3rd from left. B 3rd from right. C between A and B.

A(3), B(5) (3rd from right of 7 = position 5)
C exactly between 3 and 5 = position 4
Between C(4) and B(5) = 0 people

Q28. Eight friends circle. P opposite Q. R right of P. S left of Q. T between R and S. Opposite T?

Need full circle layout — answer cannot be determined with given info

Verbal — Step-by-Step​

Q29. Synonym of "rapid"?

Rapid = swift, fast
Answer: B) swift

Q30. Antonym of "ancient"?

Ancient = old, historic
Antonym = modern
Answer: B) modern

Q31. "Neither of the candidates ___ suitable."

Neither = singular
Answer: is

Q32. Error: "The number of employees are increasing."

"The number of" = singular → "is increasing"
Answer: C

Q33. "One of my colleagues have won the award."

Subject = "One" (singular) → "has"
Corrected: "One of my colleagues has won the award."

Q34. "The manager said that he will attend the meeting tomorrow."

No error — acceptable in reported speech

Q35. Para jumble: parcel arrived, ordered laptop, packed securely, placed order.

Order: placed order → ordered → packed securely → arrived
Answer: b, d, a, c — wait, let me re-read
(a) parcel arrived, (b) ordered laptop, (c) packed securely, (d) placed order at midnight
Logical: ordered (b) → placed order at midnight (d) → parcel arrived (a) → packed securely (c)
Wait, (c) should be before (a). Let me re-think:
He placed order at midnight (d) → ordered laptop online (b) → it was packed securely (c) → parcel arrived next morning (a)
Answer: d, b, c, a

Q36. Idiom: "To let bygones be bygones"

= to forget past grievances, forgive
Answer: A) to forgive past mistakes

Q37. "He is capable ___ handling difficult clients."

Capable of (preposition)
Answer: of

Q38. Spelling: privilege/privelege/priviledge

Correct: privilege
Answer: A) privilege

Q39. Passage: Cloud computing risk?

"long-running workloads can sometimes cost more on the cloud"
Answer: B) higher cost for long-running workloads

Q40. "Scarcely had the train left ___ the platform collapsed."

Scarcely...when (fixed phrase)
Answer: when
