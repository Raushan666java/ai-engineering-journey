Quantitative Aptitude — 100 Question Drill Bank (Any Company)

Rule: 60-90 seconds per question. Write your answer, then check against the key at the bottom. Covers all 16 topics from quant-all-topics.md. Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard. Score guide at the bottom. Use with aptitude-drill.py for timed random drills.

Section 1 — Percentages (7 questions)​

  1. (E) A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 25%. What is the net percentage change?
  2. (E) If 35% of a number is 105, what is 60% of that number?
  3. (M) The population of a town increases by 10% per year. If the current population is 1,46,410, what was it 2 years ago?
  4. (M) A's salary is 20% less than B's. By what percentage is B's salary more than A's?
  5. (M) In an exam, 40% failed in English, 30% failed in Maths, and 15% failed in both. What percentage passed in both?
  6. (M) A candidate scored 30% and failed by 15 marks. Another scored 45% and got 45 marks more than the passing mark. What is the maximum marks?
  7. (H) The price of sugar increased by 25%. By what percentage should consumption be reduced so that expenditure stays the same?

Section 2 — Profit, Loss & Discount (7 questions)​

  1. (E) A shopkeeper bought an article for ₹640 and sold it for ₹800. What is his profit percentage?
  2. (E) An article is sold at a loss of 20%. If the selling price is ₹1,440, what is the cost price?
  3. (M) A shopkeeper gives a 10% discount on the marked price and still makes a 17% profit. If the cost price is ₹500, what is the marked price?
  4. (M) A trader mixes rice costing ₹28/kg and ₹40/kg in the ratio 3:2 and sells the mixture at ₹36/kg. What is his profit percentage?
  5. (M) A dealer marks goods 40% above cost and allows a discount of 15%. What is his profit percentage?
  6. (M) Two successive discounts of 20% and 10% are equivalent to a single discount of what percentage?
  7. (H) A man sells a watch at a profit of 15%. If he had sold it for ₹120 less, he would have made a loss of 5%. What is the cost price of the watch?

Section 3 — Simple & Compound Interest (7 questions)​

  1. (E) At what rate of simple interest will ₹8,000 amount to ₹9,920 in 3 years?
  2. (E) What is the compound interest on ₹12,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually?
  3. (M) The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 8% per annum is ₹128. What is the sum?
  4. (M) A sum doubles itself in 10 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 3 times itself?
  5. (M) What sum will amount to ₹13,310 at 10% per annum compounded annually in 3 years?
  6. (M) Find the compound interest on ₹20,000 at 5% per annum for 2 years, compounded half-yearly.
  7. (H) The SI on a sum is 4/9 of the sum. The number of years equals the rate percent. Find the rate.

Section 4 — Time, Speed & Distance (7 questions)​

  1. (E) A train 200 m long crosses a pole in 10 seconds. What is its speed in km/hr?
  2. (E) A man travels 40 km at 10 km/hr and another 40 km at 40 km/hr. What is his average speed?
  3. (M) A train 150 m long crosses a platform 350 m long in 25 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
  4. (M) Two trains 120 m and 180 m long run towards each other at 36 km/hr and 54 km/hr. How long will they take to cross each other completely?
  5. (M) A car covers a distance at 40 km/hr and returns at 60 km/hr. What is the average speed for the whole journey?
  6. (M) A boy walks 8 km North, then 6 km East, then 8 km South. How far is he from the starting point?
  7. (H) A train passes a man walking at 4 km/hr in the same direction in 18 seconds. The train is 110 m long. What is the speed of the train?

Section 5 — Time & Work (7 questions)​

  1. (E) A can do a work in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish?
  2. (E) A and B together can do a job in 15 days. A alone takes 25 days. How long will B alone take?
  3. (M) 25 men can build a wall in 16 days. How many men are needed to build it in 10 days?
  4. (M) A can do a work in 24 days and B in 36 days. They work together for 6 days, then B leaves. In how many more days will A finish the remaining work?
  5. (M) A is twice as good as B and together they finish a job in 12 days. In how many days can A alone do it?
  6. (M) 12 men and 16 women can do a work in 5 days. A man does twice the work of a woman. In how many days will 4 men and 4 women do the work?
  7. (H) A, B and C can do a work in 20, 30 and 60 days respectively. A works alone for 5 days, then B and C join. In how many total days is the work finished?

Section 6 — Pipes & Cisterns (5 questions)​

  1. (E) A pipe fills a tank in 15 hours and another empties it in 20 hours. If both are opened, how long will the tank take to fill?
  2. (E) Two pipes fill a tank in 24 minutes and 36 minutes respectively. Both are opened together. How long will they take to fill the tank?
  3. (M) A pipe fills a tank in 12 hours. Due to a leak, it takes 20 hours. In how many hours will the leak alone empty the full tank?
  4. (M) A pipe can fill a tank in 8 hours. After 3 hours, a leak develops and the tank now fills in 6 more hours. In how many hours would the leak alone empty the full tank?
  5. (H) Two pipes A and B fill a tank in 20 and 30 minutes. Both are opened; A is closed after 8 minutes. How much longer will B take to fill the tank alone?

Section 7 — Ratio & Proportion (7 questions)​

  1. (E) If a:b = 3:4 and b:c = 8:9, find a:c.
  2. (E) Divide ₹3,600 among A, B, C so that A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5. What is C's share?
  3. (M) The ratio of ages of two people is 5:9. After 8 years, the ratio will be 7:11. What are their present ages?
  4. (M) Two numbers are in the ratio 4:7. If 8 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 2:5. Find the smaller number.
  5. (M) A sum of money is divided in the ratio 3:5:7. If the middle share is ₹350, what is the total?
  6. (M) The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 7:5. If 40 boys leave, the ratio becomes 3:5. How many girls are there?
  7. (H) A mixture of milk and water is in the ratio 4:1. If 5 litres of water is added, the ratio becomes 4:3. Find the quantity of milk.

Section 8 — Partnership (5 questions)​

  1. (E) A and B invest ₹40,000 and ₹60,000 for equal time. Profit is ₹30,000. What is A's share?
  2. (M) A invests ₹50,000 for 12 months, B invests ₹75,000 for 8 months. Profit is ₹54,000. What is B's share?
  3. (M) A invests ₹20,000 for 8 months, B invests ₹32,000 for 5 months, C invests ₹40,000 for 3 months. Profit is ₹18,600. What is C's share?
  4. (M) A starts a business with ₹30,000. After 4 months, B joins with ₹45,000. After how many more months will their profit shares be equal at year end?
  5. (H) A invests ₹60,000. B joins after 6 months with ₹90,000. At year end, profits are equal. What is A's monthly profit share ratio to B?

Section 9 — Averages (5 questions)​

  1. (E) The average of 12 numbers is 30. If each number is increased by 5, what is the new average?
  2. (E) The average age of 6 students is 14. A teacher aged 38 joins them. What is the new average?
  3. (M) The average of 20 numbers is 55. If one number 100 is removed, what is the new average?
  4. (M) The average of 5 numbers is 42. If the average of the first 2 is 35 and the last 2 is 45, what is the middle number?
  5. (H) The average of 15 numbers is 40. If each of 5 numbers is increased by 8 and each of the remaining 10 is decreased by 5, what is the new average?

Section 10 — Mixtures & Alligation (5 questions)​

  1. (E) In what ratio should water be mixed with milk costing ₹45 per litre so that the mixture sells at ₹36 per litre with no profit or loss?
  2. (M) How many kg of sugar at ₹32/kg must be mixed with 30 kg at ₹44/kg so that the mixture costs ₹38/kg?
  3. (M) Milk costing ₹50 per litre is mixed with water in the ratio 3:2. What is the cost of the mixture per litre?
  4. (M) A vessel contains 60 litres of milk. 12 litres is replaced with water. What is the ratio of milk to water in the new mixture?
  5. (H) Two alloys contain copper and zinc in the ratios 5:3 and 5:7. In what ratio should they be mixed to get an alloy with copper and zinc in the ratio 3:2?

Section 11 — Ages (6 questions)​

  1. (E) The ratio of father's age to son's age is 7:2. After 6 years, it will be 5:2. What is the son's present age?
  2. (E) The sum of ages of a father and son is 60. The father is 3 times as old as the son. What is the son's age?
  3. (M) A father is 4 times as old as his son. 8 years ago, he was 8 times as old. What is the father's present age?
  4. (M) The average age of a family of 5 is 30. If the youngest member aged 10 leaves, what is the new average?
  5. (M) Two years ago, the ratio of ages was 3:5. Two years hence, it will be 5:7. What is the elder's present age?
  6. (H) A is twice as old as B. 12 years ago, A was 4 times as old as B. What is B's present age?

Section 12 — Permutations & Combinations (7 questions)​

  1. (E) In how many ways can the letters of the word "AROUND" be arranged?
  2. (E) In how many ways can a committee of 2 be chosen from 10 people?
  3. (M) How many 4-digit numbers can be formed from digits 1-7 without repetition?
  4. (M) How many ways can 5 boys and 3 girls be arranged in a row so that all girls sit together?
  5. (M) How many ways can 6 people be seated around a circular table?
  6. (M) How many ways can 3 books be chosen from 8?
  7. (H) How many ways can the word "SUCCESS" be arranged?

Section 13 — Probability (7 questions)​

  1. (E) A bag has 4 red, 5 blue, 3 green balls. One ball is drawn. What is the probability it is green?
  2. (E) A card is drawn from a pack of 52. What is the probability it is a heart?
  3. (M) Two dice are thrown. What is the probability the sum is 7?
  4. (M) A card is drawn. What is the probability it is either an ace or a black card?
  5. (M) A bag has 6 red and 4 white balls. Two are drawn. What is the probability both are red?
  6. (M) A coin is tossed 3 times. What is the probability of at least one head?
  7. (H) A bag has 5 red, 6 black balls. Two are drawn. What is the probability both are of the same color?

Section 14 — Number Series (7 questions)​

  1. (E) Find the next term: 5, 10, 20, 40, 80, ?
  2. (E) Find the next term: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
  3. (M) Find the next term: 3, 8, 18, 38, 78, ?
  4. (M) Find the next term: 1, 4, 10, 22, 46, ?
  5. (M) Find the missing term: 7, 14, 30, 62, ?, 254
  6. (M) Find the next term: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
  7. (H) Find the next term: 5, 12, 26, 54, 110, ?

Section 15 — Number System (6 questions)​

  1. (E) What is the remainder when 2^100 is divided by 5?
  2. (E) The product of two numbers is 720 and their HCF is 12. What is their LCM?
  3. (M) Find the largest number that divides 285 and 1,245 leaving remainders 5 in each case.
  4. (M) What is the last digit of 7^250?
  5. (M) The HCF of two numbers is 16 and their LCM is 336. If one number is 48, what is the other?
  6. (H) Find the number which, on being divided by 3, 5 and 7 successively, leaves remainders 2, 3 and 4 respectively.

Section 16 — Geometry, Mensuration, Clocks & Calendar (5 questions)​
