# Blockchain Technology — Complete University Textbook

## Course Description

This course provides a rigorous, university-level treatment of blockchain technology, from its mathematical and cryptographic foundations to the architectures of major networks and the emerging decentralized application ecosystem. Students will develop a deep understanding of how blockchain systems achieve security, consensus, and programmability through the interplay of cryptography, game theory, and distributed systems design.

The curriculum traces the evolution of blockchain from Bitcoin's breakthrough solution to the Byzantine Generals Problem, through Ethereum's introduction of smart contracts, to modern developments in decentralized finance, enterprise blockchains, and scalability solutions. Each chapter combines formal technical exposition with real-world protocol analysis, equipping students to critically evaluate blockchain architectures and contribute to their development.

## Prerequisites

- Computer networking fundamentals (peer-to-peer systems, TCP/IP)
- Data structures and algorithms (hash tables, trees, Merkle data structures)
- Cryptography basics (hash functions, public-key encryption)
- Programming experience, preferably with exposure to Solidity or a similar contract language

## Study Path

Chapters 1–3 establish the foundational pillars: the blockchain data structure, the cryptographic primitives that secure it, and the consensus mechanisms that unite distributed nodes. Chapters 4–5 examine Bitcoin and Ethereum as case studies in protocol design. Chapters 6–7 move into application development with smart contracts and dApps. Chapters 8–10 cover the DeFi ecosystem, enterprise use cases, and the security and scalability challenges facing the industry.
