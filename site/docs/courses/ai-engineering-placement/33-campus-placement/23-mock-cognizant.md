# Cognizant GenC Mock Test — Full Pattern Simulation (40 Questions)

> Simulates the Cognizant GenC aptitude section: 16 quant, 12 logical, 12 verbal.
> Time limits: Quant 30 min, Logical 20 min, Verbal 15 min. Total: 65 minutes.
> Cognizant is competitive on speed — target 75%+ and 60 seconds/question maximum.
> Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

## Cognizant GenC Pattern — What You Need to Know

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Aptitude | 16 questions in 30 minutes |
| Logical | 12 questions in 20 minutes |
| Verbal | 12 questions in 15 minutes |
| Negative marking | No penalty |
| Passing | Competitive sectional cut-offs; every question attempted |
| Flavor | Fast arithmetic, data interpretation tables, number series heavy |

## Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (16 questions) — 30 minutes

1. (E) What is 32% of 250?
2. (E) A shopkeeper buys an article for ₹400 and sells it at a 25% profit. What is the selling price?
3. (M) The average of 12 numbers is 44. If one number 72 is removed, what is the new average?
4. (M) A train 180 m long crosses a pole in 9 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
5. (M) A and B can do a job in 12 days and 16 days. Together, in how many days?
6. (M) What is the SI on ₹15,000 at 8% per annum for 3 years?
7. (M) A sum of money becomes 4 times itself in 15 years at SI. What is the rate?
8. (M) Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If 10 is added to each, the ratio becomes 5:7. Find the numbers.
9. (M) Find the next term: 5, 13, 29, 61, 125, ?
10. (M) A bag has 8 red and 6 green balls. One ball is drawn. What is the probability it is green?
11. (M) In how many ways can 5 people be seated in a row?
12. (M) The LCM of two numbers is 420 and their HCF is 14. If one number is 84, what is the other?
13. (M) A boat covers 20 km downstream in 2 hours and 20 km upstream in 4 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
14. (H) A shopkeeper marks an item 40% above cost and gives a 20% discount. What is the profit percentage?
15. (H) A pipe fills a tank in 12 hours and a leak empties it in 18 hours. Both open. How long to fill?
16. (H) The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 12% is ₹72. What is the sum?

## Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (12 questions) — 20 minutes

17. (E) Find the next term: 2, 6, 18, 54, ?
18. (E) In a code, "ROSE" is written as "SPTF". How is "LILY" written?
19. (M) Statements: All students are readers. Some readers are writers.
    I. Some students are writers. II. All students are readers.
20. (M) A man walks 4 km North, 3 km West, 4 km South. What is his net position?
21. (M) A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. C is the wife of D. How is A related to D?
22. (M) In a row of 28 students, Riya is 9th from the left. What is her position from the right?
23. (M) Data sufficiency: What is the value of x?
    (I) 3x + 2y = 20
    (II) 2x − y = 4
24. (M) Input: 7 2 9 4 6. Step 1: 2 7 9 4 6. Step 2: 2 4 7 9 6. What is Step 3?
25. (M) Odd one out: 12, 18, 24, 30, 36
26. (H) Six friends A-F sit in a circle. A sits opposite B. C sits to the right of A. D sits to the left of B. Who is opposite C?
27. (H) Statement: "The government announced a new health insurance scheme for all citizens."
    I. The scheme will help citizens with medical costs.
    II. All citizens currently lack health insurance.
28. (H) In a code, if "TREE" is coded as "USFF", how is "MATH" coded?

## Section 3 — Verbal Ability (12 questions) — 15 minutes

29. (E) Synonym of "excellent": (A) poor (B) outstanding (C) average (D) weak
30. (E) Antonym of "expand": (A) grow (B) contract (C) extend (D) enlarge
31. (M) Fill in the blank: "The committee ___ decided to meet again." (has / have)
32. (M) Spot the error: "The number of employees (A) in the company (B) are increasing (C) each year (D)."
33. (M) Correct: "Each of the students have received their certificates."
34. (M) Para jumble: (a) The application was approved within a week. (b) She applied for the job online. (c) She then scheduled the interview. (d) Her resume was shortlisted. Order?
35. (M) Choose the correct spelling: (A) separate (B) seperate (C) separet (D) seperet
36. (M) Idiom: "To be under the weather" means: (A) to be outdoors (B) to feel unwell (C) to be happy (D) to be confused
37. (M) Fill in the blank: "He is good ___ mathematics." (at / in / with)
38. (H) Passage: "Hybrid work arrangements, where employees split time between office and home, have become standard in many companies. Studies show that while productivity often rises, team cohesion can suffer if communication is not deliberate. Companies that schedule regular in-person days report better collaboration."
    What is one risk of hybrid work mentioned?
39. (H) Fill in the blank: "Neither of the answers ___ correct." (is / are)
40. (H) The word closest in meaning to "reluctant" is: (A) eager (B) unwilling (C) careless (D) joyful

## Answer Key

### Section 1 — Quantitative (1-16)

1. **80** (250 × 0.32)
2. **₹500** (400 × 1.25)
3. **41.45** (total = 528; minus 72 = 456; 456/11)
4. **72 km/hr** (180 m in 9 s = 20 m/s)
5. **6.86 days** (12 × 16/28)
6. **₹3,600** (15000 × 0.08 × 3)
7. **20%** (interest = 3P in 15 yr → 3P = P × R × 15/100 → R = 20%)
8. **15 and 25** ((3k+10)/(5k+10) = 5/7 → 21k + 70 = 25k + 50 → k = 5)
9. **253** (×2 + 3: 125 × 2 + 3)
10. **3/7** (6/14)
11. **120** (5!)
12. **70** (420 × 14/84)
13. **2.5 km/hr** (downstream 10, upstream 5; stream = (10−5)/2)
14. **12%** (100 → 140 → 112)
15. **36 hours** (1/12 − 1/18 = 1/36)
16. **₹5,000** (72 = P × 0.0144 → P = 5,000)

### Section 2 — Logical (17-28)

17. **162** (×3)
18. **MJMZ** (+1 each letter)
19. **Only II** (I not guaranteed)
20. **3 km West** (4N − 4S cancel)
21. **Son** (C is A's mother; D is C's husband → A is D's son)
22. **20th from the right** (28 − 9 + 1)
23. **Both together** (x = 4, y = 4)
24. **2 4 6 7 9** (smallest moves left; then 6; then 7)
25. **None is odd** — all are multiples of 6 (the odd one out question is a trap: all are even multiples of 6; standard answer: none / all are alike)
26. **D** (6-seat circle: A=1, B=4, C=2, D=3, E=5, F=6; opposite C (2) = 5... wait: A opposite B (1 and 4); C right of A = 2; D left of B = 3; remaining E=5, F=6; opposite C (2) = E(5)) — **E**
27. **Only I** (II overstates — scheme implies some citizens lack insurance, but "all" is extreme)
28. **NBUI** (+1 each letter: M→N, A→B, T→U, H→I)

### Section 3 — Verbal (29-40)

29. **B) outstanding**
30. **B) contract**
31. **has** (committee = singular)
32. **C** ("the number of" → singular → "is increasing")
33. **"Each of the students has received his/her certificate"** (each = singular)
34. **b, d, a, c** (applied → shortlisted → approved → scheduled interview)
35. **A) separate**
36. **B) to feel unwell**
37. **at** (good at)
38. **Team cohesion can suffer if communication is not deliberate**
39. **is** (neither = singular)
40. **B) unwilling**
