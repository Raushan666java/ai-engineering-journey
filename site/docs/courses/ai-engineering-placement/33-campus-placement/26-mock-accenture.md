# Accenture Mock Test — Full Pattern Simulation (45 Questions)

> Simulates the Accenture cognitive aptitude section: 15 quant, 15 logical, 15 verbal.
> Time limits: Quant 25 min, Logical 25 min, Verbal 25 min. Total: 75 minutes.
> Accenture is verbal-heavy — grammar, RC and para jumbles carry the most marks.
> Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

## Accenture Pattern — What You Need to Know

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cognitive assessment | Quant + logical + verbal in one round |
| Time | Roughly 45-50 questions in 75 minutes |
| Negative marking | No penalty |
| Passing | 70%+ recommended |
| Flavor | Verbal dominates: grammar, RC, para jumbles, vocabulary |

## Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (15 questions) — 25 minutes

1. (E) What is 25% of 480?
2. (E) A shopkeeper sells an article for ₹690 at a 15% profit. What was the cost price?
3. (M) The average of 8 numbers is 40. If one number 64 is removed, what is the new average?
4. (M) A train 210 m long crosses a platform 390 m long in 20 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
5. (M) A and B can do a job in 10 days and 15 days. Together, in how many days?
6. (M) What is the CI on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years?
7. (M) A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at SI. What is the rate?
8. (M) Find the next term: 4, 12, 36, 108, ?
9. (M) A bag has 6 red and 4 blue balls. Two drawn. Probability both red?
10. (M) In how many ways can 3 books be arranged on a shelf?
11. (M) The LCM of two numbers is 180 and their HCF is 12. If one number is 36, what is the other?
12. (M) A boat travels 30 km downstream in 2 hours and 30 km upstream in 6 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
13. (M) Two numbers are in the ratio 4:5. If 4 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 3:4. Find the smaller number.
14. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 35% above cost and gives a 15% discount. What is the profit percentage?
15. (H) A pipe fills a tank in 15 hours and a leak empties it in 25 hours. Both open. How long to fill?

## Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (15 questions) — 25 minutes

16. (E) Find the next term: 5, 15, 45, 135, ?
17. (E) In a code, "TRAIN" is written as "USBJO". How is "PLANE" written?
18. (M) Statements: All cats are animals. No animal is a stone.
    I. No cat is a stone. II. All animals are cats.
19. (M) A man walks 4 km North, then 3 km East. How far is he from the start?
20. (M) A is the father of B. B is the sister of C. C is the son of D. How is D related to B?
21. (M) In a row of 36 students, Priya is 11th from the left. What is her position from the right?
22. (M) Data sufficiency: What is the value of x?
    (I) x² = 25
    (II) x > 0
23. (M) Input: 5 2 8 1 9. Step 1: 1 5 2 8 9. Step 2: 1 2 5 8 9. What is Step 3?
24. (M) Odd one out: 16, 25, 36, 49, 54
25. (M) Five people A-E sit in a row. A is at the left end. B is second from the right. C is exactly in the middle. D is between A and C. E is between C and B. Who is at the right end?
26. (H) Six friends A-F sit in a circle. A is opposite B. C is to the left of A. D is to the right of B. E is opposite F. Who is opposite C?
27. (H) Statement: "The government banned single-use plastics in the city."
    I. The ban will reduce plastic waste.
    II. All citizens used single-use plastics.
28. (H) In a code, "WATER" is coded as "XBUFS", how is "RIVER" coded?
29. (H) Seven people A-G sit in a row. A is 4th from the left. B is 2nd from the right. C is 2nd from the left. Who sits in the middle?
30. (H) Pointing to a photo, a man says, "She is the daughter of my sister." How is she related to the man?

## Section 3 — Verbal Ability (15 questions) — 25 minutes

31. (E) Synonym of "happy": (A) sad (B) joyful (C) angry (D) tired
32. (E) Antonym of "ancient": (A) old (B) modern (C) antique (D) historic
33. (M) Fill in the blank: "Neither of the options ___ suitable." (is / are)
34. (M) Spot the error: "The number of students (A) in the class (B) are decreasing (C) every year (D)."
35. (M) Correct: "Each of the girls have completed the task."
36. (M) Correct: "One of my colleagues are a data scientist."
37. (M) Para jumble: (a) The results were announced on Friday. (b) Students had waited for months. (c) The exams concluded in April. (d) A merit list was published online. Order?
38. (M) Choose the correct spelling: (A) definitely (B) definately (C) definitly (D) definetly
39. (M) Idiom: "To put your foot in your mouth" means: (A) to eat something (B) to say something embarrassing (C) to make a mistake walking (D) to be hungry
40. (M) Fill in the blank: "She is responsible ___ the project." (for / of / at)
41. (H) Passage: "Artificial intelligence is transforming customer service. Chatbots now handle routine queries around the clock, freeing human agents for complex issues. Companies report lower response times, but warn that poorly designed bots frustrate users and damage brand perception. Successful implementations route difficult cases to humans quickly."
    What is a risk of poorly designed chatbots?
42. (H) Fill in the blank: "Hardly had the play started ___ the lights failed." (than / when / that)
43. (H) The word closest in meaning to "hinder" is: (A) help (B) obstruct (C) accelerate (D) ignore
44. (H) Choose the correct spelling: (A) embarrassment (B) embarassment (C) embarrasment (D) embarassement
45. (H) The word closest in meaning to "alleviate" is: (A) worsen (B) reduce (C) increase (D) ignore

9. **1/3** (C(6,2)/C(10,2) = 15/45)

14. **14.75%** (100 → 135 → 114.75)
15. **37.5 hours** (1/15 − 1/25 = 2/75 → 37.5)

### Section 2 — Logical (16-30)

17. **QMBOF** (+1 each letter)

21. **26th from the right** (36 − 11 + 1)
22. **Both together** (x = 5)
23. **1 2 5 8 9** (already sorted — Step 3 = Step 2)
24. **54** (only non-square)

27. **Only I** (II overgeneralizes)
28. **SJWF S** (+1 each letter: R→S, I→J, V→W, E→F, R→S)

30. **Niece** (my sister's daughter = my niece)
