# 📅 Daily Practice Schedule

## Phase-Based Schedule

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-10)
**Daily Routine (4-5 hours):**

**Morning (2-2.5 hours): DSA Practice**
- 2 Easy problems (Week 1-4)
- 1 Easy + 1 Medium (Week 5-8)
- 1 Medium + 1 Easy (Week 9-10)
- Review solutions and optimize

**Afternoon (1.5-2 hours): Core Subject**
- **Mon/Wed/Fri:** Java programming
- **Tue/Thu:** DBMS concepts
- **Sat:** OS/Networks theory
- **Sun:** Review and practice

**Evening (30-60 minutes): Soft Skills**
- Resume building
- LinkedIn optimization
- Behavioral question practice

### Phase 2: Advanced (Weeks 11-20)
**Daily Routine (5-6 hours):**

**Morning (2.5-3 hours): Advanced DSA**
- 1 Medium + 1 Hard problem
- Focus on weak areas
- Time-bound practice (45 min/problem)

**Afternoon (2-2.5 hours): System Design**
- **Mon/Wed/Fri:** HLD practice
- **Tue/Thu:** LLD practice
- **Sat:** Mock design interview
- **Sun:** Review and improvement

**Evening (30-60 minutes): Specialization**
- Choose track: AI/ML, DevOps, or Web Dev
- Follow track-specific schedule

### Phase 3: Interview Prep (Weeks 21-26)
**Daily Routine (6-7 hours):**

**Morning (3-3.5 hours): Intensive DSA**
- 2-3 problems (mix of Medium/Hard)
- Company-specific problem patterns
- Mock coding interviews

**Afternoon (2-2.5 hours): System Design**
- End-to-end system design practice
- Mock system design interviews
- Industry case studies

**Evening (1-1.5 hours): Interview Skills**
- Behavioral interview practice
- Company research
- Application submissions

## Weekly Structure

### Monday: Fresh Start
- **Focus:** New topic introduction
- **DSA:** Array/String problems
- **Energy:** High - tackle challenging concepts

### Tuesday: Build Momentum
- **Focus:** Concept reinforcement
- **DSA:** LinkedList/Stack problems
- **Energy:** Good - consistent practice

### Wednesday: Mid-week Push
- **Focus:** Advanced concepts
- **DSA:** Tree/Graph problems
- **Energy:** Maintain - avoid burnout

### Thursday: Skill Application
- **Focus:** Problem-solving practice
- **DSA:** Dynamic Programming
- **Energy:** Steady - focus on quality

### Friday: Week Consolidation
- **Focus:** Review and practice
- **DSA:** Mixed problem solving
- **Energy:** Good - prepare for weekend

### Saturday: Project Day
- **Focus:** Hands-on projects
- **DSA:** Contest participation
- **Energy:** Creative - build something

### Sunday: Review & Plan
- **Focus:** Week review, next week planning
- **DSA:** Weak area practice
- **Energy:** Reflective - assess progress

## Time Management Tips

### Pomodoro Technique
- **25 minutes:** Focused work
- **5 minutes:** Short break
- **15 minutes:** Long break (after 4 pomodoros)

### Daily Time Blocks
```
06:00-08:30  DSA Practice (2.5 hours)
08:30-09:00  Breakfast & Break
09:00-11:00  Core Subject Study (2 hours)
11:00-11:15  Break
11:15-12:15  Project Work (1 hour)
12:15-13:15  Lunch & Rest
13:15-14:45  System Design (1.5 hours)
14:45-15:00  Break
15:00-16:00  Specialization Track (1 hour)
16:00-16:30  Review & Planning
```

### Weekly Goals
- **DSA Problems:** 15-20 per week
- **System Designs:** 2-3 per week
- **Project Progress:** 1 milestone per week
- **Mock Interviews:** 1-2 per week (later phases)

## Flexibility Guidelines

### High Energy Days
- Tackle difficult concepts
- Solve challenging problems
- Work on complex projects

### Low Energy Days
- Review previous topics
- Solve easier problems
- Watch educational videos

### Adjustment Rules
- **Behind Schedule:** Focus on high-impact topics
- **Ahead of Schedule:** Dive deeper into advanced topics
- **Feeling Overwhelmed:** Take a day off, then resume

## Progress Tracking
- **Daily:** Log problems solved, topics covered
- **Weekly:** Assess goal completion, adjust next week
- **Monthly:** Major milestone review, roadmap adjustment
