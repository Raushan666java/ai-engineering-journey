# 🎯 90-Day Intensive Placement Preparation Plan - Complete Detailed Guide

## 📊 Executive Summary

This comprehensive 90-day placement preparation plan is designed to transform you from a beginner to an interview-ready software engineer. The plan combines **Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)**, **Computer Science fundamentals**, **System Design**, **Project Development**, and **Interview Preparation** in a structured, progressive manner.

**Total Duration**: 90 days (12 weeks)  
**Daily Study Time**: 9 hours  
**Weekly Off**: Sunday (light revision only)  
**Target**: 300+ problems solved, 3+ projects completed, Interview-ready

---

## 🗓️ Program Overview

### **Phase 1: Foundation & Core (Days 1-30)**
- **Focus**: DSA fundamentals, Programming mastery, CS basics
- **Goal**: Build strong foundation in core concepts
- **Deliverables**: 100+ problems solved, 1 major project, CS fundamentals mastery

### **Phase 2: Advanced & Specialization (Days 31-60)**
- **Focus**: Advanced DSA, System Design, Project development
- **Goal**: Master complex problems and design scalable systems
- **Deliverables**: 200+ total problems, 2-3 projects, Mock interview readiness

### **Phase 3: Interview Mastery (Days 61-90)**
- **Focus**: Company-specific preparation, Mock interviews, Portfolio
- **Goal**: Excel in real interviews and secure job offers
- **Deliverables**: 300+ total problems, Complete portfolio, Job offers
