Explanation: Comparative degree is used when comparing two people/things. "Taller" is the comparative form of "tall."

Key Takeaway: One-syllable adjectives: add -er (tall -> taller). Two+ syllables: more + adj (beautiful -> more beautiful). Irregular: good/better, bad/worse.

Q71-Q73: Error Spotting

Q71. Neither the teacher nor the students (A) / was present (B) / in the class (C) / yesterday. (D) / No error (E)

A) A B) B C) C D) D E) No error

Show Answer

Answer: B) B (was present)

Explanation: With "neither...nor," the verb agrees with the nearest subject. "Students" is plural, so it should be "were present" not "was present."

Key Takeaway: Neither X nor Y + verb: verb agrees with Y (the nearest subject).

Q72. One of the boy (A) / has scored (B) / the highest marks (C) / in the exam. (D) / No error (E)

A) A B) B C) C D) D E) No error

Show Answer

Answer: A) A (One of the boy)

Explanation: "One of the" is followed by a plural noun. It should be "One of the boys" (plural). The verb is singular because the subject is "One" (singular): "One... has scored."

Key Takeaway: "One of the + plural noun + singular verb" is a fixed pattern. E.g., "One of the students is..."

Q73. She has been working (A) / in this office (B) / since three years (C) / now. (D) / No error (E)

A) A B) B C) C D) D E) No error

Show Answer

Answer: C) C (since three years)

Explanation: "Since" is used with a point in time (since Monday, since 2020). "For" is used with a duration (for three years, for two months). Correct: "for three years."

Key Takeaway: Since = point in time. For = duration. This is one of the most common error spotting questions.

Q74-Q76: Cloze Test

Passage: Digital India is a (74) _____ launched by the Government of India. It aims to (75) _____ citizens with digital services and infrastructure. The (76) _____ includes connecting rural areas with high-speed internet.

Q74. A) campaign B) scheme C) initiative D) movement

Show Answer

Answer: C) initiative

Explanation: "Initiative" is the most appropriate word for a government program launched to achieve specific goals. "Campaign" is time-bound, "scheme" is common in Indian context but less formal, "movement" implies grassroots participation.

Key Takeaway: Collocations matter: "government initiative" is a standard phrase.

Q75. A) provide B) provides C) provided D) providing

Show Answer

Answer: A) provide

Explanation: "Aims to" + base form of verb (infinitive without "to"). So "aims to provide" is correct.

Key Takeaway: After "aims to," "plans to," "hopes to," "wants to" — use the base form (infinitive without "to").

Q76. A) objection B) objective C) object D) objectionable

Show Answer

Answer: B) objective

Explanation: "Objective" means a goal or aim. "Objection" is a reason for disagreeing. "Object" is a thing. "Objectionable" means offensive. "The objective includes..." fits the context.

Key Takeaway: Choose words based on context. Objective = goal. Objection = protest. Object = thing/item.

Q77-Q78: Para Jumbles

Q77. Arrange: 1. This creates a cycle of poverty. 2. Poor families cannot afford education for children. 3. Without education, children remain poor. 4. Poverty is a complex social issue.

A) 4,2,3,1 B) 4,3,2,1 C) 2,3,1,4 D) 2,1,3,4

Show Answer

Answer: A) 4,2,3,1

Explanation: Start with topic sentence (4). Then cause (2) lack of education due to poverty. Effect (3) children remain poor. Consequence (1) cycle continues.

Key Takeaway: Topic sentence introduces the subject. Follow with causes, effects, and conclusions.

Q78. Arrange: 1. The internet was invented. 2. It connected people across the world. 3. Communication changed forever. 4. Information became accessible instantly.

A) 1,2,3,4 B) 1,2,4,3 C) 1,4,2,3 D) 2,1,4,3

Show Answer

Answer: B) 1,2,4,3

Explanation: Historical sequence: Invention (1) -> Connection (2) -> Information access (4) -> Impact (3). Most logical flow.

Key Takeaway: Chronological or cause-effect ordering works best for para jumbles.

Q79. Select the synonym of "ABUNDANT": A) Scarce B) Plentiful C) Limited D) Rare

Show Answer

Answer: B) Plentiful

Explanation: Abundant means existing in large quantities. Plentiful is the synonym. Scarce, Limited, Rare are antonyms.

Key Takeaway: Build vocabulary through roots: "abund-" = overflowing. Antonym: scarce.

Q80. Choose the correctly spelled word: A) Accommadation B) Acomodation C) Accommodation D) Acommadation

Show Answer

Answer: C) Accommodation

Explanation: Double c, double m: accommodation. One of the most commonly misspelled words in English.

Key Takeaway: Memory trick: Accommodation has "two c's" and "two m's" — like a room with two beds and two mirrors.

Q81-Q95: Additional English Questions

Q81. Select the antonym of "ANCIENT": A) Old B) Modern C) Historic D) Vintage

Show Answer

Answer: B) Modern

Explanation: Ancient means belonging to the very distant past. Modern is its direct antonym (belonging to the present). Old, Historic, Vintage are related to the past.

Key Takeaway: Antonyms: Ancient x Modern, Ancient x Contemporary, Ancient x Recent.

Q82. Identify the error: The committee have decided to implement the new policy.

A) The committee B) have decided C) to implement D) the new policy E) No error

Show Answer

Answer: B) have decided

Explanation: "Committee" is a collective noun, treated as singular. Should be "has decided."

Key Takeaway: Collective nouns (team, committee, audience, government) typically take singular verbs in formal English.

Q83. He is _____ of the two brothers. A) tall B) taller C) tallest D) the taller

Show Answer

Answer: D) the taller

Explanation: When comparing two specific people/things in a defined group, use "the taller" (with "the"). "He is the taller of the two brothers."

Key Takeaway: For comparing two specific items: the + comparative + of the two.

Q84. Fill: The _____ of the building collapsed during the earthquake. A) ceiling B) sealing C) cieling D) seeling

Show Answer

Answer: A) ceiling

Explanation: Ceiling = the upper interior surface of a room. Sealing = closing securely. Cieling is misspelled.

Key Takeaway: Ceiling (top of room) vs Sealing (closing). Common homophone confusion.

Q85. She said that she _____ him the previous day. A) met B) meets C) had met D) has met

Show Answer

Answer: C) had met

Explanation: Reported speech: "She said" (past) + "that she had met him the previous day" (past perfect). The reporting verb is in past tense, so the reported verb shifts one tense back.

Key Takeaway: In reported speech: Simple Past -> Past Perfect. Present Perfect -> Past Perfect. Will -> Would.

Q86. Choose the correct preposition: The dog jumped _____ the table. A) on B) onto C) in D) at

Show Answer

Answer: B) onto

Explanation: "Onto" indicates movement to a position on a surface. "On" indicates static position. "The dog jumped onto the table" emphasizes the movement.

Key Takeaway: Onto = movement + on. Into = movement + in. On = static position.

Q87. Identify the error: If I was you, I would accept the offer.

A) If I B) was you C) I would D) accept the offer E) No error

Show Answer

Answer: B) was you

Explanation: Subjunctive mood requires "were" for unreal/hypothetical situations. "If I were you" is the correct form.

Key Takeaway: For unreal conditionals: use "were" (not "was") for all persons: If I were, If he were, If they were.

Q88. He not only lost his money (A) / but also his (B) / reputation was damaged. (C) / No error (D)

A) A B) B C) C D) No error

Show Answer

Answer: B) B

Explanation: "Not only...but also" requires parallel structure. "He not only lost his money but also his reputation" — or — "He lost not only his money but also his reputation." The given sentence breaks parallelism.

Key Takeaway: In "not only X but also Y," X and Y must have the same grammatical form.

Q89. The scenery of the mountains are very beautiful. Error?

A) The scenery B) of the mountains C) are D) beautiful E) No error

Show Answer

Answer: C) are

Explanation: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun that takes a singular verb. Should be "is very beautiful."

Key Takeaway: Uncountable nouns (scenery, information, furniture, advice, knowledge, homework) always take singular verbs.

Q90. Choose the correct word: The _____ of the meeting is to discuss the budget. A) propose B) purpose C) propose D) purposive

Show Answer

Answer: B) purpose

Explanation: "Purpose" (noun) means the reason for which something is done. "Propose" (verb) means to suggest. "The purpose of the meeting" is the standard phrase.

Key Takeaway: Purpose (noun) vs Propose (verb). Purpose = aim/goal. Propose = suggest/offer.
