# Day 30 Study Plan

## Overview
This is the study plan for Day 30 of your 90-day placement preparation journey.

## Daily Schedule (9 hours total)

### Morning Session (3 hours)
- **Topic**: Phase 1 Mock Run Preparation & Review
- **Activities**:
  - Re-run Week 4 review and day 29 mock tests
  - Capture outputs and flag failing cases
  - Focused debugging of failing solutions and tests

### Afternoon Session (3 hours)
- **Topic**: Strengthening Weak Areas
- **Activities**:
  - Fix failing tests; write additional test cases for edge conditions
  - Optimize solutions where needed

### Evening Session (3 hours)
- **Topic**: System Design & Behavioral Review (short)
- **Activities**:
  - Quick system-design primer focusing on trade-offs
  - Behavioral interview practice: storytelling, STAR method

## Learning Objectives
- Ensure Phase 1 coverage across Week 4 topics and Day 29
- Identify gaps and make a remedial plan
- Be ready for mock interviews with improved confidence
