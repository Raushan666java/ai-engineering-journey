## Self-Assessment & Progress Tracking

### Weekly Self-Assessment Checklist

```
□ I studied ___ hours this week (target: 40+)
□ I attempted ___ coding problems (target: 7+)
□ I completed ___ speed drill sets (target: 5+)
□ I took ___ full-length mocks (target: 1 per week in Phase 3)
□ My mock score is ___ / target: ___ (trending up?)
□ My top 3 weak areas are: 1. ___ 2. ___ 3. ___
□ I reviewed my mistake log ___ times
□ I revised Anki cards for ___ minutes daily
□ I spent ___ minutes on current affairs
□ Next week's focus: ___
```

### Progress Dashboard

Track your preparation using the [Learning Analytics](courses/learning-how-to-learn/ch-13-learning-analytics.md) techniques from the Learning How to Learn course. Revisit your strategy weekly using the [Exam Cracking](courses/learning-how-to-learn/ch-15-exam-cracking-mastery.md) self-assessment framework.

### Recommended Learning Path

1. Start with [**Learning How to Learn**](courses/learning-how-to-learn/) — chapters 1-6 for study fundamentals
2. Take the diagnostic pre-test in the [course index](courses/learning-how-to-learn/index.md)
3. Choose your target exam from the paths above
4. Follow the structured plan with weekly self-assessment
5. Use the practice resources daily
6. Analyze mock tests using C/M/E/R taxonomy
7. Iterate and improve
