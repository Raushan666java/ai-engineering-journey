## 🔥 KEY INSIGHTS

### Pattern Selection Criteria
1. **Problem Type**: What problem are you solving?
2. **Complexity**: How complex is the solution?
3. **Flexibility**: How flexible do you need?
4. **Performance**: What are constraints?
5. **Team Knowledge**: Familiarity level?

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid
❌ Singleton abuse (hidden dependencies)
❌ God Objects (too many responsibilities)
❌ Leaky Abstraction (implementation details)
❌ Over-engineering (unnecessary complexity)
❌ Service Locator (hidden dependencies)

### Best Practices
✅ SOLID principles first
✅ Simplest solution first
✅ Refactor to patterns
✅ Know when NOT to use
✅ Combine patterns wisely
