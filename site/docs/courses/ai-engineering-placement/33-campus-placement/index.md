# 33 — Campus Placement & Aptitude

## Overview

Crack the first filter of Indian campus placements: aptitude tests, reasoning, verbal ability, company-specific test patterns, group discussions, HR interviews, full-length mocks, and previous-year questions. This module covers the TCS NQT, Infosys SP, Wipro NLTH, Capgemini, Accenture, and Cognizant selection processes that precede every AI/engineering interview round.

## Learning Path

1. Read chapters 01-03 first — the aptitude sections every company tests
2. Then chapter 04 — learn the exact test format of your target company
3. Practice with chapter 08 PYQs, then simulate with chapter 07 mocks
4. **Universal track (any company)**: follow chapter 10 (4-week master plan), memorize chapter 14 (speed rules), drill chapters 15-17 (300-question banks), then rotate chapters 18-28 (11 full mocks)
5. Before interview day: chapter 05 (GD) and chapter 06 (HR)
6. For each: solve exercises → take quiz → mark complete
7. Optional capstone: chapter 09 — build and legally ground a reward challenge
