# Day 13 Study Plan — Advanced Trees (AVL, Heap, Priority Queue)

## Overview
Day 13 focuses on **self-balancing trees (AVL)** and **heap data structures**—critical for understanding guaranteed O(log n) operations and priority-based algorithms.

## Daily Schedule (9 hours total)

### Morning Session (3 hours) — AVL Trees
**Hour 1:** Watch Abdul Bari AVL video, understand rotations (LL, RR, LR, RL)  
**Hour 2:** Implement `avl_tree.java` with insert and rotations  
**Hour 3:** Solve LC 110, practice rotation identification  

### Afternoon Session (3 hours) — Heap Fundamentals
**Hour 1:** Study heap theory, watch VisuAlgo visualizations  
**Hour 2:** Implement `min_heap.java` from scratch  
**Hour 3:** Solve LC 215 (Kth Largest), LC 347 (Top K Frequent)  

### Evening Session (3 hours) — Priority Queue Mastery
**Hour 1:** Master Java PriorityQueue, solve merge K sorted  
**Hour 2:** Solve LC 295 (Median Finder ⭐), LC 973, LC 23  
**Hour 3:** Practice 3 more problems, review solutions, mock interview  

## Learning Objectives
✅ Implement AVL with all 4 rotations  
✅ Build min/max heap from scratch  
✅ Master PriorityQueue for Top-K problems  
✅ Solve median finder using two heaps  
✅ Apply heap to scheduling problems  

## Resources Required
- `avl_tree.java`, `min_heap.java`, `priority_queue_examples.java`
- Abdul Bari AVL video, William Fiset Heap series
- LeetCode Heap tag (200+ problems)

## Expected Outcomes
- Solve 10+ heap problems

- Master Top-K and two-heaps patterns
- Complete 9-hour study session
