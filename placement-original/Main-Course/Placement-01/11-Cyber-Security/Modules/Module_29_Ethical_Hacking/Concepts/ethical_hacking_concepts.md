## 🎯 **ETHICAL HACKING FUNDAMENTALS**

### **Ethical Hacking Definition**
- **Authorized Testing:** Legally authorized security testing and assessment
- **White Hat Activities:** Defensive security research and vulnerability discovery
- **Methodical Approach:** Systematic security assessment methodologies
- **Legal Compliance:** Adherence to legal and ethical standards
- **Documentation Requirements:** Comprehensive testing documentation and reporting
- **Professional Standards:** Ethical hacking code of conduct and best practices

- **Legal Boundaries:** Navigating legal and regulatory requirements
- **Scope Limitations:** Defining clear testing boundaries and rules of engagement
- **False Positives:** Managing false positive results and validation requirements
- **Resource Intensity:** Time and resource requirements for comprehensive testing

## 📋 **ETHICAL HACKING METHODOLOGIES**

### **Penetration Testing Methodology**
- **Planning Phase:** Scope definition, rules of engagement, and legal agreements
- **Reconnaissance Phase:** Passive and active information gathering
- **Scanning Phase:** Network scanning, vulnerability scanning, and enumeration
- **Gaining Access Phase:** Exploitation of identified vulnerabilities
- **Maintaining Access Phase:** Privilege escalation and access persistence
- **Analysis & Reporting Phase:** Findings analysis and comprehensive reporting
