## Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (15 questions) — 40 minutes

1. (M) A trader mixes two qualities of rice costing ₹30/kg and ₹50/kg in the ratio 3:2 and sells at ₹44/kg. What is the profit percentage?
2. (M) The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 8% per annum is ₹64. What is the sum?
3. (M) A sum of money becomes 4 times itself in 18 years at SI. What is the rate of interest?
4. (H) Two trains 160 m and 240 m long run towards each other at 54 km/hr and 72 km/hr. How long to cross completely?
5. (H) A can do a work in 20 days and B in 30 days. They work together for 8 days, then A leaves. In how many more days will B finish?
6. (H) A pipe fills a tank in 10 hours. A leak empties it in 15 hours. Both open. How long to fill?
7. (H) The ratio of the ages of two brothers is 5:4. After 10 years, it will be 7:6. What are their present ages?
8. (H) A bag contains 6 white and 8 black balls. Two balls are drawn. What is the probability both are of different colors?
9. (X) In how many ways can the letters of the word "MISSISSIPPI" be arranged?
10. (X) Find the remainder when 2^100 is divided by 7.
11. (H) A boat travels 45 km downstream and 45 km upstream in 3 and 5 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
12. (H) The average of 9 numbers is 45. The average of the first 4 is 40 and the last 4 is 50. What is the middle number?
13. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 50% above cost and gives a 20% discount. What is the profit percentage?
14. (X) In how many ways can 5 boys and 4 girls be arranged in a row so that no two girls are together?
15. (X) A man invests ₹20,000 at 10% per annum compounded annually, withdrawing interest yearly. Total interest in 3 years?

## Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (15 questions) — 35 minutes

16. (M) Find the next term: 5, 14, 41, 122, 365, ?
17. (M) In a code, "PRIVATE" is written as "Q S J W B U F". How is "SECRET" coded?
18. (M) Statements: Some animals are cats. No cat is a fish.
    I. Some animals are not fish. II. Some fish are not cats.
19. (H) A man walks 5 km North, then 12 km East, then 5 km South. How far from the start?
20. (H) A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. C is the wife of D. D is the father of E. How is A related to E?
21. (H) In a row of 60 students, Ravi is 20th from the left and Sita is 15th from the right. How many students are between them?
22. (H) Data sufficiency: What is the value of x?
    (I) x² = 81
    (II) x is negative
23. (H) Input: 8 3 6 1 5 2. Step 1: 1 8 3 6 5 2. Step 2: 1 2 8 3 6 5. What is Step 3?
24. (H) Odd one out: 12, 18, 27, 36, 45
25. (H) Seven people A-G sit in a circle. A is opposite D. B is to the right of A. C is to the left of D. E is between B and C. F is to the right of B. Who is opposite E?
26. (H) Statement: "The company announced a 10% salary cut to avoid layoffs."
    I. The salary cut will prevent layoffs.
    II. Layoffs are always bad.
27. (X) In a code, "FRIEND" is coded as "G S J F O E", how is "BROTHER" coded?
28. (X) Eight people P-W sit in a row. P is 2nd from the left. Q is 2nd from the right. R is exactly between P and Q. S is immediately right of R. T is immediately left of P. Who sits at the 5th position from the left?
29. (X) Pointing to a photo, a man says, "She is the wife of my brother." How is she related to the man?
30. (X) A clock shows 4:30. What is the angle between the hands?
