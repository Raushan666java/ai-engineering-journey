# Day 3 — The Crunch Playbook

> **Status:** ○ Planned · Edit this line to ◐ Practicing · ● Drilled

You got the call. Interview is in 72 hours. The 7-day plan is impossible. This drill is for the candidate who must triage.

The rule: **don't try to do everything badly. Do five things well.**

---

## Inhale.

Three days is enough if you're ruthless about what you cut. The instinct is to grind every topic. That instinct will fry you.

Pick five things. Drill those five. Walk in calm.

---

## The Script — what you tell yourself

> *"I have 72 hours. I can't learn anything new — I can only sharpen what I already know. Five priorities, three days, eight hours per day cap."*

Five priorities. Not ten. Five.

---

## The Anatomy — why prioritization wins

A candidate who drills 5 things deeply will out-interview a candidate who skimmed 30 things shallowly. Interviewers ask follow-ups. Shallow knowledge fails on follow-up 2. Deep knowledge survives follow-up 5.

In 72 hours, you have time to go *deep* on 5 topics or *shallow* on 20. Deep wins.

---

## If you only have 3 days, do these 5 things

### Priority 1 — Tell Me About Yourself (90s version)

This question opens 100% of interviews. If TMAY is shaky, the rest of the round is recovery.

- [ ] Drill the 90s version 10 times in 3 days.
- [ ] Record yourself on Day 1. Listen, fix one filler word.
- [ ] Deliver cold on Day 3 morning. If it lands, you're set.

**Time cost:** 1 hour total across 3 days.

### Priority 2 — Top 3 STAR stories

Not 6. **Three.** One technical win, one conflict/team, one failure/learning. Three covers 90% of behavioral prompts.

- [ ] Write each in 4 bullets — Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- [ ] Each must have a *number* in the Result.
- [ ] Each must land at 60–90 seconds when spoken.
- [ ] Drill aloud 3× per day.

**Time cost:** 90 minutes total.

### Priority 3 — The 5 most-asked tech topics for the role

From the JD + Glassdoor pattern. For a Laravel role, that's usually: N+1 / eager loading, service container, queues, middleware, eloquent relationships. For Java: collections, multithreading, OOP, exception handling, JVM basics.

- [ ] Drill the war-room file for each.
- [ ] Re-write the key code block from memory.
- [ ] Time the script — should land at 45s for tech questions.

**Time cost:** 4 hours total. *This is your biggest block.*

### Priority 4 — Company research (1-pager)

Most candidates skip this. A 1-pager makes you stand out.

- [ ] Company About page + 1 engineering blog post.
- [ ] JD line-by-line — highlight tech keywords, map to your projects.
- [ ] **3 questions to ask the interviewer**, specific to the company.

**Time cost:** 90 minutes total.

### Priority 5 — Sleep

Yes, sleep. It is priority 5 because skipping it nullifies priorities 1–4.

- [ ] Day 3 — sleep by 11 PM.
- [ ] Day 2 — sleep by 10 PM.
- [ ] Day 1 — sleep by 10 PM, phone in another room.

**Time cost:** 7–8 hours of *removed* time per night. Cheaper than the alternative.

---

## What to skip when you have 3 days

| Cut | Why |
|---|---|
| New LeetCode problems | Won't stick under stress. Re-do 3 you already solved. |
| Reading new tech books | No retention in 72 hours. Skim a cheat sheet instead. |
| Mock interviews with strangers | High overhead to book, low ROI. Do a 20-min mock with a friend on Day 2. |
| Watching YouTube interview-anxiety videos | Increases anxiety. Net negative. |
| Adding new STAR stories beyond 3 | Stretches you thin. Three deep > six shallow. |
| Memorizing system design at this stage | Unless the role is senior. Skip for new-grad rounds. |
| Updating your portfolio site | Won't make the call. Update after the interview, not before. |

## The 3-Day Calendar

### Day 3 (72h out) — Foundation *(3 hours)*

- [ ] **(45 min)** Company research, JD breakdown, 3 questions for interviewer.
- [ ] **(60 min)** TMAY 90s version drilled aloud 5×, recorded once, listened to, fixed.
- [ ] **(60 min)** STAR stories written — 3 stories, 4 bullets each, metric in each result.
- [ ] **(15 min)** Pack bag: resume copies, JD, notepad, pen, water bottle.
- [ ] Sleep by 11 PM.

### Day 2 (48h out) — Tech Block *(4 hours)*

- [ ] **(2 hours)** Drill 5 priority tech topics. War-room files + code from memory.
- [ ] **(60 min)** Mock interview with a friend — TMAY + 2 STAR + 1 tech. 30 min total.
- [ ] **(45 min)** Re-drill the weakest topic from the mock.
- [ ] **(15 min)** Test laptop, camera, mic if virtual. Confirm address if onsite.
- [ ] Sleep by 10 PM.

### Day 1 (24h out) — Taper *(2 hours)*

- [ ] **(30 min)** TMAY 3× aloud — 30s, 90s, 5min.
- [ ] **(30 min)** Read STAR stories silently. Do not re-drill.
- [ ] **(30 min)** Skim the 5 tech topic scripts. Do not re-code.
- [ ] **(30 min)** Walk. Get blood moving. Lay out clothes. Set 3 alarms.
- [ ] **No new material.** No LeetCode. No YouTube.
- [ ] Sleep by 10 PM. Phone in another room.

### Day 0 — Execute

Open [Four Hours Before](four-hours-before.md).

## Recovery — when even 3 days falls apart

You're sick on Day 2. You had to work late. You couldn't do the mock.

> *"Compress to the 5 essentials: TMAY, 3 STAR, top 5 tech, 1-page research, sleep. Drop everything else."*

If you only have 24 hours left, jump to **[Day 1 — No New Learning](day-1.md)**.

If you have 12 hours left, focus on: TMAY (30 min) + 3 STAR (30 min) + 3 questions to ask (15 min) + sleep (the rest).

---

## Variants

=== "Got the call this morning, interview tomorrow"

    You have 12 hours. Do TMAY + 3 STAR + 3 interviewer questions + sleep. That's it. Walk in calm, win on basics.

=== "Got the call 3 days ago, interview tomorrow"

    Default to the 3-day calendar above. You're on track.

=== "Got the call this morning, interview in 3 days"

    You have the full 72 hours. Default to the 3-day calendar.

---

## Common traps

| Trap | Why it kills | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Tries to do the 7-day plan in 3 days | Burnout, foggy on Day 0 | Pick 5 priorities. Drop the rest. |
| Studies new topics | No retention in 72h | Sharpen what you have |
| Skips sleep "just one night" | Cortisol wrecks recall, mood, posture | Sleep is priority 5 for a reason |
| 6+ STAR stories | Stretches you thin | Three deep beats six shallow |
| Skips company research | Sounds like spray-and-pray | 90 minutes — non-negotiable |
| All-nighter on Day 1 | Single biggest predictor of bad performance | Sleep wins |
| No mock interview | First pressure rep = the real round | 20-min mock with anyone, Day 2 |
