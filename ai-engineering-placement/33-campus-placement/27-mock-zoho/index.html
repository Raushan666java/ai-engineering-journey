Zoho Mock Test — Full Pattern Simulation (40 Questions)

Simulates Zoho's harder aptitude section: 15 quant, 15 logical, 10 verbal. Time limits: Quant 40 min, Logical 35 min, Verbal 15 min. Total: 90 minutes. Zoho is the hardest of the mid-size companies — no partial marks, 80%+ is the safe target. Difficulty tags: (M) Medium, (H) Hard, (X) Very hard.

Zoho Pattern — What You Need to Know​

ItemDetail
Aptitude15 questions in 40 minutes
Logical15 questions in 35 minutes
Verbal10 questions in 15 minutes
Negative markingNo penalty
Passing80%+ recommended — Zoho is competitive
FlavorAdvanced quant (probability, P&C, geometry), puzzle-heavy reasoning

Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (15 questions) — 40 minutes​

  1. (M) A trader mixes two qualities of rice costing ₹30/kg and ₹50/kg in the ratio 3:2 and sells at ₹44/kg. What is the profit percentage?
  2. (M) The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 8% per annum is ₹64. What is the sum?
  3. (M) A sum of money becomes 4 times itself in 18 years at SI. What is the rate of interest?
  4. (H) Two trains 160 m and 240 m long run towards each other at 54 km/hr and 72 km/hr. How long to cross completely?
  5. (H) A can do a work in 20 days and B in 30 days. They work together for 8 days, then A leaves. In how many more days will B finish?
  6. (H) A pipe fills a tank in 10 hours. A leak empties it in 15 hours. Both open. How long to fill?
  7. (H) The ratio of the ages of two brothers is 5:4. After 10 years, it will be 7:6. What are their present ages?
  8. (H) A bag contains 6 white and 8 black balls. Two balls are drawn. What is the probability both are of different colors?
  9. (X) In how many ways can the letters of the word "MISSISSIPPI" be arranged?
  10. (X) Find the remainder when 2^100 is divided by 7.
  11. (H) A boat travels 45 km downstream and 45 km upstream in 3 and 5 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
  12. (H) The average of 9 numbers is 45. The average of the first 4 is 40 and the last 4 is 50. What is the middle number?
  13. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 50% above cost and gives a 20% discount. What is the profit percentage?
  14. (X) In how many ways can 5 boys and 4 girls be arranged in a row so that no two girls are together?
  15. (X) A man invests ₹20,000 at 10% per annum compounded annually, withdrawing interest yearly. Total interest in 3 years?

Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (15 questions) — 35 minutes​

  1. (M) Find the next term: 5, 14, 41, 122, 365, ?
  2. (M) In a code, "PRIVATE" is written as "Q S J W B U F". How is "SECRET" coded?
  3. (M) Statements: Some animals are cats. No cat is a fish. I. Some animals are not fish. II. Some fish are not cats.
  4. (H) A man walks 5 km North, then 12 km East, then 5 km South. How far from the start?
  5. (H) A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. C is the wife of D. D is the father of E. How is A related to E?
  6. (H) In a row of 60 students, Ravi is 20th from the left and Sita is 15th from the right. How many students are between them?
  7. (H) Data sufficiency: What is the value of x? (I) x² = 81 (II) x is negative
  8. (H) Input: 8 3 6 1 5 2. Step 1: 1 8 3 6 5 2. Step 2: 1 2 8 3 6 5. What is Step 3?
  9. (H) Odd one out: 12, 18, 27, 36, 45
  10. (H) Seven people A-G sit in a circle. A is opposite D. B is to the right of A. C is to the left of D. E is between B and C. F is to the right of B. Who is opposite E?
  11. (H) Statement: "The company announced a 10% salary cut to avoid layoffs." I. The salary cut will prevent layoffs. II. Layoffs are always bad.
  12. (X) In a code, "FRIEND" is coded as "G S J F O E", how is "BROTHER" coded?
  13. (X) Eight people P-W sit in a row. P is 2nd from the left. Q is 2nd from the right. R is exactly between P and Q. S is immediately right of R. T is immediately left of P. Who sits at the 5th position from the left?
  14. (X) Pointing to a photo, a man says, "She is the wife of my brother." How is she related to the man?
  15. (X) A clock shows 4:30. What is the angle between the hands?

Section 3 — Verbal Ability (10 questions) — 15 minutes​

  1. (M) Synonym of "lucid": (A) vague (B) clear (C) dark (D) heavy
  2. (M) Antonym of "meager": (A) abundant (B) scarce (C) tiny (D) weak
  3. (H) Fill in the blank: "The number of employees ___ growing." (is / are)
  4. (H) Spot the error: "Each of the students (A) have submitted (B) their assignments (C) on time (D)."
  5. (H) Correct: "The manager with his team are attending the conference."
  6. (H) Para jumble: (a) The transaction was completed. (b) The customer entered his PIN. (c) The machine dispensed the cash. (d) A receipt was printed. Order?
  7. (H) Choose the correct spelling: (A) maintenance (B) maintanance (C) maintainence (D) maintenence
  8. (H) Idiom: "To bite the dust" means: (A) to eat (B) to fail or be defeated (C) to work hard (D) to travel
  9. (X) Fill in the blank: "Hardly had the train departed ___ the platform was closed." (than / when / that)
  10. (X) The word closest in meaning to "obfuscate" is: (A) clarify (B) confuse (C) simplify (D) explain

Answer Key​

Section 1 — Quantitative (1-15)​

  1. 10% (cost 5 kg = 90+100 = 190 → ₹38/kg; SP 44 → 6/38 = 15.79% — recheck: 30×3 = 90, 50×2 = 100, total 190 for 5 kg → 38/kg; 44/38 = 1.158 → 15.79%) — 15.79%
  2. ₹10,000 (64 = P × 0.0064)
  3. 16.67% (interest = 3P in 18 yr → R = 300/18 = 16.67%)
  4. 11.43 seconds (relative = 126 km/hr = 35 m/s; 400/35)
  5. 14.67 days (8 days together = 8 × (1/20 + 1/30) = 2/3; remaining 1/3 by B = 10 days) — 10 days
  6. 30 hours (1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30)
  7. 25 and 20 ((5k+10)/(4k+10) = 7/6 → 30k + 60 = 28k + 70 → k = 5)
  8. 48/91 (C(6,1)×C(8,1)/C(14,2) = 48/91)
  9. 34,650 (11!/(4!×4!×2!))
  10. 4 (2^n mod 7: 2,4,1 (length 3); 100 = 3×33 + 1 → 1st = 2 — redo: 2^100 mod 7: cycle 2,4,1; 100 mod 3 = 1 → 2) — 2
  11. 3 km/hr (downstream 15, upstream 9; stream = (15−9)/2)
  12. 45 (total = 405; first 4 = 160, last 4 = 200; middle = 405 − 360 = 45)
  13. 20% (100 → 150 → 120)
  14. 43,200 (boys 5! = 120; 6 gaps for 4 girls: P(6,4) = 360; 120 × 360)
  15. ₹6,000 (interest withdrawn yearly = simple: 2,000 × 3)

Section 2 — Logical (16-30)​

  1. 1,094 (×3 − 1: 365 × 3 − 1)
  2. T F D S F U (+1 each letter)
  3. Only I (the cats that are animals are not fish; II is a guaranteed negation but not a conclusion that follows from the given — only I follows as the valid inference)
  4. 12 km East (5N − 5S cancel; 12E remains)
  5. Brother (C is A's mother; D is C's husband → D is A's father; E is D's child → E is A's sibling; A is male → brother)
  6. 25 students (Sita = 45th from left; 45 − 20 − 1 = 24) — 24
  7. Both together (x = −9)
  8. 1 2 3 8 6 5 (smallest moves left; 3 next)
  9. 27 (others are 12, 18, 36, 45 — all divisible by 3 and 6 except 27... 12,18,36,45 differ by pattern; standard: all are multiples of 6 except 27? 12 ✓, 18 ✓, 36 ✓, 45 ✗ — so 45 is the non-multiple of 6. But the intended odd one: 27 = 3³ (perfect cube), others not — answer 27) — 27 (only perfect cube)
  10. Cannot be determined (needs the full circle)
  11. Only I (the stated purpose; II is extreme)
  12. C S P U I F S (+1 each letter)
  13. S (positions: P at 2, T at 1; Q at 7; R exactly between P(2) and Q(7) → not integer — redo: 8 people, P at 2, Q at 7; between = positions 3-6; R exactly between 2 and 7 → 4.5 → not possible. Adjust: R between P and Q (any of 3,4,5,6); S right of R. Ambiguous — answer cannot be determined) — Cannot be determined
  14. Sister-in-law (my brother's wife = my sister-in-law)
  15. 45° (hour hand at 4.5 × 30 = 135°; minute hand at 6 × 30 = 180°; difference 45°)

Section 3 — Verbal (31-40)​

  1. B) clear
  2. A) abundant
  3. is ("the number of" = singular)
  4. A (each → has; also "their" → "his/her")
  5. "The manager with his team is attending the conference" (subject = manager)
  6. b, a, c, d (entered PIN → transaction completed → cash dispensed → receipt)
  7. A) maintenance
  8. B) to fail or be defeated
  9. when (hardly...when)
  10. B) confuse

Detailed Solution Explanations​

Quantitative — Step-by-Step​

Q1. Mix ₹30/kg and ₹50/kg in 3:2, sell ₹44/kg. Profit %?

Cost of 5 kg = 30×3 + 50×2 = 90 + 100 = 190
Cost per kg = 190/5 = ₹38
SP = ₹44
Profit = 6/38 × 100 = 15.79%

Q2. CI − SI for 2 years at 8% = ₹64. Sum?

64 = P(0.0064)
P = ₹10,000

Q3. Sum becomes 4× in 18 years at SI. Rate?

3P = P × R × 18/100
R = 300/18 = 16.67%

Q4. Trains 160m and 240m, 54 and 72 km/hr. Time to cross?

Relative = 126 km/hr = 35 m/s
Total = 400 m
Time = 400/35 = 11.43 seconds

Q5. A = 20 days, B = 30 days. Together 8 days, A leaves. B finishes?

8 days together = 8(1/20 + 1/30) = 8(5/60) = 2/3
Remaining = 1/3
B's time = 1/3 × 30 = 10 days

Q6. Pipe fills 10 hrs, leak empties 15 hrs. Both?

1/10 − 1/15 = 3/30 − 2/30 = 1/30
Time = 30 hours

Q7. Brothers ratio 5:4, after 10 years = 7:6. Ages?

(5k+10)/(4k+10) = 7/6
30k + 60 = 28k + 70
2k = 10 → k = 5
Ages: 25 and 20

Q8. 6 white, 8 black. Two drawn. P(different colors)?

P(different) = C(6,1)×C(8,1)/C(14,2) = 48/91

Q9. Letters of "MISSISSIPPI" arranged?

11!/(4!×4!×2!) = 34,650

Q10. Remainder 2^100 ÷ 7?

Cycle of 2^n mod 7: 2, 4, 1 (length 3)
100 mod 3 = 1
Position 1 = 2

Q11. Boat: 45 km down 3 hrs, up 5 hrs. Stream?

Down = 15, Up = 9
Stream = (15−9)/2 = 3 km/hr

Q12. Average 9 numbers = 45. First 4 avg 40, last 4 avg 50. Middle?

Total = 405
First 4 = 160, Last 4 = 200
Middle = 405 − 360 = 45

Q13. Markup 50%, discount 20%. Profit?

100 → 150 → 120
Profit = 20%

Q14. 5 boys, 4 girls, no two girls together?

Boys: 5! = 120
Gaps: P(6,4) = 360
Total = 43,200

Q15. ₹20,000 at 10% CI, withdraw interest yearly. Total 3 years?

Withdrawn = simple interest
10% of 20,000 = 2,000/year
Total = ₹6,000

Logical — Step-by-Step​

Q16. Next term: 5, 14, 41, 122, 365, ?

×3 − 1: 365 × 3 − 1 = 1,094

Q17. "PRIVATE" → "Q S J W B U F". "SECRET"?

+1 each: S→T, E→F, C→D, R→S, E→F, T→U
Answer: T F D S F U

Q18. Some animals are cats. No cat is a fish.

I. Some animals are not fish — follows (cats that are animals can't be fish)
II. Some fish are not cats — follows (no cat is fish → some fish aren't cats)
Both follow

Q19. 5 km North, 12 km East, 5 km South. Position?

North and South cancel (5−5=0)
Remaining: 12 km East

Q20. A brother of B. B daughter of C. C wife of D. D father of E. A related to E?

C is A's mother. D is A's father. E is D's child → E is A's sibling.
A is E's brother.

Q21. Ravi 20th left, Sita 15th right, 60 students. Between?

Sita from left = 60 − 15 + 1 = 46th
Between = 46 − 20 − 1 = 25

Q22. Data sufficiency: x² = 81, x is negative.

x = −9
Both together sufficient.

Q23. Input: 8 3 6 1 5 2. Step 1: 1 8 3 6 5 2. Step 2: 1 2 8 3 6 5. Step 3?

Smallest moves left, then 3 next
Step 3: 1 2 3 8 6 5

Q24. Odd one out: 12, 18, 27, 36, 45

12, 18, 36, 45 are all divisible by 3 and 6
27 = 3³ (perfect cube)
Answer: 27

Q25. Circle: A opposite D. B right of A. C left of D. E between B and C. F right of B. Opposite E?

Needs full circle layout — cannot be determined

Q26. Company announced 10% salary cut to avoid layoffs.

I. Will prevent layoffs — reasonable inference
II. Layoffs are always bad — extreme statement
Answer: Only I

Q27. "FRIEND" → "G S J F O E". "BROTHER"?

+1 each: B→C, R→S, O→P, T→U, H→I, E→F, R→S
Answer: C S P U I F S

Q28. Eight people row. P 2nd left. Q 2nd right. R between P and Q. S right of R. T left of P. Position 5?

P(2), Q(7), T(1)
R between 2 and 7 → could be 3,4,5,6
S right of R
Ambiguous — cannot be determined

Q29. "She is the wife of my brother." Related?

Brother's wife = sister-in-law

Q30. Clock shows 4:30. Angle?

Hour hand: 4.5 × 30 = 135°
Minute hand: 6 × 30 = 180°
Angle = |180 − 135| = 45°

Verbal — Step-by-Step​

Q31. Synonym of "lucid"?

Lucid = clear
Answer: B) clear

Q32. Antonym of "meager"?

Meager = scanty
Antonym = abundant
Answer: A) abundant

Q33. "The number of employees ___ growing."

"The number of" = singular
Answer: is

Q34. Error: "Each of the students have submitted."

Each = singular → "has submitted"

Q35. "The manager with his team are attending."

Subject = manager (singular) → "is attending"

Q36. Para jumble: transaction, PIN, cash, receipt.

Entered PIN → transaction → cash dispensed → receipt
Answer: b, a, c, d

Q37. Spelling: maintenance

Answer: A) maintenance

Q38. Idiom: "To bite the dust"

= to fail or be defeated
Answer: B

Q39. "Hardly had the train departed ___ the platform was closed."

Hardly...when
Answer: when

Q40. Closest to "obfuscate"?

Obfuscate = confuse
Answer: B) confuse
