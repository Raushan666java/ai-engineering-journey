Syllogism (5 Qs)​

Q46. Statements: All banks are financial institutions. No financial institution is a NBFC. Conclusions: I. No bank is a NBFC. II. All NBFCs are banks.

A) Only I
B) Only II
C) Both
D) Neither

Show Answer

Answer: A) Only I follows

Explanation: Banks ⊆ FIs, FIs ∩ NBFCs = ∅ → I follows. II is not true.

Q47. Statements: Some loans are secured. All secured loans are risky. Conclusions: I. Some risky things are loans. II. All risky things are secured.

A) Only I
B) Only II
C) Both
D) Neither

Show Answer

Answer: A) Only I follows

Explanation: Some loans ⊆ secured ⊆ risky → some risky things are loans ✓. II is an overgeneralization.

B) Only II
C) Both
D) Neither
