# Quantitative Aptitude — 100 Question Drill Bank (Any Company)

> Rule: 60-90 seconds per question. Write your answer, then check against the key at the bottom.
> Covers all 16 topics from `quant-all-topics.md`. Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.
> Score guide at the bottom. Use with `aptitude-drill.py` for timed random drills.

## Section 1 — Percentages (7 questions)

1. (E) A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 25%. What is the net percentage change?
2. (E) If 35% of a number is 105, what is 60% of that number?
3. (M) The population of a town increases by 10% per year. If the current population is 1,46,410, what was it 2 years ago?
4. (M) A's salary is 20% less than B's. By what percentage is B's salary more than A's?
5. (M) In an exam, 40% failed in English, 30% failed in Maths, and 15% failed in both. What percentage passed in both?
6. (M) A candidate scored 30% and failed by 15 marks. Another scored 45% and got 45 marks more than the passing mark. What is the maximum marks?
7. (H) The price of sugar increased by 25%. By what percentage should consumption be reduced so that expenditure stays the same?

## Section 2 — Profit, Loss & Discount (7 questions)

8. (E) A shopkeeper bought an article for ₹640 and sold it for ₹800. What is his profit percentage?
9. (E) An article is sold at a loss of 20%. If the selling price is ₹1,440, what is the cost price?
10. (M) A shopkeeper gives a 10% discount on the marked price and still makes a 17% profit. If the cost price is ₹500, what is the marked price?
11. (M) A trader mixes rice costing ₹28/kg and ₹40/kg in the ratio 3:2 and sells the mixture at ₹36/kg. What is his profit percentage?
12. (M) A dealer marks goods 40% above cost and allows a discount of 15%. What is his profit percentage?
13. (M) Two successive discounts of 20% and 10% are equivalent to a single discount of what percentage?
14. (H) A man sells a watch at a profit of 15%. If he had sold it for ₹120 less, he would have made a loss of 5%. What is the cost price of the watch?

## Section 3 — Simple & Compound Interest (7 questions)

16. (E) What is the compound interest on ₹12,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually?
17. (M) The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 8% per annum is ₹128. What is the sum?
18. (M) A sum doubles itself in 10 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 3 times itself?

21. (H) The SI on a sum is 4/9 of the sum. The number of years equals the rate percent. Find the rate.
