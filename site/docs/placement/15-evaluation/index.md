# 15 — AI Evaluation & Observability

## Overview

Learn systematic approaches to evaluating AI systems and maintaining observability in production. Covers metrics, LLM-as-judge, evaluation datasets, tracing, monitoring, and incident response.

## Sub-Chapters

| # | Sub-Chapter | Topics | Q&A |
|---|-------------|--------|-----|
| 01 | [Evaluation Metrics](01-evaluation-metrics.md) | accuracy, precision, recall, F1, RMSE, MAE, ROUGE, BLEU | 10 |
| 02 | [LLM-as-Judge](02-llm-as-judge.md) | bias, calibration, rubric scoring, pairwise comparison | 10 |
| 03 | [Evaluation Datasets](03-evaluation-datasets.md) | golden datasets, synthetic data, human annotation, versioning | 10 |
| 04 | [Observability Tools](04-observability-tools.md) | LangSmith, W&B, MLflow, Arize, whyLabs | 10 |
| 05 | [Tracing & Monitoring](05-tracing-and-monitoring.md) | spans, traces, latency, throughput, cost tracking | 10 |
| 06 | [Alerting & Incident Response](06-alerting-and-incident-response.md) | thresholds, drift alerts, runbooks, rollback, postmortem | 10 |

## Learning Path

1. Read sub-chapters in order
2. For each: read theory ? run code ? review Q&A ? take quiz ? do exercises
3. Mark complete when all sub-chapters done
