## 💡 KEY LEARNINGS SUMMARY

### Top 10 Insights
1. **Lazy Evaluation**: Streams don't execute until a terminal operation calls them
2. **map vs flatMap**: Use map for 1-to-1, flatMap for 1-to-many transformations
3. **Functional Interfaces**: Single Abstract Method (SAM) is key to lambda usage
4. **Method References**: 4 types cover most real-world scenarios
5. **Parallel Performance**: Not always faster; benchmark first!
6. **Thread Safety**: Stateless operations are inherently thread-safe
7. **Optional API**: Better than null checking for handling absence of values
8. **Composition**: andThen/compose enable elegant chaining
9. **Collectors**: Framework powerful enough for complex aggregations
10. **Functional Error Handling**: Optional + Stream patterns replace try-catch

### Common Pitfalls Avoided
- ✅ Not reusing streams after terminal operations
- ✅ Over-parallelizing small datasets
- ✅ Forgetting about variable capture rules
- ✅ Choosing sequential over parallel blindly
- ✅ Not handling null values with Optional
- ✅ Writing inefficient multi-step collectors
