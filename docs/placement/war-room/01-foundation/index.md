# Phase 01 — Foundation

> The first 5 minutes and the last 5 minutes of every interview live here. Master these six drills and you control the opening and the close of every round you walk into.

This is the phase where preparation gives you the most leverage. You can't predict every technical question — but **every** interview will open with "tell me about yourself" and close with "why should we hire you." Owning these is owning ~30% of the round.

---

## Inhale.

Six drills. Each one is 150–300 lines. Each one is a script + practice plan + recovery line. Read them in order. Drill them on rotation.

Don't try to memorize all six in one day. Pick one. Drill it for a week. Move to the next.

---

## The six drills

| Drill | Status | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|
| **[Tell Me About Yourself](tell-me-about-yourself.md)** | ○ Planned | The 90-second script that opens 95% of interviews. Hook + stack + close. |
| **[Why This Company](why-this-company.md)** | ○ Planned | The 60s answer that signals you did 15 minutes of homework. Three concrete reasons, no flattery. |
| **[Why Should We Hire You](why-should-we-hire-you.md)** | ○ Planned | The 45s closer. Unique skill combination + one metric + JD mirror + confident close. |
| **[Greatest Strength](greatest-strength.md)** | ○ Planned | One strength, one project, one self-awareness moment. Never list three. |
| **[Greatest Weakness](greatest-weakness.md)** | ○ Planned | The trap question. Real weakness + concrete action + measurable improvement. Never say "perfectionist." |
| **[Where In 5 Years](where-in-5-years.md)** | ○ Planned | Career trajectory tied to the company's growth path. Service-based vs product variants. |

Update each status badge as you progress: **○ Planned → ◐ Practicing → ● Drilled.**

## The drill order

If you have one week — drill **TMAY only**. It's the highest-leverage one. Owning it well makes the rest of the round flow.

If you have two weeks — add **Why This Company** and **Why Should We Hire You**. Now you've solved the opening and the closing.

If you have a month — drill all six. The middle questions (strength, weakness, 5 years) come up in ~60% of behavioral rounds. They're worth the time.

Three weeks before placements? **Stop optimizing this phase and move to Phase 02 (Behavioral STAR).** You can be 80% ready on Foundation; the marginal hour is better spent on STAR stories you don't have yet.

---

## How to drill (the method)

Every drill in this phase follows the same shape:

1. **Read the script aloud, 5 times on Day 1.** Don't memorize. Just say the words.
2. **Record yourself on Day 2.** Find one filler word you say too often. Decide to kill it.
3. **Time yourself on Day 3.** Hit the target window (30s, 45s, 60s, 90s — depends on the drill).
4. **Mock with a friend on Day 4.** Eye contact, no notes, no fidgeting.
5. **Cold delivery on Day 5.** Wake up, deliver the script before reading it. If you can do it cold, you own it.

Five days per drill. Six drills. **Thirty days** to solve the entire foundation phase. That's the price.

---

## What this phase doesn't cover

These drills handle **the predictable behavioral questions**. They don't cover:

- **STAR stories** (failure, leadership, disagreement) → see [Phase 02 — Behavioral](../02-behavioral/index.md)
- **Project deep dives** ("walk me through your project") → see Phase 03 — Project Pitch
- **Technical questions** ("explain N+1 in Laravel") → see Phase 04+ — Technical drills

Foundation is the **scaffolding**. The rest of the war room is what hangs on it.

---

## After this phase

When all six drills are **● Drilled**, you have:

- A 30s, 90s, and 5-min script for "tell me about yourself"
- A 60s answer for "why this company"
- A 45s closer for "why should we hire you"
- One strength + one weakness with real proof
- A 5-year answer tied to your target company type

That's roughly 8–12 minutes of the interview on autopilot. Every minute on autopilot is a minute your brain can spend on the questions you couldn't predict.
