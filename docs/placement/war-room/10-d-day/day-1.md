# Day 1 — No New Learning

> **Status:** ○ Planned · Edit this line to ◐ Practicing · ● Drilled

24 hours out. The single most-violated rule in interview prep: **do not learn new things today**. Today is logistics, light review, sleep.

If you don't know it by now, you won't know it tomorrow. Stop adding. Start consolidating.

---

## Inhale.

Close LeetCode. Close YouTube. Close the textbook. Open this file.

Today is not a study day. Today is a *taper* day — like an athlete the day before a race.

---

## The Script — what you tell yourself

> *"I'm not learning anything new today. I'm reviewing what I already know, packing my bag, and getting to bed by 10 PM."*

That's the entire frame. Anything that doesn't fit that frame, you don't do today.

---

## The Anatomy — why tapering wins

Marathon runners don't run 26 miles the day before the race. They jog 2 miles, eat carbs, sleep 9 hours.

Your brain is the same. The day before, learning new material:

- Adds mental load (the new thing crowds out the rehearsed thing).
- Triggers anxiety (you find out you don't fully understand it, now you panic).
- Steals sleep (you stay up "just one more video").

The candidates who outperform are not the ones who studied 14 hours on Day 1. They are the ones who slept 8 hours, walked in clear-headed, and recalled what they'd already drilled.

## The Day 1 Calendar

### Morning (1 hour) — Light Script Review

- [ ] TMAY 90s version, aloud 3 times. No notes.
- [ ] TMAY 30s version, aloud 2 times.
- [ ] TMAY 5min version, aloud 1 time.
- [ ] Read your 6 STAR stories silently. Don't deliver aloud.
- [ ] Read the top 5 tech topic scripts silently. Don't re-code.

That's it. 60 minutes max.

### Midday (30 min) — Logistics

- [ ] **Print 3 copies of your resume.** Even for virtual — keep one beside you for reference.
- [ ] **Print the JD.** Highlight the 3 tech keywords you'll work into answers.
- [ ] **Print your 5 interviewer questions.** Bring the page.
- [ ] **Pack the bag** — resume copies, JD, questions page, notepad, two pens, water bottle, ID, charger, headphones.
- [ ] **Lay out clothes** — full outfit, including socks, belt, shoes. Iron if needed.

### Afternoon (1 hour) — Tech Check (virtual only)

If virtual interview:

- [ ] Test the meeting link in the exact browser/app you'll use.
- [ ] Check camera angle — face in upper third, eyes at lens level, light from front (not behind).
- [ ] Test mic — record 30s, listen back. Adjust if echo or muffle.
- [ ] Test screen share — open your code editor, share the screen, confirm it's readable.
- [ ] Confirm internet — run a speed test. If under 25 Mbps up, switch to ethernet or move closer to router.
- [ ] Charge laptop fully. Bring a phone hotspot as backup.

If onsite:

- [ ] Look up the address on Maps. Note travel time + 30 min buffer.
- [ ] Note the floor, the office name, the reception phone number.
- [ ] Identify a backup transport plan (Uber if Metro fails).

### Evening (90 min) — Wind Down

- [ ] **Walk for 30 minutes.** Outside. No phone. Just walk.
- [ ] **Light dinner** — protein + carbs, nothing heavy or spicy. No alcohol.
- [ ] **Set 3 alarms** — primary, backup, last-resort. Phone on charger across the room.
- [ ] **Shower** — get the day off you.
- [ ] **Sleep by 10 PM.** Phone in another room or in airplane mode.

## Things to NOT do in the last 24 hours

This list matters more than the calendar above.

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| **Don't learn new topics** | No retention. Steals time from review. Increases anxiety. |
| **Don't do new LeetCode problems** | Failing one tonight will spiral your confidence for tomorrow. |
| **Don't watch interview-anxiety YouTube** | Net negative. Algorithm will serve worse videos as you watch. |
| **Don't drink heavily** | Hangover ruins recall. One beer cap. Better: none. |
| **Don't argue with anyone** | Family, partner, roommate. Walk away. Anger costs cognitive cycles for 24 hours. |
| **Don't cram on weak topics** | If it's weak Day 1, it's weak Day 0. Lean on strengths in the interview, deflect on weaknesses. |
| **Don't try a new diet or new exercise** | Stomach trouble or sore muscles ruin tomorrow. Eat what you know agrees with you. |
| **Don't sleep less than 7 hours** | Single biggest predictor of bad performance. Worse than skipping prep entirely. |
| **Don't post about it on social media** | Public commitment increases pressure. Quiet effort > loud effort. |
| **Don't text 10 friends "wish me luck"** | Each reply nudges your anxiety. Tell 1–2 people who'll respond well. |

## The 5-day practice drill *(use this to build the Day 1 habit before you need it)*

- [ ] **Day 1 — Pre-stage the bag template.** Today, list every item you'd pack for an interview. Save the list. You'll use it every time.
- [ ] **Day 2 — Run a virtual tech-check rehearsal.** Open Zoom, point camera at yourself, record 30 seconds, look at the result. Fix lighting/angle now, not the night before an interview.
- [ ] **Day 3 — Try a "no new learning" evening.** Spend a study night just reviewing, no new content. Note how it feels.
- [ ] **Day 4 — Sleep audit.** Sleep by 10 PM for 4 nights. Track if you wake more rested.
- [ ] **Day 5 — Practice the morning routine** (see [Four Hours Before](four-hours-before.md)) as a dry run before a real interview.

## Recovery — when Day 1 panic hits

It's 11 PM. You can't sleep. You're convinced you forgot everything. Your stomach is in knots.

This is normal. Every candidate gets it.

> *"I've drilled the scripts. The material is in there. Sleep loads it to retrieval. If I lie here calmly, my body rests even if my brain doesn't."*

**Don't open the laptop.** Don't "review one more time." That always becomes 2 hours and worse sleep.

Instead:

- Lie flat, eyes closed.
- Box breathing — inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Ten cycles.
- Mentally rehearse the TMAY script slowly. If you drift to sleep mid-script, you've won.
- If you genuinely cannot sleep after 90 minutes, get up, drink water, read fiction (not tech) for 20 minutes, then return.

## Variants

=== "Onsite interview tomorrow"

    Add: print the address, confirm transport, check weather, plan a snack you can eat in the lobby.

=== "Virtual interview tomorrow"

    Add: tech check is non-negotiable. Test the link, test the mic, charge devices.

=== "Multi-round day tomorrow"

    Add: pack snacks (granola bar, fruit), extra water, mints. Plan a 5-min reset between rounds — bathroom break, water, deep breaths.

=== "Out-of-town interview tomorrow"

    Day 1 was probably travel day. Adjust: light review on the flight/train, hotel check-in, walk the route to the office, then taper as above.

## Common traps

| Trap | Why it kills | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Late-night cram session | Steals sleep, no retention | Close laptop by 8 PM |
| One LeetCode "warm-up" | Spirals if you fail one | Skip entirely |
| New caffeine source | Jitters or insomnia | Drink what you usually drink |
| Skipping dinner | Low blood sugar in morning | Eat normally |
| Late-night anxiety scroll | Algorithm feeds worse content | Phone in another room |
| Argument with family | Lingers for 24 hours | Walk away from any conflict today |
| Trying new clothes | Discomfort distracts tomorrow | Wear what you know fits |
| No bag pre-pack | Morning chaos | Pack tonight, not tomorrow |
