# Deloitte Mock Test — Full Pattern Simulation (44 Questions)

> Simulates Deloitte's reasoning-heavy aptitude section: 14 quant, 15 logical, 15 verbal.
> Time limits: Quant 30 min, Logical 30 min, Verbal 30 min. Total: 90 minutes.
> Deloitte weighs reasoning and verbal heavily — quant is moderate but must not drag your score down.
> Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

## Deloitte Pattern — What You Need to Know

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cognitive round | Quant + logical + verbal in one sitting |
| Time | ~44 questions in 90 minutes |
| Negative marking | No penalty |
| Passing | 70%+ recommended |
| Flavor | Reasoning-heavy; verbal comprehension counts; data sufficiency frequent |

## Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (14 questions) — 30 minutes

1. (E) What is 35% of 280?
2. (E) A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹250 and sells at a 20% profit. What is the selling price?
3. (M) The average of 14 numbers is 42. If one number 70 is removed, what is the new average?
4. (M) A train 180 m long crosses a platform 420 m long in 20 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
5. (M) A and B can do a job in 14 days and 21 days. Together, in how many days?
6. (M) What is the SI on ₹12,000 at 7.5% per annum for 2 years?
7. (M) A sum of money becomes 6 times itself at SI in 25 years. What is the rate?
8. (M) Two numbers are in the ratio 5:8. If 10 is added to each, the ratio becomes 7:10. Find the smaller number.
9. (M) Find the next term: 3, 11, 27, 59, 123, ?
10. (M) A bag has 5 red, 4 green, 3 blue balls. One drawn. Probability green?
11. (M) In how many ways can 6 people be seated in a row?
12. (M) The LCM of two numbers is 240 and their HCF is 20. If one number is 60, what is the other?
13. (H) A boat travels 48 km downstream in 4 hours and 48 km upstream in 8 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
14. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 45% above cost and gives a 20% discount. What is the profit percentage?

## Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (15 questions) — 30 minutes

15. (E) Find the next term: 6, 18, 54, 162, ?
16. (E) In a code, "MOUSE" is written as "NPVTF". How is "KEYBOARD" written?
17. (M) Statements: All birds can fly. Some birds are penguins.
    I. Some penguins can fly. II. All birds are penguins.
18. (M) A man walks 3 km North, then 4 km West. How far from the start?
19. (M) A is the son of B. B is the wife of C. C is the father of D. How is D related to A?
20. (M) In a row of 45 students, Ravi is 16th from the left. What is his position from the right?
21. (M) Data sufficiency: What is the value of x?
    (I) x + 2y = 14
    (II) 3x − y = 7
22. (M) Input: 4 7 2 9 5. Step 1: 2 4 7 9 5. Step 2: 2 4 5 7 9. What is Step 3?
23. (M) Odd one out: 13, 17, 23, 29, 31
24. (M) Five friends A-E sit in a row. A is at the left end. B is 3rd from the left. C is at the right end. D is between A and B. E is between B and C. Who is in the middle?
25. (H) Six friends A-F sit in a circle. A is opposite B. C is to the right of A. D is to the left of B. E is opposite F. Who is opposite C?
26. (H) Statement: "The company introduced a 4-day work week to improve employee well-being."
    I. A 4-day week can improve well-being.
    II. All companies should adopt a 4-day week.
27. (H) In a code, "CHAIR" is coded as "DIBJS", how is "TABLE" coded?
28. (H) Seven people A-G sit in a row. A is 3rd from the left. B is 3rd from the right. C is exactly between A and B. Who sits at the 5th position?
29. (H) Pointing to a photo, a woman says, "He is my brother's father." How is he related to her?

## Section 3 — Verbal Ability (15 questions) — 30 minutes

30. (E) Synonym of "quick": (A) slow (B) rapid (C) late (D) lazy
31. (E) Antonym of "bright": (A) shiny (B) dull (C) glowing (D) clear
32. (M) Fill in the blank: "The committee ___ yet to announce the results." (has / have)
33. (M) Spot the error: "Each of the boys (A) have completed (B) the assignment (C) on time (D)."
34. (M) Correct: "Neither of the candidates were suitable."
35. (M) Correct: "One of my friends are a manager."
36. (M) Para jumble: (a) The city implemented a bike-sharing program. (b) Commuters embraced it within months. (c) Traffic congestion eased noticeably. (d) The program launched with 500 stations. Order?
37. (M) Choose the correct spelling: (A) embarrass (B) embarass (C) embarras (D) embarasse
38. (M) Idiom: "To break the ice" means: (A) to melt frozen water (B) to start a conversation (C) to smash something (D) to end a meeting
39. (M) Fill in the blank: "She is interested ___ learning French." (at / in / on)
40. (H) Passage: "Financial literacy programs in schools teach budgeting, saving and investing. Proponents argue that early exposure builds lifelong habits and reduces later debt. Critics say schools lack time and qualified teachers, and that parents remain the primary influence. A balanced approach embeds money skills into existing math and economics classes rather than adding separate courses."
    What do critics say about financial literacy programs?
41. (H) Fill in the blank: "Hardly had he arrived ___ the meeting started." (than / when / that)
42. (H) The word closest in meaning to "prudent" is: (A) careless (B) wise and cautious (C) wasteful (D) hasty
43. (H) Choose the correct spelling: (A) occurrence (B) occurence (C) occurrance (D) occurence
44. (H) The word closest in meaning to "reluctant" is: (A) eager (B) unwilling (C) careless (D) joyful
