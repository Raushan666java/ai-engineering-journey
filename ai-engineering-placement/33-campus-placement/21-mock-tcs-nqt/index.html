TCS NQT Mock Test - Full Pattern Simulation (54 Questions)

This mock simulates the TCS NQT aptitude section exactly: 24 quantitative, 15 logical, 15 verbal. Time limits: Quantitative 40 min, Logical Reasoning 25 min, Verbal 20 min. Total: 85 minutes. Rule: 60-90 seconds per question. Answers at the bottom - no peeking. Each question is tagged Easy / Medium / Hard.

TCS NQT Pattern - What You Need to Know​

ItemDetail
Aptitude section24 questions in 40 minutes
Logical section15 questions in 25 minutes (approx)
Verbal section15 questions in 20 minutes (approx)
Negative markingNone - no penalty for wrong answers
Marking1 mark per correct answer, non-adaptive in the aptitude part
PassingSectional cut-offs apply; attempt every question, never leave blanks
StrategyEasy questions first, flag Hard ones, return if time remains

Suggested time split for quant: aim for 90 seconds per Easy, 120 for Medium, skip Hard until the end.

Section 1 - Quantitative Aptitude (24 questions) - 40 minutes​

  1. A shopkeeper sells an article for ₹1,380 at a 15% profit. What was the cost price? (Easy)
  2. The average of 20 numbers is 45. If one number 81 is removed, what is the new average? (Easy)
  3. A train 220 m long crosses a pole in 11 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr? (Easy)
  4. A and B can complete a job in 12 days and 18 days respectively. Working together, in how many days will they finish? (Easy)
  5. What is the compound interest on ₹25,000 at 8% per annum for 2 years compounded annually? (Medium)
  6. The ratio of two numbers is 5:7. If 9 is subtracted from both, the ratio becomes 4:6. Find the smaller number. (Medium)
  7. A boat travels 36 km downstream in 3 hours and 24 km upstream in 4 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water? (Medium)
  8. 40% of a number is 128. What is 75% of the same number? (Easy)
  9. In how many ways can 4 boys and 3 girls be arranged in a row if the girls always sit together? (Medium)
  10. A die is rolled twice. What is the probability that the sum of the two numbers is at least 10? (Medium)
  11. Find the next term: 4, 10, 28, 82, 244, ? (Medium)
  12. A pipe fills a tank in 16 hours and a leak empties it in 24 hours. If both are open, how long will the tank take to fill? (Easy)
  13. The population of a town grows at 5% per year. If it is now 2,20,500, what was it 2 years ago? (Medium)
  14. A man invests ₹9,000 at 12% simple interest. How much time will it take to earn ₹4,320 as interest? (Easy)
  15. If 15 workers build a wall in 24 days, how many workers are needed to build it in 18 days? (Easy)
  16. A shopkeeper marks an item 25% above cost price and gives a 10% discount. What is his profit percentage? (Medium)
  17. Two pipes A and B fill a tank in 30 minutes and 45 minutes respectively. Both are opened, and A is closed after 10 minutes. How much longer will B take to fill the tank alone? (Hard)
  18. The ages of a father and son are in the ratio 5:2. After 12 years, the ratio will be 7:4. What is the son's present age? (Medium)
  19. In a bag of 60 balls, 24 are red, 18 are blue and the rest are green. What is the probability of drawing a green ball? (Easy)
  20. A car covers a distance at 30 km/hr and returns at 20 km/hr over the same road. What is the average speed? (Easy)
  21. The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 12 and 240. If one number is 60, what is the other? (Medium)
  22. Milk costing ₹40 per litre is mixed with water in the ratio 4:1. What is the cost price of the mixture per litre? (Medium)
  23. A sum of money becomes 4 times itself in 12 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest? (Hard)
  24. Find the next term: 1, 8, 27, 64, 125, ? (Easy)

Section 2 - Logical Reasoning (15 questions) - 25 minutes​

  1. A is the son of B. B is the sister of C. C is the daughter of D. How is A related to D? (Easy)
  2. In a code, "PLAN" is written as "QMBO". How is "TRAIN" written? (Medium)
  3. Statements: All flowers are roses. No rose is a lily. Conclusions: I. No flower is a lily. II. Some roses are flowers. Which conclusion follows? (Medium)
  4. A man walks 3 km North, then 4 km East, then 3 km South. How far is he from the start? (Easy)
  5. Five friends sit in a row: P is at an extreme end. Q is second to the left of R. S sits exactly between P and R. T is to the immediate right of P. Who sits in the middle? (Medium)
  6. Six people A, B, C, D, E, F live on six floors of a building, floor 1 being the lowest. A lives on the 4th floor. B lives immediately below A. C lives on the top floor. D lives immediately above E. F lives on the 2nd floor. Who lives on the 3rd floor? (Medium)
  7. Find the next term: 3, 8, 18, 38, 78, ? (Easy)
  8. Find the next term: J, L, O, S, X, ? (Medium)
  9. Odd one out: 16, 36, 49, 64, 81 (Easy)
  10. Odd one out: Elephant, Whale, Giraffe, Camel (Medium)
  11. Data sufficiency: What is the value of x? (I) 2x + y = 14 (II) x - y = 1 Can it be answered? (Easy)
  12. Data sufficiency: Who is the heaviest among A, B, C, D? (I) A is heavier than B and lighter than C. (II) D is lighter than A. Can it be answered? (Medium)
  13. Input-Output: Input: 45 12 78 33 60. Step 1: 12 45 78 33 60. Step 2: 12 33 45 78 60. What is Step 3? (Easy)
  14. In a row of 40 students, Aman is 14th from the left and Bhavna is 18th from the right. How many students are between them? (Medium)
  15. Statement: "Drink at least 2 litres of water daily" is printed on a fitness app. Conclusions: I. Water is essential for health. II. Everyone follows the app's advice. Which conclusion follows? (Easy)

Section 3 - Verbal Ability (15 questions) - 20 minutes​

  1. Choose the synonym of "eloquent": A) hesitant B) fluent C) silent D) unclear (Easy)
  2. Choose the antonym of "shallow": A) deep B) narrow C) light D) small (Easy)
  3. Fill in the blank: "The committee ___ yet to decide." (is / are) (Easy)
  4. Fill in the blank: "She is allergic ___ peanuts." (from / to / with) (Easy)
  5. Spot the error: "The manager (A) with his team (B) are going (C) to the meeting (D)." (Medium)
  6. Spot the error: "He has been (A) working in (B) this company (C) since five years (D)." (Medium)
  7. Correct the sentence: "Each of the boys have completed their homework." (Medium)
  8. Para jumble: (a) The company then trained staff on the new tool. (b) Customer complaints dropped sharply. (c) A bank adopted an AI chatbot. (d) As a result, response time halved. Order? (Medium)
  9. Passage: "Urban rooftop gardens cool buildings in summer by shading the roof and releasing moisture. They also absorb rainwater, cutting the load on city drains. Cities like Singapore and New York have offered tax breaks to encourage them." What are two benefits of rooftop gardens mentioned in the passage? (Medium)
  10. Which of the following is NOT a benefit mentioned in the passage above? A) cooling buildings B) absorbing rainwater C) growing rare flowers D) reducing drain load (Easy)
  11. Arrange: "the / manager / approved / finally / the / leave / application" (Easy)
  12. Idiom: "To hit the nail on the head" means: A) to work as a carpenter B) to be exactly right C) to cause damage D) to act quickly (Medium)
  13. Idiom: "A blessing in disguise" means: A) a hidden gift B) an apparent misfortune that turns out well C) a religious ceremony D) a lucky charm (Medium)
  14. Fill in the blank: "If it ___ tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic." (rains / rained / will rain) (Easy)
  15. Correct the sentence: "The data shows that this year's sales are more better than last year's." (Medium)

Answer Key​

Section 1 - Quantitative (1-24)​

  1. ₹1,200 (CP = 1380/1.15)
  2. 43.1 (total = 20 x 45 = 900; minus 81 = 819; 819/19 = 43.1)
  3. 72 km/hr (220 m in 11 s = 20 m/s = 72 km/hr)
  4. 7.2 days (12 x 18/30)
  5. ₹4,160 (CI = 25000 x (1.08^2 - 1) = 25000 x 0.1664)
  6. 45 (5k - 9)/(7k - 9) = 4/6; 30k - 54 = 28k - 36; k = 9; numbers 45 and 63)
  7. 9 km/hr (downstream 12 km/hr, upstream 6 km/hr; still water = (12 + 6)/2)
  8. 240 (number = 128 x 100/40 = 320; 75% of 320)
  9. 720 (treat the 3 girls as one block: 5! x 3! = 120 x 6)
  10. 1/6 (sums >= 10: (4,6), (5,5), (5,6), (6,4), (6,5), (6,6) = 6 of 36)
  11. 730 (x3 - 2 each step: 244 x 3 - 2)
  12. 48 hours (1/16 - 1/24 = 1/48)
  13. 2,00,000 (P = 220500/1.05^2 = 220500/1.1025)
  14. 4 years (4320/(9000 x 0.12))
  15. 20 workers (15 x 24/18)
  16. 12.5% (100 -> 125 -> 112.5)
  17. 20 minutes (A+B in 10 min = 10 x (1/30 + 1/45) = 5/9 of the tank; remaining 4/9 by B alone = 4/9 x 45 = 20 min)
  18. 12 years ((5k + 12)/(2k + 12) = 7/4; 20k + 48 = 14k + 84; k = 6; son = 12, father = 30)
  19. 3/10 (green = 60 - 24 - 18 = 18; 18/60)
  20. 24 km/hr (2 x 30 x 20/50)
  21. 48 (LCM x HCF = product; 240 x 12 = 2880; 2880/60)
  22. ₹32 per litre (4 parts milk at ₹40 + 1 part water: 40 x 4/5)
  23. 25% (interest = 3P in 12 years; 3P = P x R x 12/100; R = 25)
  24. 216 (6^3)

Section 2 - Logical (25-39)​

  1. Grandson (B is A's mother; C is B's sister; D is C's mother, so D is B's mother; A is D's grandson)
  2. USBJO (+1 on each letter: T->U, R->S, A->B, I->J, N->O)
  3. Both I and II (all flowers are roses, no rose is a lily, so no flower is a lily; some roses are flowers is the valid conversion)
  4. 4 km East (3 North and 3 South cancel)
  5. Q (arrangement: P, T, Q, S, R; Q is second to the left of R, S is between P and R, T is right of P; middle = Q)
  6. B (floors top to bottom: C=6, D=5, A=4, B=3, F=2, E=1; floor 3 = B)
  7. 158 (x2 + 2 each step: 78 x 2 + 2)
  8. D (differences +2, +3, +4, +5, +6; X(24) + 6 = 30, wraps to D)
  9. 49 (the only square with a prime base, 7^2; the others are 4^2, 6^2, 8^2, 9^2)
  10. Whale (a marine mammal; the rest live on land)
  11. Yes, both statements together (x = 5, y = 4)
  12. Yes, both together (I: C > A > B; II: A > D; so C is the heaviest)
  13. 12 33 45 60 78 (smallest moves to the front one step at a time)
  14. 8 students (Bhavna is 23rd from the left; 23 - 14 - 1 = 8)
  15. Only I (the advice implies water is essential; the claim that everyone follows it is unsupported)

Section 3 - Verbal (40-54)​

  1. B) fluent
  2. A) deep
  3. is (committee singular)
  4. to (allergic to)
  5. C (subject is "the manager" - is going)
  6. D (for five years, not "since")
  7. Each of the boys has completed his homework ("each" singular; "his" for boys)
  8. c, a, d, b (bank adopts chatbot, trains staff, response time halves, complaints drop)
  9. Cooling buildings in summer and absorbing rainwater
  10. C) growing rare flowers
  11. The manager finally approved the leave application
  12. B) to be exactly right
  13. B) an apparent misfortune that turns out well
  14. rains (first conditional)
  15. "The data shows that this year's sales are better than last year's." ("more better" is a double comparative)
