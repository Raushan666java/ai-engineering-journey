Mock Test — Any Company #1 (Generic Pattern, 30 Questions)

Company-neutral aptitude simulation: 12 quant, 9 logical, 9 verbal. Time: 45 minutes. Rule: 60-90 seconds per question. Answers at the bottom — no peeking. Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (12 questions)​

  1. (E) A shopkeeper sells an article for ₹1,035 at a 15% profit. What was the cost price?
  2. (E) The average of 15 numbers is 32. If one number 47 is removed, what is the new average?
  3. (M) A train 160 m long crosses a platform 240 m long in 20 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
  4. (M) A and B can complete a job in 15 days and 20 days respectively. Working together, in how many days will they finish?
  5. (M) What is the compound interest on ₹18,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually?
  6. (M) The ratio of two numbers is 4:7. If their difference is 45, what is the smaller number?
  7. (M) 30% of a number is 135. What is 80% of the same number?
  8. (M) In how many ways can 6 books be arranged on a shelf?
  9. (M) A die is rolled once. What is the probability of getting a number greater than 4?
  10. (M) Find the next term: 2, 5, 14, 41, 122, ?
  11. (M) A pipe fills a tank in 18 hours and a leak empties it in 27 hours. If both are open, how long will the tank take to fill?
  12. (M) The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 9 and 360. If one number is 45, what is the other?

Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (9 questions)​

  1. (E) Find the next term: 5, 11, 23, 47, 95, ?
  2. (E) In a code, "MOON" is written as "NPPO". How is "STAR" written?
  3. (M) Statements: All birds are animals. Some animals are pets. I. Some birds are pets. II. All birds are animals. Which conclusion follows?
  4. (M) A man walks 5 km North, then 12 km East. How far is he from the start?
  5. (M) In a row of 35 students, Ravi is 12th from the left and Sita is 15th from the right. How many students are between them?
  6. (M) A is the father of B. B is the sister of C. C is the son of D. How is A related to C?
  7. (M) Data sufficiency: What is the value of x? (I) x + y = 12 (II) x − y = 4
  8. (M) Input: 9 2 7 4 5. Step 1: 2 9 7 4 5. Step 2: 2 4 9 7 5. What is Step 3?
  9. (H) Five friends P, Q, R, S, T stand in a line. P is taller than Q. R is shorter than S. T is taller than P. Q is taller than R. Who is the tallest?

Section 3 — Verbal Ability (9 questions)​

  1. (E) Synonym of "prompt": (A) slow (B) quick (C) late (D) lazy
  2. (E) Antonym of "victory": (A) success (B) defeat (C) trophy (D) game
  3. (M) Fill in the blank: "The team ___ yet to submit its report." (has / have)
  4. (M) Spot the error: "He has been (A) working in (B) this company (C) since five years (D)."
  5. (M) Correct the sentence: "Each of the players were given a medal."
  6. (M) Para jumble: (a) The app then notified the user. (b) The payment was processed in seconds. (c) A customer scanned the QR code. (d) Finally, the receipt appeared on screen. Order?
  7. (M) Idiom: "To be on cloud nine" means: (A) to be flying (B) to be extremely happy (C) to be confused (D) to be working
  8. (M) Fill in the blank: "Unless you study, you ___ pass the exam." (will / will not)
  9. (H) Choose the correct spelling: (A) occurence (B) occurrence (C) occurrance (D) occurence

Answer Key​

Section 1 — Quantitative​

  1. ₹900 (CP = 1035/1.15)
  2. 30.93 (total = 480; minus 47 = 433; 433/14)
  3. 72 km/hr (400 m in 20 s = 20 m/s)
  4. 8.57 days (15 × 20/35)
  5. ₹3,780 (CI = 18000 × 0.21)
  6. 60 (3k = 45 → k = 15; smaller = 4 × 15)
  7. 360 (number = 135/0.3 = 450; 0.8 × 450)
  8. 720 (6!)
  9. 1/3 (5 and 6 → 2/6)
  10. 365 (×3 − 1: 122 × 3 − 1)
  11. 54 hours (1/18 − 1/27 = 1/54)
  12. 72 (9 × 360/45)

Section 2 — Logical​

  1. 191 (×2 + 1: 95 × 2 + 1)
  2. TUBS (+1 each letter)
  3. Only II (I not guaranteed)
  4. 13 km North-East (5-12-13 triangle)
  5. 8 students (Sita = 21st from left; 21 − 12 − 1 = 8)
  6. Grandfather (A is C's grandfather: C's sister B is A's daughter)
  7. Both together (x = 8, y = 4)
  8. 2 4 5 9 7 (smallest moves left; 5 next)
  9. T (T > P > Q > R and S > R — P vs S unknown, but T > P makes T tallest since T > P > Q and no one exceeds T)

Section 3 — Verbal​

  1. B) quick
  2. B) defeat
  3. has (team = singular)
  4. C (since → for)
  5. "Each of the players was given a medal" (each = singular)
  6. c, b, a, d (scanned → processed → notified → receipt)
  7. B) to be extremely happy
  8. will not (unless = if not)
  9. B) occurrence

Detailed Solution Explanations​

Quantitative — Step-by-Step​

Q1. A shopkeeper sells an article for ₹1,035 at a 15% profit. What was the cost price?

SP = CP × (1 + profit%/100)
1035 = CP × 1.15
CP = 1035/1.15 = ₹900

Speed trick: Divide by 1.15 directly. Mental math: 900 × 1.15 = 900 + 135 = 1035 ✓

Q2. The average of 15 numbers is 32. If one number 47 is removed, what is the new average?

Total = 15 × 32 = 480
After removing 47: 480 − 47 = 433
New count = 14
New average = 433/14 = 30.93

Q3. A train 160 m long crosses a platform 240 m long in 20 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?

Total distance = 160 + 240 = 400 m
Speed = 400/20 = 20 m/s
Convert: 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/hr

Q4. A and B can complete a job in 15 days and 20 days respectively. Working together?

Together = ab/(a+b) = 15 × 20/(15+20) = 300/35 = 8.57 days

Q5. CI on ₹18,000 at 10% for 2 years?

Amount = 18000 × (1.10)² = 18000 × 1.21 = ₹21,780
CI = 21780 − 18000 = ₹3,780

Q6. Ratio 4:7, difference 45. Smaller number?

7k − 4k = 45 → 3k = 45 → k = 15
Smaller = 4 × 15 = 60

Q7. 30% of a number is 135. What is 80%?

Number = 135/0.30 = 450
80% of 450 = 360

Q8. 6 books arranged on a shelf?

Permutation: 6! = 720

Q9. Die rolled once. Probability > 4?

Favorable: 5, 6 (2 outcomes)
Total: 6
P = 2/6 = 1/3

Q10. Find next term: 2, 5, 14, 41, 122, ?

Pattern: ×3 − 1
2 × 3 − 1 = 5
5 × 3 − 1 = 14
14 × 3 − 1 = 41
41 × 3 − 1 = 122
122 × 3 − 1 = 365

Q11. Pipe fills in 18 hrs, leak empties in 27 hrs. Both open?

Net rate = 1/18 − 1/27 = 3/54 − 2/54 = 1/54
Time = 54 hours

Q12. HCF = 9, LCM = 360, one number = 45. Other?

LCM × HCF = Product
360 × 9 = 45 × x
x = 3240/45 = 72

Logical — Step-by-Step​

Q13. Next term: 5, 11, 23, 47, 95, ?

Pattern: ×2 + 1
5 × 2 + 1 = 11
11 × 2 + 1 = 23
23 × 2 + 1 = 47
47 × 2 + 1 = 95
95 × 2 + 1 = 191

Q14. "MOON" → "NPPO". How is "STAR" written?

M+1=N, O+1=P, O+1=P, N+1=O → NPPO
S+1=T, T+1=U, A+1=B, R+1=S → TUBS

Q15. All birds are animals. Some animals are pets.

I. Some birds are pets — NOT guaranteed (birds and pets may not overlap)
II. All birds are animals — directly stated
Answer: Only II follows

Q16. 5 km North, 12 km East. Distance from start?

Right triangle: √(5² + 12²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13 km
Direction: North-East

Q17. Ravi 12th from left, Sita 15th from right, 35 students total.

Sita's position from left = 35 − 15 + 1 = 21st
Between = 21 − 12 − 1 = 8 students

Q18. A is father of B. B is sister of C. C is son of D. How is A related to C?

A → father → B (daughter)
B → sister → C (son of D)
A is C's grandfather

Q19. Data sufficiency: x + y = 12, x − y = 4.

Adding: 2x = 16 → x = 8
Subtracting: 2y = 8 → y = 4
Both together sufficient.

Q20. Input: 9 2 7 4 5. Step 1: 2 9 7 4 5. Step 2: 2 4 9 7 5. Step 3?

Pattern: smallest element moves left each step
Step 3: 2 4 5 9 7

Q21. Five friends. P > Q. R < S. T > P. Q > R. Tallest?

T > P > Q > R
S > R (but S vs P/T unknown)
T is tallest (T > P > Q and no one exceeds T)

Verbal — Step-by-Step​

Q22. Synonym of "prompt"?

Prompt = quick, immediate
Answer: B) quick

Q23. Antonym of "victory"?

Victory = success
Antonym = defeat
Answer: B) defeat

Q24. "The team ___ yet to submit its report."

Team = singular (collective noun acting as one unit)
Answer: has

Q25. Spot error: "He has been working in this company since five years."

Error: "since five years" should be "for five years"
Since = point in time, For = duration
Answer: C

Q26. "Each of the players were given a medal."

Each = singular verb
Corrected: "Each of the players was given a medal."

Q27. Para jumble: app notification, payment, QR scan, receipt.

Logical order: scan → process → notify → receipt
Answer: c, b, a, d

Q28. Idiom: "To be on cloud nine"

Cloud nine = state of extreme happiness
Answer: B) to be extremely happy

Q29. "Unless you study, you ___ pass the exam."

Unless = if not → negative outcome
Answer: will not

Q30. Correct spelling: occurence/occurrence/occurrance

Correct: occurrence (two c's, two r's)
Answer: B) occurrence

Speed Strategies​

  1. Quantitative: Solve Q1-Q7 first (easy), skip H/X if stuck > 90s
  2. Logical: Coding-decoding and series are fastest — do them first
  3. Verbal: Read passage questions first, then passage — saves re-reading

Common Mistakes in This Mock​

MistakeWhy It HappensPrevention
Q3: Forgetting to convert m/s to km/hrUnit conversionAlways multiply m/s by 18/5
Q15: Choosing "Some birds are pets"Assumption beyond givenOnly conclude what's guaranteed
Q24: Using "have" for teamCollective noun confusionTeam = singular unless members act separately
Q27: Wrong para orderNot finding the anchorFind the sentence that must come first

Retake Protocol​

  1. Wait 3 days before retaking (memory needs time to reset)
  2. Focus on your weakest 3 topics from the analysis table
  3. Target: 90%+ accuracy on retake
  4. Time yourself strictly — 65 minutes max
  5. Compare scores — improvement = learning happened
