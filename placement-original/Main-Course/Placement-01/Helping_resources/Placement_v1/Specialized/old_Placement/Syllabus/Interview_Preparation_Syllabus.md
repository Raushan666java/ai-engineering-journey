# Interview Preparation Syllabus

## Coding Rounds

- Solve 300+ problems on LeetCode
- Company-specific questions (Amazon, Google, Infosys, TCS, etc.)
- 1–2 hour timed practice

## Technical Interviews

- DSA + Core CS questions
- Project deep dive
- System design Q&A
- DBMS, OS, CN conceptual clarity
- LLD design interview prep

## HR/Behavioral Round

- Tell me about yourself
- Strengths & weaknesses
- STAR technique (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Why should we hire you?
- Conflict management examples
