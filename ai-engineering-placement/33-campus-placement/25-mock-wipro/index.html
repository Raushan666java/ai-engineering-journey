Wipro NLTH Mock Test — Full Pattern Simulation (44 Questions)

Simulates the Wipro National Level Talent Hunt aptitude section: 18 quant, 14 logical, 12 verbal. Time limits: Quant 35 min, Logical 25 min, Verbal 20 min. Total: 80 minutes. Some Wipro years include negative marking — if so, skip questions you cannot do. Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

Wipro NLTH Pattern — What You Need to Know​

ItemDetail
Aptitude18 questions in 35 minutes
Logical14 questions in 25 minutes
Verbal12 questions in 20 minutes
Negative markingSometimes yes (check your test instructions)
Passing70%+ recommended
FlavorArithmetic-heavy: percentages, TSD, time & work; reasoning with puzzles

Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (18 questions) — 35 minutes​

  1. (E) What is 60% of 450?
  2. (E) A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹350 and sells it for ₹420. What is the profit percentage?
  3. (M) The average of 10 numbers is 35. If each number is increased by 6, what is the new average?
  4. (M) A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 5 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
  5. (M) A can do a work in 18 days and B in 27 days. Together, in how many days?
  6. (M) What is the SI on ₹20,000 at 9% per annum for 2 years?
  7. (M) A sum becomes 5 times itself at SI in 20 years. What is the rate?
  8. (M) Divide ₹5,400 among A, B, C in the ratio 2:3:4. What is B's share?
  9. (M) Find the next term: 2, 10, 26, 50, 82, ?
  10. (M) A bag has 7 red and 8 blue balls. One ball drawn. Probability it is red?
  11. (M) In how many ways can 4 people be seated in a row?
  12. (M) The HCF of two numbers is 15 and their LCM is 300. If one number is 60, what is the other?
  13. (M) A boat travels 24 km downstream in 2 hours and 24 km upstream in 4 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
  14. (M) A pipe fills a tank in 20 hours and a leak empties it in 30 hours. Both open. How long to fill?
  15. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 20% above cost and gives a 10% discount. What is the profit percentage?
  16. (H) Two pipes A and B fill a tank in 30 and 45 minutes. Both open; A closed after 12 minutes. How much longer will B take?
  17. (H) The ratio of the ages of two people is 3:4. After 12 years, it will be 5:6. What are their present ages?
  18. (H) Find the remainder when 7^55 is divided by 4.

Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (14 questions) — 25 minutes​

  1. (E) Find the next term: 3, 9, 27, 81, ?
  2. (E) In a code, "HOUSE" is written as "IPVTF". How is "PHONE" written?
  3. (M) Statements: All fruits are sweet. Some sweets are healthy. I. Some fruits are healthy. II. All fruits are sweet.
  4. (M) A man walks 5 km North, then 12 km East. How far from the start?
  5. (M) A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. How is A related to C?
  6. (M) In a row of 50 students, Ravi is 20th from the left. What is his position from the right?
  7. (M) Data sufficiency: Is x > 10? (I) x + y = 15 (II) y = 3
  8. (M) Input: 6 4 9 2 7. Step 1: 2 6 4 9 7. Step 2: 2 4 6 9 7. What is Step 3?
  9. (M) Odd one out: 21, 33, 45, 57, 63
  10. (M) Five people P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row. P is in the middle. Q sits to the immediate left of P. R sits at an end. S sits to the immediate right of T. T sits between P and R. Who sits at the other end?
  11. (H) Six friends A-F sit in a circle. A is opposite B. C is to the right of A. D is to the left of B. E is between C and D. Who is opposite E?
  12. (H) Statement: "The school will be closed on Monday for a public holiday." I. The school closes on public holidays. II. Monday is a public holiday.
  13. (H) In a code, "LIGHT" is coded as "MJH IU", how is "NIGHT" coded?
  14. (H) Seven people A-G sit in a row. A is 2nd from the left. B is 2nd from the right. C is exactly between A and B. Who sits at the 4th position?

Section 3 — Verbal Ability (12 questions) — 20 minutes​

  1. (E) Synonym of "brave": (A) cowardly (B) courageous (C) timid (D) weak
  2. (E) Antonym of "generous": (A) kind (B) stingy (C) giving (D) warm
  3. (M) Fill in the blank: "Each of the answers ___ correct." (is / are)
  4. (M) Spot the error: "The committee (A) have decided (B) to postpone (C) the event (D)."
  5. (M) Correct: "Neither of the students were present."
  6. (M) Para jumble: (a) The CEO announced the merger. (b) Employees were initially concerned. (c) The announcement followed months of negotiations. (d) The new company promised better benefits. Order?
  7. (M) Choose the correct spelling: (A) receive (B) recieve (C) receve (D) reciive
  8. (M) Idiom: "To kick the bucket" means: (A) to clean (B) to die (C) to play sports (D) to resign
  9. (M) Fill in the blank: "He insisted ___ paying the bill." (on / at / for)
  10. (H) Passage: "Remote work has reduced commuting emissions in many cities. Yet researchers caution that the gains can be offset if remote workers take more flights or move farther from offices. Companies tracking both outcomes find that policies supporting public transit and local co-working spaces maximize the environmental benefit." What can offset the emissions saved by remote work?
  11. (H) Fill in the blank: "Scarcely had the concert begun ___ it started raining." (than / when / that)
  12. (H) The word closest in meaning to "fragile" is: (A) strong (B) delicate (C) heavy (D) solid

Answer Key​

Section 1 — Quantitative (1-18)​

  1. 270 (450 × 0.6)
  2. 20% (70/350)
  3. 41 (average shifts +6)
  4. 108 km/hr (150 m in 5 s = 30 m/s = 108 km/hr)
  5. 10.8 days (18 × 27/45)
  6. ₹3,600 (20000 × 0.09 × 2)
  7. 20% (interest = 4P in 20 yr → R = 400/20)
  8. ₹1,800 (B = 5400 × 3/9)
  9. 122 (differences 8, 16, 24, 32 → +40: 82 + 40)
  10. 7/15
  11. 24 (4!)
  12. 75 (15 × 300/60)
  13. 3 km/hr (downstream 12, upstream 6; stream = (12−6)/2)
  14. 60 hours (1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60)
  15. 8% (100 → 120 → 108)
  16. 18 minutes (A+B in 12 min = 12 × (1/30 + 1/45) = 2/3; remaining 1/3 by B = 15 min) — 15 min
  17. 18 and 24 ((3k+12)/(4k+12) = 5/6 → 18k + 72 = 20k + 60 → k = 6)
  18. 3 (7 mod 4 = 3; 7^1 = 7 → 3 mod 4; 7^odd ≡ 3 mod 4; 55 odd → 3)

Section 2 — Logical (19-32)​

  1. 243 (×3)
  2. QIPOF (+1 each letter)
  3. Only II (I not guaranteed)
  4. 13 km North-East (5-12-13)
  5. Son (C is A's parent)
  6. 31st from the right (50 − 20 + 1)
  7. Both together (x = 12 > 10)
  8. 2 4 6 9 7 (smallest moves left; 6 next)
  9. 63 (all others are 3 × odd number pattern: 21=7×3, 33=11×3, 45=15×3, 57=19×3; 63 = 21×3 — all multiples of 3; the odd one: 63 is the only one divisible by 7 and 9... standard: all are multiples of 3; 63 is the only one also a multiple of 9 — answer 63 by the "difference 12" rule: 21,33,45,57 differ by 12; 63 breaks the pattern) — 63 (breaks the +12 pattern)
  10. Q or R (arrangement: R Q P T S or Q R P T S — with P middle and T between P and R, S right of T: P T S and R ... P: order R Q P T S — Q is at left end, R right end... redo: positions 1-5, P at 3. T between P and R. S immediate right of T. Q immediate left of P → Q at 2. T between P(3) and R: T at 4, R at 5. S right of T → S at 5? conflict with R. So T at 4, R at 5, S right of T = 5 conflict — instead T between P and R means R at 1: R _ P T S with Q at 2 → R Q P T S. Other end (position 1) = R; answer: R sits at the other end (Q at 2, R at 1... wait the question asks who sits at the other end: R)
  11. F (6 seats: A=1, B=4; C right of A = 2; D left of B = 3; E between C(2) and D(3) → impossible between adjacent seats — E and F fill 5,6; opposite E(5) = position 2 = C) — C
  12. Both I and II (both follow)
  13. OJI HU (+1 each letter, space preserved)
  14. C (A at 2, B at 6, C exactly between at 4 — C sits at position 4)

  4. A (committee singular → "has decided")
  5. "Neither of the students was present" (neither = singular)

  7. A) receive
