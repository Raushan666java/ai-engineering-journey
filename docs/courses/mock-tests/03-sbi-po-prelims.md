### Q11. [Inequality] | 1 Mark | Inequalities

**Given: P > Q >= R = S < T. Which is definitely true?**

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) P > S

**Explanation:** P > Q and Q >= R = S, so P > Q >= S, implying P > S. Q and T cannot be compared (Q >= S < T breaks the chain). R < T (opposite of C). P and R cannot be directly compared (P > Q >= R gives P > R, but not equality).

### Q14. [Data Sufficiency] | 1 Mark | Data Sufficiency

**What is the value of x? I. x + y = 10. II. 2x - y = 8.**

A) I alone  B) II alone  C) Both needed  D) Neither

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Both needed

**Explanation:** I alone: x = 10 - y (infinite solutions). II alone: 2x = 8 + y, x = (8+y)/2 (infinite solutions). Together: add I and II: 3x = 18, x = 6.

**Key Takeaway:** Two linear equations in two unknowns are needed for a unique solution (assuming they are independent).
