# BSNL JTO vs SET — Complete Exam Analysis

**Candidate profile analysed:** B.Tech Computer Science Engineering, graduated 2023, aggregate 70–79%, age 25, SC category, Indian, beginner-to-intermediate preparation, target = permanent BSNL post.

**Analysis date:** 28 November 2026. All 2026-cycle facts below were checked against the BSNL exam portal (`externalexam.bsnl.co.in`), the BSNL recruitment portal (`recruitment.bsnl.co.in`), and dated press/coaching coverage. Anything I could not verify is labelled **UNVERIFIED** or **ESTIMATE** — it is never presented as official.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

1. **You are eligible for both tracks.** CSE is a named eligible branch for SET (Telecom) and for JTO (Telecom). Your 70–79% clears the SET 60% floor. Age 25 with SC relaxation to 35 is comfortable.
2. **Both 2026 cycles are over.** SET was notified 29 Jan 2026 and examined 5 Jul 2026; JTO was notified 26 May 2026 and examined 23 Aug 2026. Results/cutoffs are not verifiable as published — so **there is no official "last year cutoff" to quote yet**.
3. **The 2027 cycle is the target**, and by the 2026 calendar the SET notification lands in **late January / early February** — roughly eight weeks from now.
4. **SET is the correct primary target for a CSE graduate.** JTO's paper is an ECE paper: network theory, EM theory, analog circuits, microwave. Only 2 of its 13 subject blocks are CS-native.
5. **The single most important correction to the popular advice:** "prepare with a GATE CSE foundation" is *wrong* for BSNL SET. GATE CSE maps to roughly **25–30%** of SET Part II. The remaining 70% is telecom-domain knowledge (wireless, transmission, wireline signalling, project management) that GATE CSE never touches. GATE CSE is a good *fallback* strategy, not a BSNL strategy.
6. **Competition is real but the field is small.** 14,149 candidates sat SET 2026 for 120 posts (~118:1). That is far gentler than SSC/RRB-scale exams, because the 60% + named-branch filter removes most of the market.
7. **Your realistic path:** clear the SC qualifying bar easily (56/160 in Part II, 14/40 in Part I, 90/200 overall), then win category merit. For SC, that means roughly **100–115/200** on 2026-grade difficulty.

## 2. Recruitment Overview

### 2.1 What BSNL is

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is BSNL? | Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited — India's state-owned telecom operator, incorporated 2000, providing wireline, mobile (4G/5G rollout), broadband/FTTH, leased lines and enterprise connectivity. |
| Department, PSU or private? | A **Central Public Sector Enterprise** (100% Government of India), under the administrative control of the **Department of Telecommunications (DoT), Ministry of Communications**. It is **not** a government department. |
| Why that matters | Pay is **IDA (industrial DA) scale**, not 7th CPC. Recruitment, promotion and superannuation follow BSNL's own rules, not UPSC/SSC rules. Verify pension/EPF terms in the notification — BSNL is not on the NPS track in the way a central department is. |
| How it differs from other telecom bodies | **DoT** = policy/licensing (government). **BSNL** = service operator (PSU). **TCIL** = consultancy/execution PSU. **C-DOT** = R&D society. **ITI Ltd** = equipment manufacturing PSU. Only BSNL and TCIL run large regular recruitment of engineers. |
| Which posts are permanent | JTO (Telecom/Civil/Electrical) and SET (Telecom/Finance) are **regular BSNL posts** — SET joins on a fixed stipend as a trainee and is absorbed into the E-3 scale. Postings on `recruitment.bsnl.co.in` (advocates, consultants, contractual engagements) are **not** permanent. |

## 5. Syllabus — Mapped to a CSE Graduate

### 5.1 SET Part II, official weights

| Block | Weight | Q | CSE-native? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Telecommunications (signal processing, modulation, multiplexing, spectrum) | 15% | 24 | ⚠️ Partly — modulation/multiplexing overlap with a networks/comm elective |
| Wireless (2G/3G/4G/**5G**, antennas, satellite LEO/MEO, telco-cloud, backhaul) | 15% | 24 | ⚠️ Low — 5G architecture and cloud-native core are learnable fast, RF is not |
| Wireline (NGN, call routing, broadband, FTTx, SS7/SIP/Megaco, IMS) | 15% | 24 | ✅ Half — SIP/IMS/broadband map onto your networking knowledge |
| Transmission (optical fibre, DWDM, OTN, FTTH, GPON/EPON) | 15% | 24 | ❌ New — but highly fact-based, high-yield memorisation |
| **IP / Data Networks** (routing, OSI & TCP-IP, caching, IPv4/IPv6 subnetting, MPLS, firewalls) | 15% | 24 | ✅ **Strong** |
| **Emerging Tech** (IoT/M2M, blockchain, SDN, cloud/data centres, AI in telecom) | 15% | 24 | ✅ **Strong** |
| **Project Management** (selection, planning, bar/Gantt, PERT, CPM) | 10% | 16 | ✅ **Easy marks** |

**Read the weights.** 64 of 160 marks sit in blocks you already own or can own in two weeks. Add aptitude (40) and you are at 104/200 — already above the UR qualifying line, well above SC's 90. The 72 marks of Basic Telecom + Wireless + Transmission are the only genuinely foreign territory.

### 5.2 GATE CSE syllabus vs SET — the mapping that matters

| GATE CSE subject | SET relevance | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| Computer Networks | **High** | IP/Data Networks 15% + Wireline signalling |
| Operating Systems | Low | Occasional NGN/cloud question |
| DBMS | Very low | Not a SET topic |
| Data Structures & Algorithms | Very low | Not a SET topic |
| Theory of Computation | Nil | — |
| Compiler Design | Nil | — |
| Digital Logic | Low | Only if you also attempt JTO |
| Computer Organisation | Low | Occasional |
| Engineering / Discrete Maths | Low | Only via aptitude quant |
| Programming | Nil | — |

**Conclusion: GATE CSE covers about 25–30% of SET Part II.** The popular "prepare with a GATE CSE foundation" advice is a *fallback* strategy (keeps MTech/PSU doors open), not a BSNL strategy. If BSNL is the target, 70% of your technical study must be telecom-domain material that no GATE CSE course teaches.

### 5.3 JTO syllabus — and why it is an ECE paper

| Stream | Subjects |
|---|---|
| I | Materials & components · Physical electronics, devices & ICs · **Network theory** · **Electromagnetic theory** · Electronic measurements & instrumentation · Power electronics |
| II | **Analog circuits** · Digital circuits · **Control systems** · **Communication systems** · **Microwave engineering** · Computer engineering · Microprocessors |
| III (GAT) | English comprehension · current affairs · everyday science · history/geography · telecom awareness |

Only **Computer Engineering** and **Microprocessors** are CS-native. Everything else needs an ECE textbook and 4–6 months. A CSE graduate *can* pass JTO, but it costs roughly 3× the study time of SET for the same selection odds — and pays less at entry (E-1 vs E-3).

## 6. Cutoff Analysis

### 6.1 The distinction people get wrong

- **Qualifying marks** = the floor to be *considered* (SC: 35% each part, 45% overall). Missing any one part disqualifies you regardless of total.
- **Selection cutoff** = the last selected candidate's total in **your category, in your stream**. This is the number that actually decides your fate, and it is published only with the result.

### 6.2 What actually exists for 2026

| Exam | Exam date | Result / official cutoff status (28 Nov 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| SET 2026 | 5 Jul 2026 (answer key 10 Jul 2026) | **No official cutoff verified as published.** Any "SET 2026 cutoff" figure you see online is an estimate. |
| JTO 2026 | 23 Aug 2026 (answer key 29 Aug 2026) | **Result pending / not verified.** No cutoff exists to quote. |

### 6.3 The only cutoff numbers in circulation, correctly labelled

| Source | Figures | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Testbook exam analysis (Jul 2026), SET out of 200 | UR 105–120 · OBC 95–110 · EWS 95–110 · **SC 85–100** · ST 80–95 | **ESTIMATE** by a coaching portal, not official |
| Scribd "BSNL SET Cut Off 2020" (UR 75 / OBC 71 / EWS 68 / SC 63 / ST 58) | — | **UNVERIFIED and probably misattributed.** SET did not run a 200-mark CBT cycle in 2020; the scale does not match this paper. Do not plan against it. |
| JTO GATE-era cutoffs (2016–2019) | GATE marks, SC ~40–50/100 | **Not comparable** — different exam, different branches (Civil/Electrical), different metric |
| JTO pre-2016 CBT (180-mark paper) | SC ~45–50/180 | **Not comparable** — old pattern, old difficulty |

**Honest bottom line: there is no usable historical cutoff for either current paper.** Both formats were reintroduced in 2026. The first real data point will be the SET 2026 and JTO 2026 results.

### 6.4 Three scenarios for SET 2027 (SC, Telecom)

Built on 2026-grade difficulty (moderate paper, 14k candidates, 18 SC seats).

| Scenario | Assumption | Estimated SC selection cutoff (/200) | Your target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Optimistic** | Paper harder than 2026, vacancies expand beyond 120, weak SC field | **95–105** | 110 |
| **Realistic** | Same difficulty, similar vacancy count, normal field | **105–115** | **120** |
| **Difficult** | Easier paper, vacancies cut, strong SC field | **120–130** | **135** |

Note this is deliberately above the coaching estimate (85–100) because coaching estimates model *qualification*, while selection within a small SC pool tracks the top of that pool. Plan for 120+, not 100.

### 6.5 Expected JTO 2027 cutoff

No defensible estimate exists — the 480-mark format has never produced a published cutoff. What can be said: with +4/−1 and 480 marks, the *distribution* is wide, so category cutoffs will look high in absolute terms (a 55% score is 264/480).

## 8. Reservation and Government Rules

| Rule | Position |
|---|---|
| SC reservation | **15%** of vacancies, applied stream-wise (2026: 14/95 Telecom, 4/25 Finance) |
| SC certificate | Must be a **Central Government format SC certificate** (not state-only) issued by the competent authority; OBC needs **NCL** and within validity; EWS needs the current-year income certificate |
| Age relaxation | SC/ST **+5 years** (JTO 35, SET 35). OBC-NCL +3. PwBD +10 (cumulative with category) |
| PwBD | Separate sub-quota within each category (2026 SET Telecom: PwBD(C) 2, PwBD(D/E) 2) |
| Document verification | Original + photocopies: degree, marksheets, category certificate, ID, photo, signature. Mismatch between application data and documents is the #1 rejection cause |
| Medical | BSNL medical standards; telecom field roles have physical requirements |
| Appointment | Offer → training → posting; probation per BSNL rules |

**Action for you:** if your SC certificate is old, state-format, or in a name variant (initials, spelling), get it reissued in Central format **before** the notification. This is the single most common way eligible SC candidates lose a selection.

## 11. Risks and Challenges

1. **The GATE-CSE trap.** Following generic "PSU = GATE" advice will consume 6 months on material worth 25% of SET Part II. This is the most expensive mistake available to you.
2. **JTO's ECE wall.** Realistic only with 4–6 months of dedicated ECE study. Not worth it while SET is open.
3. **The 60% / full-time filter.** Binary and unforgiving. Verify now.
4. **Certificate risk.** State-format or stale SC certificate = disqualification after selection.
5. **Notification timing risk.** SET 2026 was notified 29 Jan; if you start in February you have ~5 months to the July exam. Starting now buys 8 months.
6. **Result uncertainty.** SET 2026's result has not been verified as published ~5 months after the exam. Do not build a plan that assumes a fast 2027 timeline.
7. **Vacancy risk.** 120 SET posts is a small number; a single-year dip would hurt. The mitigation is to run a parallel track (see §12).

### 12.4 After the notification (Feb → exam, ~5 months)

- **Weeks 1–6:** second pass on the four weak blocks + 2 full mocks/week.
- **Weeks 7–14:** previous-year and mock volume; error log reviewed weekly; aptitude at 32+/40 consistently.
- **Weeks 15–20:** revision only — formula sheet, flashcards, one full mock every 3 days, taper the last week.

**Weekly target that actually predicts selection:** Part II accuracy ≥ 75% (120/160) with Part I ≥ 80% (32/40). That is 152/200 — comfortably above every scenario in §6.4.

### 12.5 What not to do

- Do not buy a GATE CSE full course and call it BSNL prep.
- Do not attempt JTO in the same cycle as SET.
- Do not skip aptitude because it is only 40 marks — a 35% sectional floor applies to it separately, and 40 marks is 20% of your total.
- Do not guess aggressively: at −0.25 on +1, four blind guesses cost you one mark net.

## 14. Final Expert Verdict

**Target SET (Telecom) 2027, not JTO.** Your CSE degree is a named eligible branch for both, but SET pays at E-3 instead of E-1, has no interview, and its technical paper is ~30% material you already know plus ~40% material that is fact-recall learnable in weeks. JTO would demand an ECE conversion you do not need to undertake.

**Your number is 120/200, not 90/200.** The SC qualifying bar (90) only buys consideration. With 18 SC seats against roughly 1,700–2,000 SC applicants, selection is decided in the top decile of your category — plan for 120, treat 110 as the floor.

**The biggest available edge is syllabus arbitrage.** Most SET candidates are ECE graduates who are weak on IP networks, cloud, SDN and project management — 64 marks that a CSE graduate can take almost for free. Most CSE graduates, meanwhile, waste the cycle on GATE CSE material that appears nowhere on this paper. Play the arbitrage: own the 64 CS-native marks completely, then grind the 72 telecom marks with flashcards.

**Do this week:** verify your aggregate percentage and full-time status, get a Central-format SC certificate issued, and start IP/Data Networks. The 2027 notification is roughly eight weeks out.
