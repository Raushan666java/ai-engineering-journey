# Mock Test — Any Company #3 (Hard Generic Pattern, 45 Questions)

> Company-neutral aptitude simulation, hard difficulty: 18 quant, 15 logical, 12 verbal. Time: 60 minutes.

> Difficulty tags: (M) Medium, (H) Hard, (X) Very hard.

## Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (18 questions)

1. (M) A trader mixes two qualities of rice costing ₹24/kg and ₹36/kg in the ratio 3:2 and sells the mixture at ₹32/kg. What is his profit percentage?
2. (M) The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is ₹150. What is the sum?
3. (M) A sum of money becomes 3 times itself in 16 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest?
4. (M) Two trains 140 m and 260 m long run towards each other at 45 km/hr and 63 km/hr. How long will they take to cross each other completely?
5. (M) A can do a work in 30 days and B in 45 days. They work together for 10 days, then B leaves. In how many more days will A finish the remaining work?
6. (M) A pipe can fill a tank in 16 hours. Due to a leak, it takes 24 hours. In how many hours will the leak alone empty the full tank?
7. (M) The ratio of the ages of a father and son is 7:3. After 10 years, the ratio will be 9:5. What are their present ages?
8. (M) A bag contains 5 red and 7 blue balls. Two balls are drawn. What is the probability both are of the same color?
9. (H) In how many ways can the letters of the word "TALENT" be arranged?
10. (H) Find the remainder when 3^100 is divided by 7.
11. (H) A boat travels 36 km downstream and 36 km upstream taking 3 hours and 6 hours respectively. What is the speed of the stream?
12. (H) The average of 11 numbers is 50. If the average of the first 5 is 45 and the last 5 is 52, what is the 6th number?
13. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 30% above cost and gives a 10% discount. What is the profit percentage?
14. (H) In how many ways can 5 boys and 4 girls be arranged in a row so that no two girls are together?
15. (X) A man invests ₹10,000 at 10% per annum compounded annually. He withdraws the interest every year. How much total interest does he earn in 3 years?
16. (X) The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 14 and 588. If one number is 98, what is the other?
17. (X) A train 200 m long passes a man walking at 5 km/hr in the same direction in 16 seconds. What is the speed of the train?
18. (X) Two pipes A and B fill a tank in 24 and 40 minutes. Both are opened, and A is closed after 12 minutes. How much longer will B take?

## Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (15 questions)

19. (M) Find the next term: 3, 8, 22, 63, 185, ?
20. (M) In a code, "PRIVATE" is written as "Q S J W B U F" (each letter +1, with spaces). How is "PUBLIC" coded?
21. (M) Statements: Some singers are dancers. No dancer is a painter.
    I. Some singers are not painters. II. Some painters are not dancers.
22. (M) A man walks 3 km North, then 4 km East, then 3 km South. How far and in what direction is he from the start?
23. (M) A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. C is the wife of D. How is A related to D?
24. (M) Six people A-F live on six floors, floor 1 lowest. A lives on floor 5. B lives immediately below A. C lives on floor 3. D lives above E. F lives on floor 1. Who lives on floor 2?
25. (M) Data sufficiency: What is the age of the son?
    (I) The father is 40 years old.
    (II) The father's age is 4 times the son's age.
26. (M) Input: 14 6 9 3 12. Step 1: 3 14 6 9 12. Step 2: 3 6 14 9 12. What is Step 3?
27. (H) Five friends have different heights. P is taller than Q. R is shorter than P but taller than S. T is shorter than Q. Who is the shortest?
28. (H) Eight people sit in a circle. A is opposite B. C is to the right of A. D is to the left of B. E is between C and D. Who is opposite E?
29. (H) In a row of 45 students, Rohit is 15th from the left and Mohit is 20th from the right. How many students are between them?
30. (H) If "BEAUTY" is coded as "C F B W U Z", how is "MUSIC" coded?
31. (X) Pointing to a photo, a man says, "She is the mother of my brother's wife." How is she related to the man?
32. (X) Six people sit in a row. A is at one end. B is second from the right. C sits exactly in the middle. D is between A and C. E is immediately right of D. F is at the other end. Who is between C and B?
33. (X) A clock shows 3:00. What is the angle between the hands?

## Section 3 — Verbal Ability (12 questions)

34. (M) Synonym of "lucid": (A) vague (B) clear (C) dark (D) heavy
35. (M) Antonym of "meager": (A) abundant (B) scarce (C) tiny (D) weak
36. (M) Fill in the blank: "The number of students ___ increasing." (is / are)
37. (M) Spot the error: "Each of the girls (A) have submitted (B) their assignment (C) on time (D)."
38. (M) Correct: "He is one of the best players who plays for the national team."
39. (M) Correct: "The committee have decided to postpone the event."
40. (M) Para jumble: (a) The lights flickered and went out. (b) The city plunged into darkness. (c) A transformer at the substation failed. (d) Crews worked through the night to restore power. Order?
41. (M) Idiom: "To burn the midnight oil" means: (A) to waste fuel (B) to work late into the night (C) to start a fire (D) to relax
42. (H) Choose the correct spelling: (A) maintenance (B) maintanance (C) maintainence (D) maintenence
43. (H) Passage: "Electric vehicles (EVs) have lower running costs than petrol cars because electricity is cheaper per kilometre and EVs have fewer moving parts to maintain. The main barrier remains the upfront price and charging infrastructure. As battery costs fall, analysts expect EVs to reach price parity with petrol cars within five years."
    What is the main barrier to EV adoption mentioned? 
44. (H) Fill in the blank: "Hardly had he opened the door ___ the phone rang." (than / when / that)
45. (X) The word closest in meaning to "obfuscate" is: (A) clarify (B) confuse (C) simplify (D) explain
