# Phase 03 — Project Pitch

> **The single biggest lever in your interview.** When the interviewer says *"walk me through your project"*, they are deciding in the first 90 seconds whether you actually built it or just claimed it on your resume. Most candidates lose here.

You will leave this phase with **three versions** of every project — a 30-second pitch, a 90-second default, and a 5-minute panel deep dive — plus the bug story, the tradeoffs narrative, and the scaling answer.

---

## Inhale.

Pick one project. The one you're most proud of. Right now, in your head, finish this sentence:

> *"The hardest thing about building it was…"*

If nothing comes immediately — that's the problem this phase fixes.

## How to run this phase

Spend **two weeks** here. Not one. This is the phase that flips an interviewer from *"another resume"* to *"book this one for the next round"*.

- **Week 1** — Drills 1–7. Build the muscle. Use a generic project as the placeholder while you learn the structure.
- **Week 2** — Drills 8 and 9. Fill in YOUR actual projects. Record yourself. Watch it back. Iterate.

By the end of Week 2, you should be able to deliver any of the three versions of either project, cold, without notes.
