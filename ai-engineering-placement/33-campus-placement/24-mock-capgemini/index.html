Capgemini Mock Test — Full Pattern Simulation (42 Questions)

Simulates the Capgemini aptitude section: 18 quant, 12 logical, 12 verbal. Time limits: Quant 30 min, Logical 20 min, Verbal 15 min. Total: 65 minutes. Capgemini is arithmetic-heavy — profit/loss, SI/CI and mixtures appear every year. Difficulty tags: (E) Easy, (M) Medium, (H) Hard.

Capgemini Pattern — What You Need to Know​

ItemDetail
Aptitude18 questions in 30 minutes
Logical12 questions in 20 minutes
Verbal12 questions in 15 minutes
Negative markingNo penalty in most years
Passing70%+ recommended
FlavorProfit/loss, SI/CI, mixtures & alligation, number system heavy

Section 1 — Quantitative Aptitude (18 questions) — 30 minutes​

  1. (E) What is 45% of 320?
  2. (E) A shopkeeper sells an article for ₹828 at a 15% profit. What was the cost price?
  3. (M) A man buys 20 pens for ₹200 and sells each at ₹12. What is his profit percentage?
  4. (M) A train 240 m long crosses a platform 360 m long in 30 seconds. What is the speed in km/hr?
  5. (M) A and B together can do a job in 8 days. A alone takes 24 days. How long will B take?
  6. (M) What is the CI on ₹12,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years?
  7. (M) In what ratio should water be mixed with milk costing ₹48 per litre to sell at ₹36 with no profit or loss?
  8. (M) The average of 15 numbers is 60. If one number 90 is removed, what is the new average?
  9. (M) Find the next term: 6, 14, 30, 62, 126, ?
  10. (M) A bag contains 9 red and 5 blue balls. Two are drawn. What is the probability both are red?
  11. (M) The product of two numbers is 840 and their HCF is 14. What is their LCM?
  12. (M) A sum of money becomes 3 times itself in 12 years at SI. What is the rate of interest?
  13. (M) A boat travels 28 km downstream in 2 hours and 28 km upstream in 7 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
  14. (M) Two pipes fill a tank in 18 minutes and 27 minutes. Both open. How long to fill?
  15. (H) A shopkeeper marks goods 25% above cost and gives a 12% discount. What is the profit percentage?
  16. (H) The ages of a father and son are in the ratio 5:2. After 10 years, the ratio will be 7:4. What is the son's present age?
  17. (H) In how many ways can the letters of the word "BETTER" be arranged?
  18. (H) Find the remainder when 5^87 is divided by 6.

Section 2 — Logical Reasoning (12 questions) — 20 minutes​

  1. (E) Find the next term: 4, 8, 16, 32, ?
  2. (E) In a code, "CHINA" is written as "DIJOB". How is "JAPAN" written?
  3. (M) Statements: All engineers are graduates. Some graduates are managers. I. Some engineers are managers. II. All engineers are graduates.
  4. (M) A man walks 6 km East, then 8 km North. How far is he from the start?
  5. (M) A is the son of B. B is the sister of C. How is C related to A?
  6. (M) In a row of 40 students, Priya is 15th from the left. What is her position from the right?
  7. (M) Data sufficiency: What is the age of the father? (I) The son is 15 years old. (II) The father is 3 times the son's age.
  8. (M) Input: 8 3 6 1 5. Step 1: 1 8 3 6 5. Step 2: 1 3 8 6 5. What is Step 3?
  9. (M) Odd one out: 9, 16, 25, 36, 45
  10. (H) Seven people A-G sit in a row. A is 3rd from the left. B is 3rd from the right. C is exactly between A and B. How many people sit between C and B?
  11. (H) Statement: "The company offers free training to all new hires." I. New hires need training. II. All companies offer free training.
  12. (H) In a code, "SUN" is coded as "TVO", how is "MOON" coded?

Section 3 — Verbal Ability (12 questions) — 15 minutes​

  1. (E) Synonym of "huge": (A) tiny (B) enormous (C) small (D) narrow
  2. (E) Antonym of "increase": (A) grow (B) decrease (C) expand (D) boost
  3. (M) Fill in the blank: "The number of accidents ___ declining." (is / are)
  4. (M) Spot the error: "Each of the boys (A) have completed (B) the assignment (C) on time (D)."
  5. (M) Correct: "The manager, along with his team, are attending the meeting."
  6. (M) Para jumble: (a) The website went live at 9 AM. (b) Within an hour, thousands had registered. (c) The team had prepared for weeks. (d) Servers handled the load without issues. Order?
  7. (M) Choose the correct spelling: (A) accommodation (B) accomodation (C) accommadation (D) accommodaton
  8. (M) Idiom: "To make a long story short" means: (A) to write a book (B) to summarize briefly (C) to exaggerate (D) to delay
  9. (M) Fill in the blank: "She is allergic ___ dust." (to / from / with)
  10. (H) Passage: "Data centers consume about 1-2% of global electricity, a share expected to grow with AI workloads. Water is also used for cooling, raising concerns in water-scarce regions. New designs use liquid cooling and renewable energy to reduce both impacts." What two resources are consumed by data centers?
  11. (H) Fill in the blank: "Hardly had she finished ___ the bell rang." (than / when / that)
  12. (H) The word closest in meaning to "candid" is: (A) secretive (B) frank (C) rude (D) hesitant

Answer Key​

Section 1 — Quantitative (1-18)​

  1. 144 (320 × 0.45)
  2. ₹720 (828/1.15)
  3. 20% (cost 10/pen, sells 12 → profit 2/10 = 20%)
  4. 72 km/hr (600 m in 30 s = 20 m/s)
  5. 12 days (1/8 − 1/24 = 1/12)
  6. ₹2,520 (12000 × 0.21)
  7. 1:3 (water:milk = (48−36):(36−0) = 12:36)
  8. 57.86 (total = 900; minus 90 = 810; 810/14)
  9. 254 (×2 + 2: 126 × 2 + 2)
  10. 36/91 (C(9,2)/C(14,2) = 36/91)
  11. 60 (840/14)
  12. 16.67% (interest = 2P in 12 yr → 2P = P × R × 12/100 → R = 16.67%)
  13. 2.5 km/hr (downstream 14, upstream 4; stream = (14−4)/2)
  14. 10.8 minutes (18 × 27/45)
  15. 10% (100 → 125 → 110)
  16. 10 years ((5k+10)/(2k+10) = 7/4 → 20k + 40 = 14k + 70 → k = 5; son = 2k = 10)
  17. 360 (6!/2! — T repeated)
  18. 5 (5^n mod 6 = 5 always: 5, 25, 125... all ≡ 5 mod 6)

Section 2 — Logical (19-30)​

  1. 64 (×2)
  2. KBQBO (+1 each letter)
  3. Only II (I not guaranteed)
  4. 10 km North-East (6-8-10)
  5. Aunt or uncle (B is A's mother; B's sister/brother C is A's aunt/uncle — gender not stated)
  6. 26th from the right (40 − 15 + 1)
  7. Both together (father = 45)
  8. 1 3 6 8 5 (smallest moves left; 6 next)
  9. 45 (only non-square)
  10. 0 (A at 3, B at 5; C exactly between at 4; between C and B = none)
  11. Only I (II overgeneralizes — not all companies)
  12. NPPO (+1 each letter: M→N, O→P, O→P, N→O)

Section 3 — Verbal (31-42)​

  1. B) enormous
  2. B) decrease
  3. is ("the number of" = singular)
  4. A (each → has)
  5. "The manager, along with his team, is attending the meeting" (subject = manager)
  6. c, a, b, d (prepared → went live → registered → handled load)
  7. A) accommodation
  8. B) to summarize briefly
  9. to (allergic to)
  10. Electricity and water
  11. when (hardly...when)
  12. B) frank
