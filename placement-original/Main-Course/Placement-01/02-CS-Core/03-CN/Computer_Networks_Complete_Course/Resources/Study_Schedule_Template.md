## Exam Preparation Timeline

### 30 Days Before Exam
- Complete all course modules
- Begin intensive practice testing
- Identify and address weak areas
- Create final review materials

### 14 Days Before Exam
- Daily practice exams
- Focus on problem areas
- Review key formulas and concepts
- Simulate exam conditions

### 7 Days Before Exam
- Light review only
- Maintain regular sleep schedule
- Prepare exam day materials
- Relax and stay confident

### Exam Day
- Good night's sleep (7-8 hours)
- Healthy breakfast
- Arrive early at test center
- Bring required identification
- Stay calm and focused
