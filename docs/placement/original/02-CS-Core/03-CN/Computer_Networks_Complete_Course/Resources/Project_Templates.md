## Project Evaluation Criteria

### Technical Excellence (40%)
- **Architecture Quality**: Scalable, secure, and efficient design
- **Technology Selection**: Appropriate tools and platforms
- **Implementation Quality**: Professional configuration and deployment
- **Innovation**: Creative solutions and best practices

### Documentation Quality (25%)
- **Completeness**: All required sections included
- **Clarity**: Clear and understandable content
- **Professional Presentation**: Well-formatted and organized
- **Technical Accuracy**: Correct technical information

### Project Management (20%)
- **Planning**: Comprehensive project plan
- **Execution**: Adherence to timeline and budget
- **Risk Management**: Identification and mitigation of risks
- **Communication**: Regular updates and stakeholder engagement

### Business Value (15%)
- **Problem Solving**: Addresses real business needs
- **Cost Effectiveness**: Reasonable cost-benefit ratio
- **User Impact**: Positive effect on end users
- **Strategic Alignment**: Supports organizational goals

These templates provide a structured approach to documenting and presenting network projects professionally, ensuring all critical aspects are covered and stakeholders receive comprehensive information.
