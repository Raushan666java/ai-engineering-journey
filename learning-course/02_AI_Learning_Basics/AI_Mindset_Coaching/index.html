🤖 AI Growth Mindset Coach​

AI Mindset Assessment​

"Assess my current mindset and provide growth coaching:

My thoughts about learning:
- When I struggle: [HOW YOU REACT]
- When I fail: [YOUR TYPICAL RESPONSE]
- About my abilities: [FIXED OR GROWTH BELIEFS]
- About effort: [HOW YOU VIEW HARD WORK]
- About feedback: [HOW YOU HANDLE CRITICISM]

Analyze:
1. Fixed vs growth mindset patterns
2. Limiting beliefs identification
3. Strength areas to build on
4. Specific mindset shifts needed
5. Personalized growth strategies

Provide encouraging but honest assessment."

"Help me reframe these limiting thoughts:

5. Success stories for inspiration

Make reframes personally meaningful and believable."
